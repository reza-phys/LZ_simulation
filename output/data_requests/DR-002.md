# DR-002: LZ arXiv:2609.02823 Data Release — science-sample event coordinates and per-component background/signal PDFs in {S1c, log10 S2c}
- Requested by: P052            - Filed at step: 147 (process_log.md), batch 6
- Priority: important (materially improves result: the toy-calibrated L10 significance, reproduced as 3.0–3.2σ against LZ's 3.4σ, and the 0.5σ isoscalar-inelastic deficit are limited by the unpublished S1c dependence of the background inside the 250–500 phd Fig. 5 panel)
- Dataset: LZ Collaboration, "Data Release" accompanying arXiv:2609.02823 (extended-energy WIMP search, 220 live days, 4.71 t fiducial mass, posted 2 September 2026). The paper states that results for all probed interactions and confidence intervals are in the Data Release; previous LZ releases (WS2022, WS2024) provided event lists and binned PDFs.
- Exact content needed: (i) the science-sample event list after all selections: S1c [phd], log10 S2c (or S2c [phd]), and if available drift time / r, z, for all 1710 events (at minimum all events with S1c > 250 phd, and the candidate's exact log10 S2c); (ii) the post-fit background model per component (internal β, ν ER, ¹³⁶Xe, CH₃T/¹⁴C, ¹²⁴Xe, ⁸³ᵐKr, ¹²⁵I, detector ERs, accidentals, ¹²⁷Xe+¹²⁵Xe, atmospheric ν, ⁸B+hep, MSSI, detector NRs) as 2D histograms in {S1c, log10 S2c} over the WS ROI (3–600 phd, 2.75–4.15), or equivalently the Fig. 5 band-distance histograms split into finer S1c ranges (at least 250–350, 350–450, 450–500, 500–550, 550–600 phd), in expected events per bin; (iii) the NR-band median μ_NR(S1c) and width σ_NR(S1c) used to define the Fig. 5 variable, and the signal PDF in {S1c, log10 S2c} for the 1000 GeV L10^s model (and, if released, for O1^s at δ = 300 and 350 keV) so that the signal density at the candidate can be compared with our 3.1×10⁻³ per event per (25 phd × 0.5σ) cell; (iv) if released, the observed q0 (or −2 ln λ) and the toy distribution / p0 for L10^s (1000 GeV) and O1^s (1000 GeV, δ = 350 keV), and the PDFs of the prompt-veto and delayed-veto samples.
- Where to find it: the LZ collaboration data-release page for arXiv:2609.02823 (unverified location; earlier LZ releases were posted on the collaboration website and Zenodo/HEPData — unverified); INSPIRE record of arXiv:2609.02823; HEPData record if one exists.
- Expected format and size: CSV/YAML/JSON/ROOT histograms and an event-list table, < 50 MB.
- Access or licence constraints, if known: public (CC-BY expected for HEPData; unverified).
- Why it is needed: P052 rebuilt LZ's three-sample (S1c, d) likelihood from Tables I/S1/S2 and the three Fig. 5 panels and reproduces Tables S6/S7 to rms 0.42σ, but the single-event p0 decomposes into 2.4×10⁻⁴ from the S1c > 500 phd panel (published bin by bin; 3.5σ alone) plus 3.8×10⁻⁴ from the NR-band core at 350–500 phd, where Fig. 5 only gives a 250 phd-wide projection and P052 had to assume flat MSSI, exponential accidentals and NR spectra in S1c. LZ's 3.4σ (p0 = 3.4×10⁻⁴) implies that the 350–500 phd band-core background is ≈ 10⁻⁴, i.e. concentrated at the low-S1c end of the panel, or that the signal density at the candidate is larger than reconstructed from the Fig. 5 L10 curve (0.070 of the signal in the event's d-bin). Only the released PDFs (or finer Fig. 5 slices) and the event coordinates can settle which.
- How it will be used: replace the within-panel S1c weights (`w_flat_mid`, `w_acc_mid`, `w_nu_mid`, `w_er_mid`) and the panel d-histograms in `output/code/P052_reproduce_significance.py` (Section 5 of the script) by the released 2D histograms; replace the 'Asimov-like' data cells by the event list; compare the released L10 signal PDF with `signal_cells()` output and `P052_fig5_L10_digitised.csv`; re-run stages fit/sa/toys/final.
- What could change: the toy-calibrated L10 Z (3.03 [2.98, 3.10]σ; 3.22σ with the Fig. 1 spectrum) and O1s δ = 350 (2.98σ) could move up to LZ's 3.4/3.3σ; the isoscalar-inelastic mean offset (−0.53σ) could shrink; the rms (0.42σ) would likely improve to ≤ 0.2σ. The ingredient ranking (2D vs 1D 0.6σ ≫ nuisances 0.03σ ≈ veto samples 0.00σ) and the ŝ = 1.18 (+1.60 −0.83) best fit would not change qualitatively.
- Fallback in the meantime: P052 v1 uses the stated within-panel S1c shapes and the digitised Fig. 5 histograms; the toy-calibrated significances are labelled "provisional pending DR-002".
