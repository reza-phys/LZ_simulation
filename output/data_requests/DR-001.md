# DR-001: LZ arXiv:2609.02823 Data Release — L10^s signal model normalisation and confidence intervals
- Requested by: P012 (also relied on by P045 for the lower-edge construction)            - Filed at step: 065 (process_log), batch 2
- Priority: important (materially improves result; the headline coupling d10^s changes by a factor 2 if the normalisation convention differs from the one inferred here)
- Dataset: LZ Collaboration, "Data Release" accompanying arXiv:2609.02823 (extended-energy WIMP search, 220 live days, 4.71 t fiducial mass, posted 2 September 2026). The paper states: "Results for all of the probed interactions are in the Data Release" and "Confidence intervals for all models can be found in the Data Release".
- Exact content needed: (i) the L10^s (and L10^v) recoil-energy spectra used as signal models, in events/(t·yr·keV) at the stated unit coupling d10^s = 1/m_v² (Anand et al. Eq. 68 normalisation), for WIMP masses 100, 200, 400, 1000, 4000 GeV — or, equivalently, the expected number of signal events in the science sample per unit d10² for those masses; (ii) the two-sided 90% CL intervals on d10^s versus mass (the numbers plotted in Fig. 6 bottom: lower edge, upper edge, median sensitivity, 68%/95% bands) in tabular form; (iii) if released, the fitted signal event count and its 68% interval for L10^s at each mass (Table I gives only 1000 GeV: 1.0 +1.4 −0.7), and the definition of d_j used (whether the quoted d_j multiplies c_p = c_n = d_j/m_v² or the isoscalar c^0 = (c_p + c_n)/2, and the value of m_M in Anand's Eq. 68).
- Where to find it: the LZ collaboration data-release page for arXiv:2609.02823 (unverified location; previous LZ releases were posted on the collaboration website and/or Zenodo and HEPData — unverified), INSPIRE record of arXiv:2609.02823.
- Expected format and size: CSV/YAML/ROOT/JSON tables, < 10 MB.
- Access or licence constraints, if known: public (CC-BY expected for HEPData; unverified).
- Why it is needed: P012 finds that LZ's Fig. 1 unit-coupling L10^s spectrum is 3.86× the WimPyDD rate obtained from the recalled Anand reduction L10 → 4(m_N²/m_M²)[(q²/m_N²)O4 − O6] with m_M = m_N and the WimPyDD convention c^0 = c_p + c_n (already calibrated on O1 by P003). The factor is mass-independent (3.859 at 200 GeV, 3.857 at 1000 GeV), i.e. a factor 2 in the amplitude normalisation of d10. Fig. 6 (digitised) favours the Fig. 1 normalisation, but the lower edge of the 90% interval then corresponds to 0.38 expected events rather than the 0.105 of a background-free single count; the released signal normalisation and interval table would settle both the convention and the interval construction.
- How it will be used: replace `SCALE` (Fig. 1 / WimPyDD factor) in `output/code/P012_magnetic_dipole.py` Part C by the released N(d10 = 1) per mass; compare the released interval edges with the digitised `output/work/P012/fig6_bottom_digitised.csv`.
- What could change: d10^s(N = 1) at each mass (currently 0.24, 0.19, 0.20, 0.28, 0.52 for 100–4000 GeV in the Fig.-1 normalisation, 2× larger in the Anand×WimPyDD normalisation); the equivalent magnetic-moment numbers scale linearly with d10. The low-energy-shoulder and photon-dipole conclusions are normalisation-independent and would not change.
- Fallback in the meantime: use the digitised Fig. 1 curve as LZ's normalisation (validated to 1–3% on O1 by P003) and quote both normalisations; label the coupling values "provisional pending DR-001".
