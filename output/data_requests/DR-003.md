# DR-003: LZ arXiv:2609.02823 — waveform-level quantities of the 248 keV candidate and the template distributions used in the Supplemental "Waveform Analysis"
- Requested by: P093            - Filed at step: 175 (process_log.md), batch 10
- Priority: important (materially improves result: the NR-versus-ER/artefact odds, the smallest margins in P093's odds matrix, are the only ones an unpublished handle can still move; the modelled-background odds are settled)
- Dataset: LZ Collaboration, Data Release accompanying arXiv:2609.02823 (extended-energy WIMP search, 220 live days, 4.71 t; posted 2 September 2026), if it contains event-level or waveform-derived quantities for the event of interest and the calibration templates referred to in the Supplemental Material section "Waveform Analysis"; otherwise the numerical content of that section.
- Exact content needed: for the event of interest (S1c = 540.1 phd, S2c = 9268 phd, 16 June 2023 21:22:39 UTC): (i) the summed S1 and S2 waveforms (phd per 10 ns sample) and, if released, per-PMT S1 and S2 areas or hit patterns; (ii) the derived pulse quantities: S1 prompt fraction / rise time / mean arrival time, S1 top-bottom asymmetry, S2 width (sigma or 10-90 % width, us), drift time (us), reconstructed (x, y) in cm and the corrected position; (iii) the template distributions used for the comparisons in the supplement: S1 pulse-shape distributions of 131mXe ER calibration events and of AmBe NR events near the event's depth; the S2-width-versus-drift-time single-site template built from alpha decays; the TBA-versus-z relation and its spread; the S1 hit-pattern likelihoods for the SS-ER, wall-MSSI and RFR-MSSI templates at the event (the supplement quotes "2 sigma in z, 1 sigma in (x, y)" and "inconsistent with RFR" without numbers); (iv) detector-condition quantities around the event: electron lifetime versus time (hourly, +-24 h), the 83mKr S2c-correction map value at the event's (x, y) and the local map uncertainty, and the S2 width expected at 859 us drift for a bulk versus a cathode-emission S2.
- Where to find it: the LZ collaboration data-release page for arXiv:2609.02823 (unverified location; earlier LZ releases were on the collaboration website and Zenodo/HEPData — unverified); the arXiv source ancillary files of 2609.02823 if any; INSPIRE record of arXiv:2609.02823.
- Expected format and size: CSV/JSON/HDF5 waveform arrays and small template histograms; < 20 MB.
- Access or licence constraints, if known: public if in the Data Release (CC-BY expected, unverified); waveform-level quantities may not have been released, in which case the request should be recorded as "not publicly available".
- Why it is needed: P093 combines every quantified topological handle (position, band distance, pulse shape as stated by LZ, veto, timing, energy) into a classification posterior over origins. The modelled backgrounds (wall/RFR MSSI, accidentals, neutrons, neutrinos) are 3.6-6.9 dex below a single nuclear recoil per unit signal expectation; the remaining margins are NR : artefact +2.5 dex and NR : charge-poor ER +3.6 dex, with 68 % bands of +-0.6 and +-1.2 dex set by untestable tails (P010, P041, P056). Both classes have ER-like S1s, so the S1 pulse shape (P077: LR <= 2-5 per event), the S2 width against a cathode-drift template (P022), the TBA/hit-pattern likelihoods and the lifetime/Kr-map audit (P041) are the only handles that address them. LZ states these were examined but publishes no numbers.
- How it will be used: the released S1 pulse quantities will be compared with the ER and NR templates (P077's toy model, `output/code/P077_s1_psd.py`) to obtain a per-event PSD likelihood ratio; the S2 width will be compared with the bulk and cathode-emission templates (P022, `output/code/P022_accidentals.py`); the hit-pattern likelihoods will replace the W = 1 / 0.01 / 0.1 entries for WALL / RFR / ACC in `TAB` in `output/code/P093_topology_odds.py`; the lifetime and map values will replace the P041 residual's testable 1.6e-3 part in the ART entry. The script is then re-run (6 s).
- What could change: the log10 odds NR : (ER + ART) could move by +0.5 / -0.6 dex (P093 section 3.5), i.e. P(NR) at a prior-weighted s = 0.01 from 0.63 to 0.82 (NR-like) or 0.28 (bkg-like); the ranking ART > ER > NU among backgrounds could change; the modelled-background odds (WALL, RFR, ACC, NEU) would not change materially.
- Fallback in the meantime: P093 v1 uses LZ's qualitative statements as bounded likelihood factors (W column of the handle table) and P077's LR <= 2-5 bound; the NR : ER and NR : ART odds and the unpublished-handle ranking are labelled "provisional pending DR-003".
