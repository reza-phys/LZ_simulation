# P017: Xenon nuclear response at q ≈ 250 MeV: how form-factor nodes and isotope mixtures shape the inferred couplings
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-05
- Primary arXiv category: nucl-th (cross-list hep-ph)
- Author profile: nuclear-structure theorists working on dark-matter response functions
- Category (from your taxonomy): NUC
- Builds on / responds to: LZ arXiv:2609.02823 (Theory paragraph, Figs. 1, 6); P002, P003 (node at ≈266 keV, WimPyDD conventions); P007 (digitised O1 intervals); P009 (energy)

**Abstract.** A 248 keV xenon recoil has q ≈ 246 MeV, 19 keV below the second diffraction minimum (267 keV) of the shell-model spin-independent (M) response LZ uses. Comparing WimPyDD's per-isotope responses with Helm and two-parameter-Fermi form factors, the M response at 248 keV is ×5.6 below Helm and varies ×40 across the ±23 keV window, so inferred O1 couplings carry a nuclear-structure factor ≈3–5. The Σ′/Σ″ responses behind L10, O4, O6 are smooth (×1.2–1.9) and robust to ±15 %.

**Question and approach.** How do the M, Σ′, Σ″, Δ, Φ″ responses of ¹²⁸⁻¹³⁶Xe behave at q(248 keV) = 243–251 MeV, how far do Helm and 2pF descriptions differ from the DMFormFactor-v6 shell-model tables shipped with WimPyDD, and what does that imply for the couplings LZ quotes? We read `Xe.func_w` per isotope, transform a 2pF density numerically (c = 5.42, a = 0.57 fm, recalled), locate nodes, and rescale per-isotope WimPyDD spectra (1000 GeV, Baxter SHM) by ratios of normalised responses—exact for the velocity-independent O1, O4, O6, L10-like (q²/m_N²)O4 − O6. Rates in 225–271 keV, smeared (σ = 23 keV) and efficiency-weighted, give coupling² factors, propagated to P007's O1 intervals and to a digitisation of Fig. 6 (L10).

**Results.** *Nodes.* Natural-Xe M minima: shell 102.0/266.9 keV, Helm 94.3/278.9, 2pF 103.3/308.8 keV; the second node spreads 251 (¹³⁶Xe) to 278 keV (¹²⁸Xe), filling the natural-Xe minimum ×23 relative to ¹³²Xe. At matched rms (4.79 fm) 2pF still lies 16 keV above Helm. Shell/Helm F² = 1.00 (50 keV), 0.34 (200), 0.27 (225), **0.18 (248)**, 0.11 (265); a Helm re-weighting of WimPyDD reproduces `lzcommon.dRdE_SI` to 0.2–2 %.

*Spin.* Σ″ has no node below 420 keV for either odd isotope; Σ′ nodes at 375 (¹²⁹Xe) and 292 keV (¹³¹Xe). ¹²⁹Xe's q⁴Σ′ (L10) kernel peaks at 233 keV; ¹²⁹Xe carries 70 % of the L10 rate at 248 keV (O4 52 %, O6 40 %).

*Rates (alt/shell in 225–271 keV).* O1 elastic: Helm 4.5, 2pF 10.2 (smeared 3.4/5.6); O1 δ = 300 keV: 4.6/10.8; shell dR/dE max/min over 230–270 keV = 41 (O1), 30 (inelastic) vs 1.8 (L10), 2.0 (O4), 1.2 (O6). Thin-shell spin proxy: 1.64 (L10), 0.88 (O4), 0.48 (O6).

*Intervals.* Full-ROI coupling² factor shell/Helm = 0.38/0.35/0.30 at δ = 250/300/350 keV: LZ's (c₁^s m_v²)² = 1.3×10⁻⁵–2.6×10⁻⁴ (δ = 300) would read 4.5×10⁻⁶–9.0×10⁻⁵ with a Helm-like response; 265 keV instead of 248 costs another ×7. Fig. 6 bottom is linear in d₁₀^s m_v²: 0.154–0.553 at 1000 GeV (0.31–3.9 events with P003's normalisation); the spin-shape band moves it by ±7 %.

**Caveats.** LZ's "modifications as in LZ 2023" to the density matrices are unreproducible; the 2pF parameters and two-body-current sizes (SI few–30 %, SD 10–30 %, smooth in q) are recalled; the Helm-shaped spin proxy is a strawman; window ratios are a proxy for the PLR; halo time-averaged.

**Conclusion.** The event sits inside the isotope-spread of the M node: SI-type couplings from it are uncertain by ≈3–5 in coupling² and ×7 per 17 keV of energy, while Σ′/Σ″-driven couplings (L10, O4, O6) are nuclear-structure-robust at ±15 %. Quote event counts, or spin operators, not O1 couplings.
