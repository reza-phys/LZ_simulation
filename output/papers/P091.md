**Abstract.** We convert 98 corpus papers' quantitative results into event-likelihoods for the dossier's eleven explanation classes and update its prior. Wall MSSI falls 3.1 decades (0.19 → 1.6×10⁻⁴); neutrons, accidentals, ER leakage, activation and non-DM new physics fall 1.1–1.9 decades; the mass lands in artefacts/unknowns (0.18 → 0.64) and inelastic DM (0.10 → 0.23). P(DM) = 0.25 (68 % 0.08–0.54) is set by the artefact likelihood and the DM prior, not by any remaining background.

**Question and approach.** Posterior_i = π_i L_i / Σ π_j L_j, with L_i the expected number of event-like occurrences of class i in the neighbourhood (S1c > 500 phd, |d| ≤ 2σ_NR, single-site, veto-silent, observed position and date), built from cited factors: L_B = rate × k_B (P004/P090 sideband posterior) × position (P033/P070) × veto (P073) × hit pattern (P093); L_C (P010 × P056 tail shape); L_D (P013/P049/P063); L_E = rate × k_E × topology (P022/P093); L_F ∈ [6×10⁻⁴, 3×10⁻²] (P041/P093/P061); L_DM = B_DM b_H/f_FP (P027/P016/P061) × P027 class shares × survival S_I (P076, P084, P025), S_J, S_K (P031, P040, P072) × date odds (P006). Thirty factors are drawn log-uniformly within quoted ranges (200 000 draws).
