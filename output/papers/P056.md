# P056: ER/NR discrimination at 250 keV: the leakage predicted by the double-skew-Gaussian recombination model and how far the tails must be trusted
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-11
- Primary arXiv category: physics.ins-det
- Author profile: LXe recombination-physics modellers
- Category (from your taxonomy): RESP
- Builds on / responds to: LZ paper (Tables S3–S4, Figs 2/4/5, Discussion); P010, P024, P009, P041, P004

**Abstract.** We rebuild LZ's tuned ER response (Table S3 means, Table S4 double-skew-Gaussian σ_p) with an exact tail integrator. Taken literally, σ_p·N_i gives a band 1.85× the drawn 10–90 % lines; k = 0.50 reproduces them. The lines are symmetric in log space (0.98–0.99), favouring a near-Gaussian recombination distribution over NEST's positive skew. Band-preserving skew choices change the β leakage at S1c 500–600 phd by eleven orders of magnitude; the Gaussian gives 4×10⁻⁵ events (7×10⁻⁷ at the event).

**Question and approach.** What ER-band width and shape does LZ's model predict at S1c = 300–600 phd, what leakage below the NR median follows for β-like ERs and for the ¹²⁴Xe KK (64.3 keV) and ¹²⁵I (67.3 keV) lines with charge suppression f, how does it compare with Fig. 5, and how sensitive is it to α₁, α₂ and to the skew of N_e? We take N_q ~ Fano 1, N_i binomial, Var(N_e|N_i) = r(1−r)N_i + (kσ_p N_i)², N_e skew-normal with shape s·a_NEST(E) (a_NEST from nestpy; 1.8 at 70 keV), P024's detector terms, and integrate deterministically on a (S1c, log₁₀S2c) grid (MC-verified to 0.0005 dex). Figs 2, 4 and 5 are digitised.

**Results.** *Width.* σ(N_e) at 70 keV: 303 e literal, 154 e at k = 0.503 (Gaussian 0.501; P010: 0.50), 86 e in nestpy's LZ_WS2024 (v2.4.0) model. The ²¹²Pb scatter at 450–560 phd (≈490 points) has half-width 0.173 dex, 1.5× the drawn 0.117, with 6 % below −2 half-widths (LZ: wall leakage).

*Shape.* Drawn asymmetry (p90−p50)/(p50−p10) = 0.983–0.993; Gaussian 0.974–0.988, NEST skew 1.25/1.17/1.07 at 300/400/540 phd, mirrored 0.77.

*β leakage* (89 ERs/keV_ee):

| variant (k = 0.5 unless stated) | N(ROI, 500–600) | N(gap G) | N(V) |
|---|---|---|---|
| NEST skew | 3×10⁻⁹ | 4×10⁻⁹ | 10⁻¹² |
| Gaussian | 4×10⁻⁵ | 7×10⁻⁵ | 7×10⁻⁷ |
| mirrored skew | 6×10⁻³ | 9×10⁻³ | 5×10⁻⁴ |
| k = 0.75 Gaussian | 0.14 | 0.18 | 0.017 |
| k = 1 Gaussian (21 ROI events > 300 phd) | 3.1 | 3.1 | 0.61 |

α₁ ±50 %: ±5 %; α₂ ×0.5/×1.5: ÷80/×1.1 — the αᵢ set the width, not the tail. LZ's continuous-ER curve at S1c > 500 holds 7.2×10⁻³ in +1…+5.5σ_NR (170× our Gaussian) and nothing below +1; its middle-panel floor of (2–8)×10⁻⁴/bin down to −3σ_NR is unreproducible by any recombination model (ours < 10⁻⁸): conservative PDF floors.

*EC lines* (f = 0.2, Gaussian): KK (564 decays) 0.13 in the ROI, 0.07 in G, 2×10⁻³ in V; ¹²⁵I (10⁴ decays) 1.4 / 1.2 / 0.03; f = 0.3 puts 1–19 events in the empty gap. LZ's magenta tail (2.5×10⁻³) matches the NEST-skew KK prediction (2.4×10⁻³).

**Caveats.** How v2.4.5 combines σ_p with N_i and whether it keeps the v2.4.0 skewness are not stated; k and the shape are inferred from drawn lines (±0.005 dex) and a wall-contaminated ²¹²Pb sample. The continuum rate is a flat ±50 % extrapolation; ε_ext = 0.80 and the 2 % position residual are assumed. Testing a 10⁻⁴–10⁻⁵ tail needs 4×10⁵–4×10⁶ ²¹²Pb (2×10⁵–2×10⁶ ¹⁴C) decays free of ¹³³Xe.

**Conclusion.** The Table S4 fit fixes the ER width, not its tail: band-preserving skew hypotheses span 10⁻¹⁴–10⁻² leaked events at S1c 500–600 phd. LZ's own tails are conservative but truncated; the favoured Gaussian gives ≲10⁻⁶ at the event, beyond any achievable calibration.
