# P007: Higgsino-like inelastic dark matter confronting the LZ event

**Abstract.** A pure Higgsino scatters off nuclei only inelastically, via Z exchange with a gauge-fixed σ_n = G_F²μ²/2π = 7.4×10⁻³⁹ cm², six orders above LZ's intervals at δ = 300–350 keV. One event is possible only if the splitting sits just below the kinematic limit: δ ≈ 358–380 keV at 1 TeV (best 366 keV), requiring PeV-scale gauginos. The model is excluded at every tabulated δ ≤ 350 keV and predicts 4–8 June-clustered events in LZ's next 1000 live days.

**Question and approach.** For a Higgsino doublet whose neutral Majorana states are split by δ, what Z-mediated inelastic rate does LZ expect, which (μ, δ) give one event, and is that compatible with the published two-sided intervals, collider bounds, loop-level elastic limits and the relic density? We derive the couplings (c_p = (G_F/√2)(1−4sin²θ_W), c_n = −G_F/√2, dominantly isovector: c¹/c⁰ = −1.17), validate WimPyDD's isospin convention against Helm SI (agreement 1–2 %), digitise Fig. 6/S7 from the PDF vector paths, and compute N(μ, δ) with WimPyDD shell-model spectra, the paper's efficiency (0.96 plateau, 50 % at 269.9 keV) and Sun-frame, annual-mean, June and December SHM halos, for μ = 300–4000 GeV and δ = 0–400 keV.

**Results.** The Xe amplitude c_pZ + c_nN matches an isoscalar coupling with (c_eq^s m_v²)² = 0.0777 (σ_SI,eq = 2.3×10⁻³⁹ cm²); σ_n/σ_SI,eq = (A/((A−Z)−(1−4sin²θ_W)Z))² = 3.21 is the paper's "≃3.2". LZ's digitised 90 % upper edges at 1 TeV, 2.6×10⁻⁴ (δ = 300 keV) and 2.25×10⁻² (350 keV), are exceeded ×299 and ×3.5 in coupling, ×226 and ×1.9 in events; with the Sun-frame halo those edges correspond to 3.2–3.8 events (3.65 expected for one event). Expected events at 1 TeV: 790, 11.1, 2.6, 0.49, 0.098 at δ = 300, 350, 360, 370, 380 keV.

LEP/LHC allow all masses (Δm± ≈ 355 MeV); loop-level elastic σ_SI ~ 10⁻⁴⁹–10⁻⁴⁸ cm² is unobservable; thermal Ωh² = 0.099 at 1 TeV. At δ(N=1) the June/December ratio is 46–16 (1–4 TeV) and LZ's next 1000 live days should hold 4–5 events.

**Caveats.** N(δ) falls ×5 per 10 keV near δ_max, so the halo tail (v_esc, non-Maxwellian tails) dominates the δ uncertainty; LZ's grid stops at 350 keV, so its own likelihood in the allowed window is unpublished; digitised edges carry ±5 %; the χ2 lifetime (~10⁶ s) and loop-level cross-sections are recalled estimates; one event cannot distinguish a Higgsino from other O1-like inelastic models.

**Conclusion.** A pure Higgsino fits the LZ event only in a 20 keV window just below the kinematic limit, δ ≈ 358–380 keV at 1 TeV, with PeV gauginos; it is excluded at the paper's δ ≤ 350 keV grid points and predicts a June-phased signal for the next release.
