# P085: Earth's velocity on 16 June 2023 21:22:39 UTC and gravitational focusing: the exact kinematic inputs for the LZ event

**Abstract.** We compute the LZ observer's velocity at the event instant with a real ephemeris (astropy 8.0.1, offline) including the Earth's rotation at SURF, and add solar and terrestrial gravitational focusing via Liouville's theorem. |v_E| = 265.74 km/s, 0.2 km/s below every corpus model; focusing raises v_max by 1.10 km/s, δ_max(248 keV) by 0.90 keV and the inelastic rate by 4–16 % nearly independently of date. δ_max ≥ 387 keV holds 9.8 % of the year.

**Question and approach.** What exactly are v_E, v_max, δ_max and their uncertainties for the event, and does the Sun's gravity alter the high-velocity tail that inelastic readings rely on? Heliocentric Earth velocity and position from `get_body_barycentric_posvel` (built-in ephemeris), rotation from `EarthLocation.get_gcrs_posvel`, Baxter-2021 solar motion (variants: Schönrich peculiar velocity, v₀ = 220–250). Focusing: f_lab(**w**) = f_gal(**v**_∞(**w** + **v**_orb) + **v**_⊙) with the hyperbolic-orbit mapping (verified against backward orbit integration to 4 × 10⁻⁴ km/s), integrated on a 320 × 48 angular grid; Δη arrays fed to WimPyDD per-stream kernels (1 TeV, P007 Higgsino couplings, LZ efficiency). Hourly annual scan for duty cycles.

**Results.** *Velocity.* **v**_orb = (1.012, 14.724, −25.344) km/s (29.33 km/s at 1.0159 AU); **v**_rot = 0.333 km/s at 102.7° to **v**_E (projection −0.074); **v**_E = (12.279, 264.862, −17.763), **|v_E| = 265.74 ± 2.5 km/s** (solar motion). wimprates 265.98, WimPyDD 265.92, P055 265.96, lzcommon 265.06. Apex (l, b) = (87.35°, −3.83°), altitude −0.4° at SURF: the wind was horizontal from the north. Annual maximum 266.19 km/s on 2 June 10 h UTC (event 14.5 d later, −0.45 km/s), minimum 237.19, mean 252.11, Sun frame 250.55. *Kinematics.* δ_max(248 keV) = 341.60/387.13/409.89 keV at 0.4/1/4 TeV (v_esc = 544); annual mean 375.92; E_max(elastic, 1 TeV) = 1417.5 keV. *Solar focusing.* n/n_∞ = 1.0084 on 16 June (1.0195 on ≈ 5 March, 1.0058 in September). η rises ×1.022/1.031/1.049/1.12 at v_min = 600/700/750/790 km/s and the edge by 1.09–1.13 km/s all year, so **δ_max → 388.03 keV**; rate ×1.038/1.104/1.158/1.156 (June), ×1.043/1.115/1.158/1.170 (annual) at δ = 300/350/366/380 keV; June/annual ratios change ≤ 1.3 %. Higgsino δ(N = 1): annual 365.77 → 366.66, event date 372.05 → 373.00 keV. *Earth.* Rate +0.3–1.1 %, δ_max +0.063 keV; inelastic scattering in rock is kinematically forbidden. *Duty cycle* (1 TeV, 544): δ_max ≥ 380/385/387 keV for 39.6/22.2/9.8 % of the year (144.7/80.9/35.8 d); |v_E| ≥ event value 8.5 %; never ≥ 390.

**Caveats.** Solar-motion errors are recalled. Focusing assumes a steady, collisionless, sharply truncated Maxwellian (non-Maxwellian tails change the size, not the sign or date-independence). O₁ shapes at 1 TeV; our absolute counts sit 1–6 % below WimPyDD's day halos (P018/P055 offset). Uniform livetime.

**Conclusion.** Adopt **v**_E = (12.28, 264.86, −17.76) km/s, |v_E| = 265.74 ± 2.5, v_max(focused) = 810.84 km/s, δ_max(1 TeV) = 388.0 ± 2.0 keV. Solar focusing is a fixed +4–16 % rate and +0.9 keV edge correction, not a timing effect; the June date is favourable but not remarkable.
