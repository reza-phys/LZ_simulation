# P029: Activation products eight days after AmBe: activities on 16 June 2023 and which decays could produce an MSSI-like topology
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-08
- Primary arXiv category: physics.ins-det (cross-list nucl-ex)
- Author profile: low-background nuclear physicists
- Category (from your taxonomy): BKG
- Builds on / responds to: LZ paper (arXiv:2609.02823) Table I, Discussion, supplement MSSI section; P004 (wall MSSI), P010 (¹²⁵I timing, EC charge suppression), P013

**Abstract.** LZ's candidate came 8.4 d after an AmBe calibration. We reconstruct the xenon activation inventory on 16 June 2023 (normalised to LZ's ¹²⁵I count) and ask which decays could form an MSSI-like topology. None can: EC and isomeric nuclides deposit quantised 4.9–236 keV locally, never 12 keV, and their γ-rays survive 20 cm of LXe with P ≤ 10⁻³. Activation adds ≤ 1.5 × 10⁻⁴ correlated events per run, ≤ 10⁻⁹ within ±1 h of the event.

**Question and approach.** Which of ¹²⁵Xe, ¹²⁵I, ¹²⁷Xe, ¹²⁹ᵐXe, ¹³¹ᵐXe, ¹³³Xe, ¹³³ᵐXe, ¹³⁵Xe, ¹³⁷Xe were present, at what activity, and could any supply LZ's MSSI decomposition (12 ± 2 keV at the vertex, 471 phd S1-only in a dead region ≥ 20 cm away)? Captures ∝ abundance × σ for a 1-day irradiation (thermal and fast sets bracket the unknown capture spectrum; a Table-I-anchored intermediate set is central), decayed with ICRP-107 data; ¹²⁵I follows LZ's 3.6 d removal. The scale comes from ¹²⁵I = 8.9 events via a NEST-LZ ROI acceptance (0.74 % with P010's f = 0.2 charge suppression); ¹²⁷Xe + ¹²⁵Xe = 1.5 cross-checks it. Recalled XCOM-like attenuation and Klein–Nishina give the geometry factors.

**Results.** ¹²⁵I = 8.9 events ↔ 1200 FV decays per run ↔ 1.6e4 ¹²⁴Xe captures per calibration; the implied ¹²⁷Xe/¹²⁵Xe production ratio is 0.22. Activities on 16 June (10 t; thermal–fast brackets): ¹³³Xe 0.14 (0.006–0.66) Bq, ¹³¹ᵐXe 0.015, ¹²⁹ᵐXe 0.008, ¹²⁷Xe 1.25e-3, ¹²⁵I 4.9e-4 (34 % of peak), ¹²⁵Xe 3e-5. FV decays in ±1 h: 570 (27–2700); ¹²⁵I 1.65, ¹²⁷Xe 0.2–7.6.

Geometry: λ = 0.62/0.99/1.42/2.9 cm at 164/203/243/375 keV, so exp(−20/λ) = 8e-15/1.5e-9/7.7e-7/1.0e-3 (9.5e-5 at the actual 26.9 cm; a longer attenuation bracket gives 2.6e-2 at 375 keV).

Topology: EC daughters deposit 33.2 (K) or 4.9 keV (L), S2c = 20 900 or 5320 phd versus 9268 observed; isomers deposit everything within a millimetre. Only β continua reach 10–14 keV, and only ¹³⁵Xe (608 keV γ, 2.9 %) and ¹³⁷Cs pair one with a penetrating γ. Correlated expectation: 1.5e-4 per run (9e-6–8e-3; the maximum needs fast captures, one calibration and unremoved ¹³⁷Cs), all in the first day after calibrations; 4e-10 within ±1 h of the event. Reverse route ≤ 1e-5; random coincidences 2e-8–2e-5. LZ's MSSI model: 4.9e-3 (neighbourhood 1.7e-4, P004). Activation does produce ¹³³Xe at S1c ≥ 499 phd in the ER band (LZ's 500 phd cut) and ¹²⁷Xe RFR-MSSI near the cathode: 0.02–0.26 ROI events at S1c ≈ 490 phd, log₁₀S2c ≈ 3.7, z < 5 cm.

**Caveats.** Emission data, cross-sections and attenuation are recalled (±30 %; capture spectrum ×80 in ¹³³Xe, bracketed). The ¹²⁵I ROI acceptance rests on P010's Gaussian tail model (f = 0.1–0.3 changes the scale ×8/×0.2). Calibration duration, resume time and Cs removal are assumptions. Fast/epithermal captures matter more than the thermal cross-sections suggest.

**Conclusion.** Xenon activation eight days after AmBe cannot produce the event: no product deposits 12 keV at a vertex while sending a γ 20 cm through LXe. LZ should publish the ¹³¹ᵐXe/¹²⁹ᵐXe line rates, isolated-S1 rates and S1c > 500 phd ER counts versus time since calibration.
