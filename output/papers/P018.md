# P018: The high-velocity tail: escape velocity, non-Maxwellian tails and the reach of inelastic dark matter at LZ
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-05
- Primary arXiv category: astro-ph.GA (cross-list hep-ph)
- Author profile: Galactic-dynamics and dark-matter astrophysicists
- Category (from your taxonomy): HALO
- Builds on / responds to: LZ arXiv:2609.02823; P002 (δ_max, rate fall-off), P006 (Earth velocity), P007 (Higgsino window)

**Abstract.** Inelastic-dark-matter readings of the LZ 248 keV event rest on the halo's high-velocity tail (v > 640 km/s for δ ≥ 250 keV). We build Earth-frame halo functions for truncated Maxwellians (v_esc = 500–600, v₀ = 220–250 km/s), Lisanti-type smooth tails (k = 1–3) and an SHM++ Sausage, and propagate them through WimPyDD to LZ rates. δ_max shifts 0.82 keV per km/s; Gaia-range parameters change the δ = 350 keV rate by ×30 and P007's Higgsino window by −30 to +37 keV.

**Question and approach.** How do v_esc, v₀, ρ₀ and the tail shape change δ_max(248 keV), the O₁ inelastic rate, the one-event coupling and P007's Higgsino δ(N=1)? We compute η(v_min) = ∫_{v>v_min} f_E(v)/v d³v by Gauss–Legendre integration over the truncation cap, with WimPyDD's Earth-velocity vector on 16 June and averaged over twelve days, feeding the Δη arrays into WimPyDD `diff_rate` kernels (O₁; 400/1000/4000 GeV; LZ efficiency; 2.84 t·yr). The Maxwellian case reproduces the analytic η to 3×10⁻⁵ and WimPyDD rates to 0.8 %; WimPyDD's own bin-averaged η is 2–5 % high within 30 km/s of v_max.

**Results.** *Kinematics.* δ_max is linear in v_max with slope 0.82 keV/(km/s) for every mass: v_esc = 500→600 km/s moves δ_max(248 keV, 1 TeV, June) from 350 to 433 keV; v₀ adds ∓10 keV. *Tails.* At v_min = 700 km/s, η/η_SHM = 0.55/0.85/1.13/1.38 for v_esc = 500/528/560/600, 0.39/1.65 for v₀ = 220/250, 0.56/0.018/5×10⁻⁴ for k = 1/2/3. A 20 % anisotropic Sausage (β = 0.9, σ_φ = 84 km/s) contributes nothing antiparallel to the solar motion, so η → 0.80 η_SHM above 650 km/s; an isotropic σ_r Maxwellian would give 1.7, the wrong sign. *Rates* (1 TeV, 16 June, log₁₀ ratio to SHM):

| δ(keV) | v_esc=528/560 | v₀=220/250 | k=1/3 | SHM++ | Gaia-range spread |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 300 | −0.11/+0.08 | −0.47/+0.25 | −0.33/−3.7 | −0.10 | 0.72 dex |
| 350 | −0.56/+0.35 | −1.03/+0.49 | −0.71/−5.2 | −0.10 | 1.52 dex |
| 380 | −1.60/+1.16 | −2.20/+1.15 | −1.10/−6.3 | −0.10 | 3.6 dex |

Inferred couplings shift by the inverse factors; ρ₀ = 0.3–0.5 adds 0.22 dex. *Higgsino window* (1 TeV, annual): δ(N=1) = 365.7 keV becomes 335/355/376/402 keV for v_esc = 500/528/560/600, 349/377 for v₀ = 220/250, 354/317/278 for k = 1/2/3, 364 for SHM++ and 349 for literal SHM++ (233/528); P007's ∓11/+9 keV is confirmed; Gaia-range 90 % bands span 341–391 keV. *Astrophysics dominates:* the Gaia-range spread exceeds ×5 (the nuclear-response uncertainty) for δ > 261/293/309 keV (400/1000/4000 GeV), ×10 for δ > 291/324/343 keV and the one-event Poisson band (×35) for δ > 309/349/373 keV; a free tail form (k ≤ 3) exceeds ×10 already at δ ≈ 105–120 keV.

**Caveats.** O₁ shapes only (ratios are hamiltonian-insensitive to 0.02 dex). k = 3 and v_esc = 500/600 are envelope values, not likelihoods. The Sausage is Gaussian; streams are not modelled. Near δ_max the rate lives in the last 20 km/s of the tail, where every parametric form is an extrapolation.

**Conclusion.** For δ ≥ 300 keV the LZ inelastic rate is an astrophysical quantity: Gaia-range halo parameters move it by ×5–30 and the tail shape by orders of magnitude, so any δ-window or coupling inferred from one event is meaningful only jointly with an explicit halo prior.
