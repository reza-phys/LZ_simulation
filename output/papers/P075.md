# P075: Thermal history of ~1 TeV pseudo-Dirac dark matter with the LZ-fitted coupling: freeze-out, coannihilation and non-thermal production
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-15
- Primary arXiv category: hep-ph
- Author profile: particle-cosmology phenomenology group
- Category (from your taxonomy): MODEL
- Builds on / responds to: LZ arXiv:2609.02823; P007, P011, P021, P025, P026, P054 (uses P014, P018, P045)

**Abstract.** We map, over m_χ = 0.3–10 TeV and δ = 250–390 keV, which production mechanism is compatible with the coupling that gives one LZ event for Higgsino, dark-photon and heavy-Z′ pseudo-Dirac dark matter. A Boltzmann solver validated on the thermal Higgsino (1150 GeV) shows that δ is irrelevant to freeze-out (10⁻¹⁰), freeze-in and asymmetric DM are excluded, the Higgsino is thermal only at 1.03–1.29 TeV (δ = 360–385 keV), and contact Z′ models fail beyond δ ≈ 375 keV.

**Question and approach.** Is the LZ-required coupling consistent with (a) freeze-out of the coannihilating χ₁/χ₂ pair, (b) freeze-out into mediator pairs, (c) non-thermal production (moduli decay, freeze-in, asymmetric DM), (d) the gauge-fixed Higgsino relic? We take N_unit(m, δ) from P011's isoscalar and proton grids, extend them with WimPyDD to 0.5/2/5/10 TeV (reproducing P011 to 10⁻⁴), use P007's Higgsino kernels, and solve dY/dx with g_*(T) for s-wave ⟨σv⟩ (Higgsino: Arkani-Hamed–Delgado–Giudice coannihilation formula; contact Z′/A′: SG²m²/π; A′A′: πα_D²/2m²), apply Griest–Seckel weights for δ, and compute the thermal fraction, dilution D, annihilation-limited T_RH and UV freeze-in yield.

**Results.** ⟨σv⟩ = 2.2×10⁻²⁶ cm³/s gives Ωh² = 0.119–0.124; the Higgsino reaches 0.120 at 1150 GeV (Fig. 1). *Coannihilation:* δ/T_f = 10⁻⁶–10⁻⁵ and χ₂ enters σ_eff and g_eff alike: |Δσ_eff/σ_eff| ≤ 2×10⁻¹⁰. *Higgsino:* f_th = Ω_th/Ω_DM = 0.073/0.20/0.76/1.0/2.9/64 at 0.3/0.5/1/1.15/2/10 TeV; thermal (±25 %) only for 1025–1290 GeV, where N = 1 needs δ = 368–371 keV. Lighter Higgsinos need a 24–93 % non-thermal top-up with T_RH ≤ 29.5/3.8/0.86 GeV (1/0.5/0.3 TeV), windows shifting 2–8 keV; heavier ones need D = (m/1.15 TeV)². *Heavy Z′ (contact):* N_th = Ω_th(G_LZ)/Ω_DM = 0.093/0.30 (B−L/U(1)_B, matching P054), 0.0018/0.0059, 5×10⁻⁶/1.5×10⁻⁵ at δ = 300/350/380 keV (1 TeV); freeze-out is consistent (0.105–3.65) only for δ ≤ 298/318 keV (1 TeV), ≤ 256 keV (3 TeV), nowhere above 5 TeV; elsewhere non-thermal yields need T_RH ≤ 3.7–12 GeV (300 keV), 20–60 MeV (365 keV), < 5 MeV beyond 372/377 keV. *Dark photon:* A′A′ fixes α_D = 0.010–0.35; LZ's ε fits between P025's Planck floor and ε ≤ 10⁻³ only for δ ≤ 356 keV (1 TeV), 312 keV (3 TeV) — but the floor scales as α_D³, so D = 1.3–2.3 reopens δ = 365–380 keV. P054's fixed point (0.02 events) needs m_A′ ≳ 2m_χ; below 100 GeV the s-channel is ≤ 3×10⁻⁴ of the relic rate. Freeze-in overproduces by 10²⁵–10³³; asymmetries oscillate away in 2×10⁻²¹ s.

**Caveats.** Annual-mean halo (Sun-frame changes N_unit ×1.4–4 at δ ≥ 350 keV); Sommerfeld enhancement at freeze-out ignored (≤ 20 %); g_*, the ADG formula, BBN floor, ε ceiling and Planck criterion are recalled; mass interpolation is uncertain (≥ ×2) for δ ≥ 375 keV between tabulated masses; the light Z′ (Z′Z′) case is P054's and not re-analysed; T_RH assumes instantaneous reheating.

**Conclusion.** δ ≈ 300 keV is inert at freeze-out. LZ's coupling is thermally natural only for a 1.03–1.29 TeV Higgsino at δ = 360–385 keV or a secluded dark photon at δ ≲ 356 keV (any δ with D ≈ 2); contact Z′ models need δ ≲ 300 keV.
