# P055: Known substructures and the June event: Sagittarius, the Gaia Sausage, S1/S2 and the Helmi streams as sources of a 248 keV recoil
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-11
- Primary arXiv category: astro-ph.GA (cross-list hep-ph)
- Author profile: Gaia-era Galactic-dynamics group
- Category (from your taxonomy): HALO
- Builds on / responds to: LZ arXiv:2609.02823; P006, P018, P030 (competing), P034; uses P002, P007

**Abstract.** P030 treated generic streams; we take the specific Gaia-era substructures with their recalled anisotropic kinematics and ask what they do to the *timing* and *direction* of a 248 keV recoil. Each has its own modulation phase: Sagittarius(+W) and Helmi(+W) peak on 14–15 June, but at 290–415 km/s cannot enter the inelastic rate. Every known component is a pure (1−f) deficit for δ ≥ 300 keV and leaves the 16 June likelihood ratio unchanged to 0.6 %.

**Question and approach.** For Sagittarius (0,0,±300), the SHM++ Sausage (β = 0.9, 20 %), S1 (−30,−297,−73; σ = 83,27,59), S2, the Helmi clumps and retrograde shards (all recalled, flagged), we integrate the v_esc-truncated Gaussians numerically into Earth-frame speed distributions on 33 days, using a from-scratch J2000 ecliptic→Galactic Earth-velocity vector (validated to 0.08 km/s against wimprates). WimPyDD O₁ per-stream kernels (1 TeV, P007 coupling, LZ efficiency) give composite (1−f) SHM + f rates at δ = 250–380 keV, their June/December ratio, first-harmonic phase, P006's run-window date LR = R(t_ev)/⟨R⟩_run, and arrival directions.

**Results.** *Phases.* Components anti-parallel to the solar motion inherit the SHM's peak (day 152.5; S1 159, shards 156) and 11–15 km/s amplitude; vertical streams reach 29 km/s: Sgr(+W) peaks day 165.3 (v_lab 356–415), Helmi(+W) day 166.3 (233–292); Sgr(−W) and S2 peak in late December. *Kinematics.* On 16 June v_lab = 387/415 (Sgr), 565 (S1), 556 (shards), 253–292 km/s (S2, Helmi); δ_max(248 keV, 1 TeV) ≤ 231 keV at +2σ_∥, ≤ 348 keV at the truncated 6σ edge, versus 387 for the SHM. *Rates.* At δ ≥ 300 keV no known component contributes (S1: 0.9 events pure vs 1154 SHM), so composites are exactly (1−f): 0.970 (Sgr), 0.800 (Sausage), 0.900 (S1), 0.990 (shards). This supersedes P030's isotropised S1 boosts 1.09/1.05/1.67 (σ_∥ is 27.6 km/s, not 60). *Timing.* SHM LR(16 June) = 1.455/2.451/3.101 at δ = 300/350/380 keV; composites change it by ≤ 0.6 %. P030's rescuing 1 % retrograde v_esc stream boosts the rate ×3.7/28/47 but *lowers* LR to 0.76/0.86/0.88 of the SHM's. *Sausage.* η ratio 0.814/0.801/0.800 at 650/700/750 km/s (P018: 0.80); June/Dec and phase unchanged; only elastic a₁ rises (7–17 %). *Direction.* Near δ_max the recoil arrives within 12° of the anti-apex (l, b) = (268°, +4°) and recoils within 8.5°, for any halo. Sagittarius (elastic 248 keV possible, +2.8 %) arrives 46–57° off the wind in a 7° cone.

**Caveats.** All substructure velocities, dispersions and fractions are recalled (S2, Helmi, shards uncertain; Sgr local density contested). Truncated Gaussians; ρ₀ fixed; O₁ at 1 TeV; uniform livetime; 12-day sampling under-resolves the δ = 380 keV spike (a₁ 1.49 vs P034's 1.66, LR ±10 %); cap resolution ≤ 5 % in η at 800 km/s (v_esc reference ×47 vs P030's ×51).

**Conclusion.** No known Gaia substructure changes the inelastic reading of 16 June: none reaches 700 km/s, all are (1−f) deficits, and the date evidence stays the SHM's ×1.5–3.1. The June-16-phased components are too slow; a fast stream would weaken the timing. Directionality, not timing, would expose Sagittarius.
