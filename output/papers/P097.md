# P097: A dark disk or co-rotating substructure: would a slow, co-rotating dark-matter component change anything for the 248 keV event at δ ≈ 300 keV?
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-16
- Primary arXiv category: astro-ph.GA (cross-list hep-ph)
- Author profile: Galactic-dynamics group
- Category (from your taxonomy): HALO
- Builds on / responds to: LZ arXiv:2609.02823; P030, P055 (competing); uses P003, P006, P007, P018, P021, P034, P058, P068

**Abstract.** P030/P055 asked whether *fast* substructure feeds the inelastic reading of LZ's 248 keV event; we ask the opposite: what does a *slow*, co-rotating dark disk (lag ≤ 100, dispersion ≤ 90 km/s, fraction f_DD of a fixed ρ₀) do? Nothing above 640 km/s: every inelastic rate is exactly (1 − f_DD) × SHM with phase and 16 June likelihood ratio unchanged, the Higgsino window moves ≤ 2 keV, elastic companions rise ×1.2–1.7, the exothermic ROI rate *falls*.

**Question and approach.** Accreted satellites dragged into the stellar disk leave a co-rotating dark component (Read et al. 2008; recalled) with v_lag ≈ 0–150 and σ ≈ 50–90 km/s; Gaia density limits are surface-density limits, firm only for thin disks (uncertain), so we scan f_DD = 0.1–0.5. Three disks (cold 0/30, fiducial 50/50, hot 100/90 km/s) are isotropic Galactic-frame Gaussians co-rotating at V₀ − v_lag; their Earth-frame η(v_min, t) is analytic (P030/P055), tail fractions are exact non-central χ² integrals, and rates follow from per-stream WimPyDD kernels at 1 TeV (own O₁/O₆; P058's endothermic/exothermic O₁ and P068's Higgsino/L10 caches) on 93 days with LZ's efficiency and 2.84 t·yr. Composite: (1 − f) SHM + f disk at ρ₀ = 0.3.

**Results.** *Kinematics.* Mean lab speeds on 16 June are 34.6/80.1/128.9 km/s (cold/fid/hot), modulating by 33/18/12 %; ⟨1/w⟩ is 6.5/3.3/2.0 × the SHM's. Fractions above 642 km/s (the δ = 250 ROI threshold) are ≤ 3 × 10⁻⁸, above 704 km/s (248 keV, δ = 300) ≤ 5 × 10⁻¹⁰. *Inelastic.* Pure-disk ROI counts are ≤ 7.5 × 10⁻⁷ of the SHM's at δ = 250–380 keV; composites are (1 − f) to 10⁻⁶, a₁ and LR(16 June) untouched. *Elastic.* The disk adds soft recoils and removes 200–270 keV rate, raising N_lo (5.4–55 per 200–270 keV):

|---|---|---|---|---|
| O₁ | 2787 | 3257 | 4600 | 7017 |
| L10 | 0.205 | 0.229 | 0.295 | 0.415 |
| O₆ | 0.443 | 0.473 | 0.561 | 0.718 |

*Exothermic.* Since ΔE(v) ≃ (2μ/m_N) v √(2μ|δ|), the 1/v cross-section cancels against the window width: the integrated down-scatter rate is velocity-independent; a slow disk concentrates it into a peak of half-width 45–85 keV at E* = |δ|μ/m_N = 248–339 keV, mostly beyond the 270 keV roll-off. At f = 0.2 the ROI rate is ×0.89/0.87/0.81 at |δ| = 278/300/366 keV; companions in 125–200 per 200–270 keV drop from 6.5 to 4.3. *Density.* Couplings rise by 1/(1 − f): P021's κ̂(380) 2.19 → 2.43/3.13, Higgsino δ(N = 1) 365.6 → 365.0/363.5 keV (f = 0.1/0.3).

**Caveats.** Disk parameters and density limits are recalled; untruncated Gaussians (truncation alters tail fractions ≤ 10 %, all ≤ 10⁻⁹); O₁/L10/O₆ shapes at 1 TeV; corpus erf efficiency; 92-day sampling; fixed-ρ₀ reading — an *additive* disk would leave the inelastic numbers at the SHM's.

**Conclusion.** No co-rotating component helps the inelastic reading; it can only hurt, by (1 − f_DD) in rate, shifting the Higgsino window by ≤ 2 keV against v_esc's ±11–30 keV. For elastic interpretations it worsens the companion problem; it does not rescue the exothermic reading.
