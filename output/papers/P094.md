# P094: Multi-component dark matter with a populated excited state: electron-recoil lines, low-threshold recoils and the two-state phenomenology across experiments
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-16
- Primary arXiv category: hep-ph
- Author profile: dark-sector model-building group
- Category (from your taxonomy): MODEL
- Builds on / responds to: LZ arXiv:2609.02823; P026, P058 (central), P011, P065, P066

**Abstract.** If the excited state χ₂ of an inelastic-DM reading of the LZ event survives with fraction f₂, what signals arise outside LZ's nuclear ROI? The exothermic electron line χ₂e → χ₁e at E = δ is, at the dark-photon coupling, 10⁶ times weaker than the nuclear channel; low-threshold experiments never see the 100–450 keV down-scatters; argon gains 21–25× per kilogram. Halo χ₁χ₁ → χ₂χ₂ is open and sustains f₂ ≈ 10⁻⁵(m_A′/2.4 GeV)⁻⁸.

**Question and approach.** P026 leaves f₂ = 0.42–0.50 excited; P058 bounds f₂(today) < 2.8×10⁻³–1.7×10⁻⁷ from LZ's nuclear counts. We compute (1) exact relativistic χ₂e → χ₁e kinematics, the rate with σ_e/σ_p = μ_e²/μ_p² (P011's ε, α_D, m_A′), the line's position in LZ's {S1c, log₁₀S2c} space (LZ-tuned NEST) and a line sensitivity from an estimated 300–380 keV ER background (2νββ computed, ²¹⁴Pb scaled from LZ's internal-β count); (2) WimPyDD O₁ exothermic spectra (δ < 0) on Xe, Ar, Ge, Si, O, Ca, W, Na, I, CaWO₄, NaI; (3) re-population by self-scattering, nuclear up-scattering and cosmic rays; (4) the (τ, f₂) map.

**Results.** *Electron line.* T_e = δ − p_f²/2m + **p**_f·**v**: 300 ± 0.53 keV at 250 km/s (p_f = 630 keV), Doppler σ = 0.46 keV versus σ_E ≈ 6 keV; bound electrons deposit δ too. σv = σ_e c (p_f/m_e)(1 + δ/2m_e) = 1.59 σ_e c, so R_e = 1.1×10⁴⁴ f₂σ_e/cm² per t·yr: 2.9×10⁻⁴ (δ = 300) to 2.3 (380 keV) events per t·yr at f₂ = 1, 10⁶–4×10⁵ times below P058's nuclear rate. A 300 keV ER sits at S1c = 1408 phd, log₁₀S2c = 5.52, outside the WS ROI (the ER band leaves it at 20 keV_ee). In LZ's high-energy ER data (B ≈ 74 per t·yr·keV, 51 from 2νββ) a ±1.5σ line search allows S₉₀ ≈ 117 events: f₂σ_e < 3.7×10⁻⁴³ cm² (the only direct handle on TeV DM–electron couplings; dark photon: f₂ < 10⁵). *Light targets.* Per kg·yr per unit f₂ at σ_p(N = 1), δ = 300 keV: Xe 0.34, Ar 7.1, Ge 1.6, Si 8.5, O 5.4, W 0.41, CaWO₄ 2.6, NaI 1.5 (Ar/Xe = 20.9/24.0/24.5 at 300/350/380 keV); medians 162 (Xe) to 280 keV (Si); nothing below 20 keV. DEAP-3600's 2.1 t·yr would hold 1.5×10⁴ f₂ events (LZ 962), SuperCDMS 7, CRESST 0.04. *Re-population.* χ₁χ₁ → χ₂χ₂ needs v_rel ≥ √(8δ/m) = 464 km/s (28 % of halo pairs); σ₀ = 4πα_D²m²/m_A′⁴ = 8.9×10⁻²⁶ cm² (α_D = 0.0245, m_A′ = 2.4 GeV) gives Γ_up = 1.6×10⁻²² s⁻¹ and f₂,ss = Γ_upτ = 1.3×10⁻⁵, exceeding the primordial remnant for m_A′ > 2.97 GeV. Nuclear up-scattering is forbidden on Fe (v_min = 1043 km/s), ≤ 10⁻¹⁴ on Pb; Sun, ISM, cosmic rays give 10⁻²⁶–10⁻²⁰. *Map.* f₂ = 3×10⁻³–10⁻⁵ ↔ τ = 8.4–4.0×10¹⁶ s ↔ m_A′ = 2.4–2.9 GeV (Fig. 2).

**Caveats.** ER background is an estimate (T½ = 2.2×10²¹ yr; ²¹⁴Pb from a 25 ± 12 keV_ee window; no Compton continuum; ±10 %); other experiments' exposures and ranges are recalled; the argon number is a projection needing a 100–500 keV_nr analysis; self-scattering omits O(1) identical-particle factors and inherits α_D² from P011.

**Conclusion.** For the LZ-fitted dark photon the nuclear down-scatter is the only exothermic signal worth searching; argon is 20× more sensitive per kilogram than xenon, electron lines and low-threshold detectors are irrelevant, and halo self-up-scattering guarantees f₂ ≈ 10⁻⁵(m_A′/2.4 GeV)⁻⁸.
