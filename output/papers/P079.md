# P079: Optimal ROI and fiducial design for the next LZ high-energy analysis: trading fiducial mass against MSSI with a 3D likelihood
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-15
- Primary arXiv category: hep-ex (cross-list physics.ins-det)
- Author profile: analysis-optimisation experimentalists

**Abstract.** LZ's high-energy backgrounds are surface-concentrated while the signal is uniform, so the fiducial volume (FV) and the S1c edge should be re-optimised for the 200–400 keV window. Using P070's digitised contour and MSSI profiles calibrated to LZ's own sideband table, we scan stand-off, bottom cut and edge. The edge carries the gain; LZ's 8 cm stand-off is already at the optimum; a position likelihood is worth +16–20 % effective mass.

**Question and approach.** For (s, z_b, edge), what are the fiducial mass, the NR-band background and the discovery sensitivity in LZ's untouched 524 live days (P020)? FV: P070's mean Fig. 3 contour shifted by (8 − s) cm, z_b–132.8 cm, masses calibrated ×1.104 to 4.71 t (the same factor gives 5.42 t for the 5.4 t volume). Wall MSSI ∝ ∫e^{−(R−r)/λ}dV, RFR ∝ ∫e^{−z/λ_z}dV, normalised to 0.0048/10⁻⁴ (×f_nb = 0.035) plus P038's edge-dependent MSSI; uniform 2×10⁻⁴ ∝ mass. Signal: 1 event/220 d at LZ's cuts, scaled by mass and by the P050/P038 acceptance ratio. Metrics: Asimov Z, exact-Poisson P(5σ), 5σ live time, exclusion reach (M·A); a (d, z)-binned likelihood for the soft FV; reconstruction floor d_reco ≥ 3 cm.

**Results.** *Calibration.* LZ's table (5.4 t annulus/4.7 t FV: 6.25 wall, 30 RFR) implies λ = 1.23 cm and λ_z = 2.0 cm, 7× steeper than P033's 4.3 cm; the leakage criterion (10⁻⁴→10⁻² over 2.3 cm) is a low-S2 population, the origin of the 8 cm.

*Stand-off (Fig. 1).* Z(s) is flat: at 600 phd it peaks at s ≈ 1 cm (5.92 vs 5.73 at 8 cm); at 1000 phd at 6.5 cm (6.955 vs 6.946; within 1 % over 3.5–10 cm), since Z ≈ √(2s[ln(s/b) − 1]) trades mass gain g against background factor (1+g)⁶. Lowering z_b at 1000 phd costs Z (6.95 → 6.72 at 5 cm).

*Edge.* 1000 phd: signal ×1.89/3.0/6.3 (L10/δ350/δ366), b 8.9×10⁻⁴ → 8.0×10⁻³; Z 5.73 → 6.95/9.1/14.1; P(5σ|best-fit) 0.43 → 0.83 (L10), 1.00 (δ366); 5σ at 272/158/66 live days. FV changes add ≤ 1 %: s = 7/6 cm (4.86/5.02 t) keeps Z = 6.95 at b = 0.010/0.012 (worst 0.030/0.064); a 3 cm reconstructed margin (5.55 t) gives 6.82, worst b = 0.29.

*Soft FV.* Position binning in LZ's volume gives 7.47 vs 6.90 (equivalent mass 5.45 t); to s = 6 cm, 7.61 (5.65 t), and 7.8–7.9 under λ = 1.2 cm, k = 2 (hard cut 5.7). Wall-adjacent events (3–5 cm) enter with weight ≤ 0.1.

**Caveats.** L10 anchored to one event above 200 keV (2.7× LZ's whole-ROI best fit; ratios unaffected). Asimov Z overstates few-event gains (≲ 15 %); MSSI extension from P038's interpolation (×2); wall profile 1.2–6 cm; masses digitised (8 % scale); wall leakage at high S2 unquantified beyond the 3 cm floor; the S2c ceiling must track the NR band.

**Conclusion.** Spend the budget on the edge (1000 phd), not the wall: hard cut s = 7 cm, z_b = 9 cm keeps b ≤ 0.012 (three events = 5σ); s = 6 cm only with a position likelihood or after validating the wall-MSSI profile on the HE-SB wall sample.
