# P082: A global direct-detection combination for inelastic dark matter after the LZ event: the surviving (m_χ, δ, σ_n) region
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-16
- Primary arXiv category: hep-ph
- Author profile: global-fit / phenomenology group
- Category (from your taxonomy): IDM
- Builds on / responds to: LZ arXiv:2609.02823; competing with P059 and P021; uses P038, P016, P007, P015, P046, P069, P035

**Abstract.** We combine LZ's one-event extended-window likelihood (P021 model, P038 efficiency edge) with every zero-event archival xenon exposure above 100 keV and the published tungsten/iodine limits, over m_χ = 0.2–10 TeV. The surviving isoscalar-O₁ region is m_χ ≥ 400 GeV, δ = 335–390 keV (1 TeV, 90 %), σ_n = 8×10⁻⁴²–3×10⁻³⁶ cm². The archival zeros lower the best-fit coupling by 24 % and Z by 0.06–0.11σ, confirming P059, but shave only 2–3 % of the region.

**Question and approach.** Which (m_χ, δ, σ_n) survives all data, where is the joint best fit, what window remains for a fixed-coupling Higgsino, what does the combination exclude if the event is background, and which future exposure helps most? Signal: WimPyDD O₁ˢ spectra (unit coupling via `wd_c_from_anand`), annual-averaged Baxter halo (LZ's time-averaged SHM, P007/P021), eight masses (400/1000/4000 GeV from P021's cache). LZ: P021's likelihood (Fig.-5 bins, empty 125–200 keV bin, event at 248 keV over b_H = 5.7×10⁻⁴) with P038's NEST-LZ roll-off (E50 = 271.6 keV). Archival: Poisson zeros for SR1 (0.90 t·yr, g1-rescaled edge), XENON1T, LUX, PandaX-II, XENONnT 3.1, PandaX-4T 1.54 t·yr with recalled ROIs, counted only above 100 keV (below, they were background-limited). Regions from Δ(−2lnL) = 2.30/4.61.

**Results.** *Archival counts* at the LZ fit (1 TeV): 0.33/0.31/0.25/0.11 events at δ = 300/350/380/390 keV, 99.6 % from SR1, none from the low-energy ROIs. *Shift:* κ̂_joint/κ̂_LZ = 0.76 for δ ≤ 370 keV, rising to 0.91 at 390 (SR1's lower edge); ΔZ = −0.11 to −0.03. *Region* (joint, 2 dof): m ≤ 300 GeV excluded (Δ ≥ 5.3), flat above 1 TeV; 90 % δ = 320–340/325–370/335–390/340–410/340–415 keV at 0.4/0.6/1/4/10 TeV. At 1 TeV: 68 % δ = 360–385 keV, σ_n = 2.8×10⁻⁴⁰–7.4×10⁻³⁷ cm²; 90 % 330–390 keV, 8.2×10⁻⁴²–2.8×10⁻³⁶; best δ = 380 keV, σ̂_n = 4.9×10⁻³⁸. At 4 TeV: best 400 keV, 1.0×10⁻³⁷ (68 % 375–410 keV). *Higgsino* (1 TeV, fixed σ): δ = 370 keV, 68 % 365–375, 90 % 365–380 keV. *Exclusion mode* (event = background): σ_n < 3.9×10⁻⁴²/2.0×10⁻⁴⁰/2.1×10⁻³⁹/1.1×10⁻³⁷ cm² at δ = 300/350/365/380 keV, ×0.75–0.81 of LZ-only; the Higgsino is excluded for δ ≤ 365 keV. *Non-xenon:* CRESST-II/PICO lie 3800×/1440× above the 90 % edge at 350 keV and (extrapolated) 2.5×/1.9× at 390. *Future* (area fraction if the fit / the Higgsino is true): LZ +6.76 t·yr, same edge, 0.55/0.59; XENONnT 3.1 t·yr to 270 keV 0.70/0.74; LZ with a 1000 phd edge 0.04/0.19; CaWO₄ 100 kg·yr 0.006/0.06 (643/42 events); zero events in the latter two remove δ ≥ 380 keV (Z → 2.5–2.6).

**Caveats.** All archival exposures, edges and "zero events" are recalled (SR1 exposure likely; LUX/PandaX-II edges uncertain); other TPCs' high-energy acceptance is assumed LZ-like; 1D observed-energy proxy with asymptotic Z; Sun-frame halo moves the peak to 370 keV and widens the region 22 %; Fig.-S7 curves beyond 367 keV are extrapolated; CaWO₄ counts rest on a Helm tungsten form factor (×2) and zero background.

**Conclusion.** Nothing published removes the LZ region: the joint fit is m_χ ≳ 400 GeV, δ within 5–30 keV of δ_max(248), σ_n 10⁻⁴¹–10⁻³⁶ cm², with the Higgsino allowed only at δ = 365–380 keV. Only data beyond 270 keV — LZ's own extended ROI or tungsten — can shrink it.
