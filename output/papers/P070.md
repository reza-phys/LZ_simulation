# P070: Position as a discriminant: the (r, z) likelihood ratio between a uniform signal and wall/RFR MSSI at the LZ event, and what LZ left on the table by not using position
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-12
- Primary arXiv category: physics.ins-det (cross-list physics.data-an)
- Author profile: likelihood-modelling experimentalists
- Category (from your taxonomy): BKG/STAT
- Builds on / responds to: LZ paper (arXiv:2609.02823) FV paragraph, Figs. 3/S5, MSSI supplement; P033 (geometric PDF), P004, P022, P019, P013, P016, P008

**Abstract.** LZ uses event position only to define its fiducial volume. From an exact vector digitisation of Fig. 3 we build normalised (r, z) PDFs for a uniform signal, wall MSSI (P033 maps), RFR MSSI, accidentals, atmospheric neutrinos and neutrons, evaluated at the event. Position favours a uniform origin over wall MSSI by 15 (2–50, γ-source dependent) but over the full background mixture by only 1.8, because accidentals and CEνNS are uniform: a 3D likelihood would give ≈ 3.6σ.

**Question and approach.** What is L_sig/L_bkg from position alone, per background, and how much would a (S1c, S2c, position) likelihood have moved LZ's 3.4σ? The Fig. 3/S5a PDFs are fully vectorised (1710/66/55 markers, contours, ticks), so FV contours, wall and every event are read exactly. PDFs normalised over the digitised FV: uniform; P033's per-source wall12/rfr12 maps (f_pos reproduced to 3 %) and exponentials (λ = 3–6 cm); ²¹⁴Pb-RFR via ½E₂(z/λ_γ); neutron exp(−d/14 cm); a cathode-emission accidental slab (P022). Adding position replaces b by b_eff = Σ b_i/LR_i; Z = √(2[ln(1/b_eff) − 1 + b_eff]) anchored to 3.4σ; LEE with N_eff = 12.2.

**Results.** The drawn FV (z = 9.6–135.8 cm) reproduces the paper's stand-offs (mean 10.8/7.3 cm) but weighs 4.36 (4.22–4.50) t at 2.86 g/cm³, 8 % below 4.71 t. The event is 14.1 cm inside the contour at its depth (r = 58.7–61.2 cm); 45 % of a uniform signal lies farther from the wall.

Bottom-array sources put their 12 keV scatters at low z (λ_z ≈ 6.5 cm), where the event sits; with the bottom corner removed by the real contour, the detector-γ RFR class is nearly uniform at the event. Composite: R_pos = 0.56, b_eff = 6.4 × 10⁻⁴: Z 3.40 → 3.57σ (+0.09 to +0.19 across scenarios; cap +0.19 since 52 % of b is uniform); global 2.64 → 2.85σ. A cathode-emission accidental would give −0.37σ. Science/delayed samples are uniform (KS p = 0.08/0.43); prompt-veto crosses fall exponentially from the nearest surface (λ = 3.3 cm); Fig. S5a's three vetoed events lie 2.9–11.3 cm from a dead region (p = 0.005).

**Caveats.** The wall-MSSI LR depends on where LZ's γ-rays originate (2–50), which the paper does not give; P033's maps use a simplified FV and recalled attenuation lengths. Neighbourhood shares come from figure digitisations (P004, P022, P019). The 3.4σ anchor is a Poisson-in-a-box approximation (P016's b_H shifts ΔZ by 0.015). The 8 % mass tension is unresolved.

**Conclusion.** Position is decisive against wall-sourced and ²¹⁴Pb-driven MSSI but nearly blind to bottom-array MSSI, accidentals and CEνNS; a 3D likelihood would add only 0.1–0.2σ. LZ's FV cut already spent most of the position information.
