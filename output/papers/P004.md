# P004: Wall MSSI as the origin of the LZ event: the mismodelling factor required and what the sidebands allow
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-03
- Primary arXiv category: hep-ex (cross-list physics.ins-det)
- Author profile: former xenon-TPC experimentalists
- Category (from your taxonomy): BKG
- Builds on / responds to: LZ paper (arXiv:2609.02823); dossier §4.3/§6.2

**Abstract.** LZ cannot distinguish its 248 keV candidate from a wall multiple-scintillation single-ionization (MSSI) event, and the wall-MSSI rate is simulation-only (100% uncertainty). We ask by what factor k the model must be too low for wall MSSI to be a likely origin, and whether LZ's own sidebands allow it. Sidebands give k_ML = 0.62, k < 1.6 (95%); a 10% chance of one event in the candidate's neighbourhood needs k ≈ 620 (410–930).

**Question and approach.** A geometry error in the ≤3 mm charge-dead layer scales wall MSSI by a common factor k across volumes and energy sidebands (the paper: rate ∝ dead volume). We fit a Poisson likelihood for k to the six wall bins of the supplementary MSSI table (blind 4.7 t search bin excluded): predicted 0.03/0.1/0.5 vs observed 0/0/0 (science; WS ROI 5.4 t, HE SB 4.7 t, HE SB 5.4 t) and 0.1/0.3/2.2 vs 1/1/0 (prompt). The fraction f_nb of wall MSSI at S1c > 500 phd within ±2σ of the NR median comes from a pixel-colour digitisation of Fig. 5 (bottom panel), validated by recovering the caption's total (0.01053 vs 0.0106).

**Results.** Sidebands: k_ML = 0.62; likelihood-ratio limits k < 0.85/1.36/1.64 (68/90/95%; Bayesian 1.95). Science-only bins give k < 2.15; a 20% wall/RFR-split nuisance gives 1.77; reassigning 20% of observed RFR events to wall (unphysical) still gives k < 3.6.

Digitised Fig. 5: MSSI at S1c > 500 phd is 5.1 × 10⁻⁴ events; within ±2σ_NR it is 1.7 × 10⁻⁴, so f_nb = 0.035 (0.024–0.053). With μ_nb = 0.0048·f_nb·k:

| Target | k for P = 10% | k for P = 50% |
|---|---|---|
| anywhere in WS ROI | 22 | 144 |
| ±2σ_NR neighbourhood | **619** (413–929) | **4070** |

k_required/k_allowed(95%) = 619/1.64 ≈ 380 (250–570); the whole-ROI requirement alone exceeds it 13-fold. At k = 1.64, P(≥1 in neighbourhood) = 2.8 × 10⁻⁴. Bayes factor, k free (sideband posterior) vs k = 1: 0.93 (1.4 with LZ's prior).

Topology: a 12 keV forward Compton deposit followed by ≥ 54–75 cm of LXe traversal (λ = 6–10 cm), versus ≈ 21 cm for a generic FV-edge wall MSSI, costs a further 10⁻⁴–0.04; the fraction of wall MSSI ≥ 25 cm from the wall, f_pos ≈ 10⁻⁴–0.12, raises k_required to ≥ 5 × 10³. The 6 ± 2% veto-untagged fraction is a ±33% effect; even zero veto efficiency gives 3.4 × 10⁻³ expected in the neighbourhood.

**Caveats.** The common-k assumption is essential; a wall-MSSI class populating the inner FV but not the 5.4 t annulus or HE SB is unconstrained by the sidebands, though attenuation makes the inner population the tail of the outer one. Attenuation lengths are recalled (±20%); f_nb rests on a validated figure digitisation. RFR MSSI and unmodelled topologies are not treated.

**Conclusion.** A wall-MSSI origin requires the modelled wall-MSSI rate to be low by ≳ 600 (≳ 5000 with the position penalty), whereas LZ's own sidebands limit the factor to < 1.6 (95%; < 3.6 in any extreme). Wall MSSI is not a viable explanation of the event.

**References.** LZ Collaboration, arXiv:2609.02823 (2026); Baker & Cousins, NIM 221, 437 (1984); Feldman & Cousins, PRD 57, 3873 (1998); Klein & Nishina, Z. Phys. 52, 853 (1929); Berger et al., NIST XCOM; LZ Collaboration, PRD 108, 012010 (2023); dossier `00_evidence_dossier.md`.
