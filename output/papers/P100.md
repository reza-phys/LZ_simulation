# P100: Two weeks after: a critical review of the interpretations of the LZ 248 keV event
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-16
- Primary arXiv category: hep-ph (cross-list hep-ex, physics.data-an)
- Author profile: invited critical review by a senior direct-detection group not affiliated with LZ
- Category (from your taxonomy): STAT
- Builds on / responds to: LZ arXiv:2609.02823; the whole corpus P001–P099 (35 pages read in full, 99 ledger rows)

**Abstract.** We review 99 community papers on LZ's lone 248 keV recoil and state, category by category, what is established, by whom, with what confidence, and what remains open. The event is a genuine single nuclear recoil with every modelled background excluded; its dark-matter probability is prior-dominated (0.015–0.25). We identify five convention inconsistencies, the results resting on uncertain recalled numbers or pending data requests, a labelling bias, and seven questions the corpus never asked.

**Question and approach.** Where does the evidence stand in STAT, BKG, RESP, IDM, EFT, MODEL, NUC, HALO, XEXP, COMP, EXO and PROJ, and how much of the corpus's collective conclusion is data rather than convention or prior? We read the ledger (99 rows, P090/P091 included) and 35 papers in full, tallied stances, confidences, result types and data-request dependencies with a script (`P100_review_tallies.py`), scanned every page for its halo-frame convention, and hand-built a per-category verdict table (`work/P100/verdict_table.csv`). Nothing new is computed beyond counts and ratios.

*Inconsistencies.* Halo: 9 papers Sun-frame, 8 annual, 10 both (rates ×1.08–10). "Best-fit" rates ×2.9 apart (P020/P079 band- vs P069/P081 ROI-anchored). Wall λ 4.3 vs 1.2 cm, 0.4σ (P033, P090). *Bias.* 81 neutral, 5 supports (eliminations), 0 favours background, although the corpus raised LZ's neighbourhood background ×2.7 (P093); the null is an unsized artefact class (P091: 0.64).

**Caveats.** Recalled "uncertain" inputs steer model viability (P076 IceCube, P084 H.E.S.S./Fermi, P054 dijets) and the calendar (P069/P088 exposures ±50 %), never event-level conclusions. Six papers are provisional pending DR-001/002/003; DR-003 carries 60 % of P091's variance. Our tallies use self-declared stance and confidence labels; verdicts are judgement. The ledger grew from 97 to 99 rows while we wrote; the final ledger is used.

**Conclusion.** The event is a real 248 keV nuclear recoil of unpredicted rate; nothing conventional explains it and nothing yet shows it is dark matter. Unasked, and answerable only by LZ: the blinding chronology, the 2024 high-S1c population, 16 June detector logs, AmBe data at 200–330 keV.
