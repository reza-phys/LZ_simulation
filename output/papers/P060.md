# P060: Diffuse supernova, Galactic supernova, solar-flare and other astrophysical neutrinos at 248 keV: could a neutrino burst on 16 June 2023 explain the event?
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-11
- Primary arXiv category: astro-ph.HE (cross-list hep-ph)
- Author profile: neutrino astrophysicists
- Category (from your taxonomy): EXO
- Builds on / responds to: LZ paper (arXiv:2609.02823) Table I, Discussion; dossier hypotheses A, H; P019 (atmospheric ν; competing), P040 (N_lo floor), P013/P049 (neutrons)

**Abstract.** Beyond the steady atmospheric flux (P019), no astrophysical neutrino source can produce LZ's 248 keV recoil. Coherent scattering needs E_ν ≥ 123 MeV, while diffuse-supernova (DSNB) and supernova spectra end near 60 MeV (E_R,max = 59 keV); their only route, P019's incoherent Fermi-recoil channel, opens at 31 MeV and yields ≤ 4×10⁻⁶ DSNB events. A burst explanation needs ≳ 10¹² ν cm⁻² above 123 MeV: ≳ 3×10⁵⁴ erg at 10 kpc, ~10⁶ Super-K events, hundreds of low-energy LZ recoils.

**Question and approach.** Which astrophysical neutrinos—DSNB, a Galactic core-collapse supernova, SN 2023ixf, solar flares, the IceCube diffuse flux, reactor/geo/solar—could give a lone 200–270 keV xenon recoil, coherently or through P019's incoherent knock-out channel, and what fluence would a hypothetical transient at 21:22:39 UTC need? We use CEνNS with the Helm form factor in P019's conventions, Keil–Raffelt spectra (α = 2.5) with recalled normalisations (DSNB 30 cm⁻² s⁻¹ all flavours, bracketed by Super-K's ν̄_e limit; supernova 3×10⁵³ erg), P019's Llewellyn-Smith quasi-elastic cross-section with Pauli blocking, a new impulse-approximation threshold for the Fermi-recoil channel (S_n = 8.5 MeV), P009's efficiency and 4.71 t × 220 d.

**Results.** *Kinematics.* E_R,max = 2E_ν²/(m_N + 2E_ν) = 3.7/14.7/40.8/58.8 keV at 15/30/50/60 MeV; the window needs 110.7–128.9 MeV (123.3 at 248 keV). The incoherent channel (neutron knock-out, daughter recoiling with the hole momentum, E_max = 279 keV) requires p_max = E_ν + √(2m_n(E_ν − S_n − E_R)) ≥ p(E_R): E_ν ≥ 30.6 MeV for 225 keV. Gated, it reproduces P019's 1.7×10⁻⁴ to 2 %.

A 10 kpc supernova gives 27 CEνNS recoils/t (23–46 in the ROI within ~10 s).

*Transient.* One window event needs F = 1.18×10¹² cm⁻² at 150 MeV (5.7×10¹¹ at 300 MeV): 3.4×10⁵⁴ erg isotropic at 10 kpc, ten supernovae at ten times the energy; a magnetar giant flare fully converted to 200 MeV neutrinos gives 5×10³ cm⁻². F_req would produce 6×10⁵–1.2×10⁶ inverse-β events in Super-K and 451–893 CEνNS recoils below 200 keV inside LZ within seconds; LZ reports no anomalous population.

**Caveats.** DSNB normalisation (×3), supernova hardness, S_n, the Pauli factor and P019's escape/untagged/ground-state factors (×60 range) are recalled or order-of-magnitude; the threshold gate is generous, so incoherent counts are upper estimates. IceCube cross-sections and flare/GRB/magnetar fluences are recalled (uncertain). Helm is the conservative form factor (shell model ×15 lower). We have no access to GCN/SNEWS/GOES logs; no conclusion depends on them.

**Conclusion.** No astrophysical neutrino source, steady or transient, standard or hard, approaches one event; the most permissive case—a Galactic supernova's incoherent channel, 6×10⁻³—did not occur. A burst is excluded by energetics, by Super-K's silence and by LZ's own missing low-energy companions. The neutrino sector is closed.
