# P087: The atmospheric-neutrino floor in the extended window: CEνNS rates, spectral shape and uncertainty at 55–420 keV in xenon, and whether it limits the decisive tests
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-16
- Primary arXiv category: hep-ex (cross-list hep-ph)
- Author profile: neutrino-background / next-generation-detector group

**Abstract.** Atmospheric-neutrino CEνNS is the only irreducible nuclear-recoil background in LZ's extended window. Anchored to Table I (0.11 ± 0.02 events), it gives 3.1 × 10⁻⁵ events/(t·yr) in 200–270 keV and 1.2 × 10⁻⁵ in 270–420 keV (Helm; shell-model form factor ×0.14 and ×0.7), 10⁴ below LZ's best-fit signal. It first alters a discovery at 31–43 t·yr, its systematic floor is 10⁻⁴–10⁻³ of the best fit, and DSNB/solar contributions vanish above 55 keV. The decisive tests are not neutrino-limited.

**Question and approach.** What atmospheric CEνNS rate, shape and uncertainty does the 55–420 keV window face, and does it limit the modulation (P034), 10–100 t·yr (P050) and untouched-exposure (P069) tests? Flux: a recalled Honda/Battistoni-like double-broken power law (E·dΦ/dE flat 13–100 MeV, index 2.3 to 1 GeV, 3.0 above 2 GeV; 10.5 cm⁻²s⁻¹ ± 25 %), eleven shape variants. Cross-section (G_F²m_A/4π)Q_W²(1 − m_AE_R/2E²)F² with Helm, Helm + 0.15 fm skin and WimPyDD shell-model form factors (η(v_min) divided out, improving P019). LZ efficiency (P021/P050) and P038's 1000-phd edge; the sub-threshold turn-on is calibrated on LZ's ⁸B+hep 0.057. Signals: P050's WimPyDD isoscalar O₁ spectra (1 TeV, annual mean; re-verified). Discovery limits: exact Poisson, 90 % power at 3σ, 25 % log-normal systematic.

**Results.** The recalled flux predicts 0.081 (0.067–0.089) events versus Table I's 0.11 ± 0.02; we anchor to Table I (×1.36). The ⁸B+hep count fixes the turn-on width to 0.74 keV. CEνNS and DM recoils of 248 keV share S1c ≈ 539 phd, log₁₀S2c = 4.02.

Flux shapes span ×0.5–1.2 at 200–270 keV (16 % of P016's total). The form factor dominates: the window straddles xenon's second diffraction node (279/265/245 keV, Helm/skin/shell): ×7 at 200–270, ×1.4 at 270–420 keV. DSNB ≤ 10⁻⁵/t·yr above 55 keV; ⁸B/hep end at 5.8 keV. *Floor.* Discovery limits (Fig. 2) are background-free (2.30 events) until bε = 1.3 × 10⁻³: 43 t·yr (200–270 keV), 31 t·yr (200–420 keV), where one event stops being 3σ; all NR-band backgrounds: 6.7 and 1.1 t·yr. Systematic floor κ_floor/κ_LZ = 3.6/1.3/0.85 × 10⁻⁴ (δ = 300/350/366; bracket ×4.5). *Shape.* 5 (δ = 366, 200–420 keV) to 52 (LZ window) events separate the shapes; δ = 300 is indistinguishable inside 200–270 keV. *Modulation.* 2 % seasonal amplitude needs 45 000 events; P034's a₁ is 21–83× larger.

**Caveats.** The flux shape above 100 MeV is recalled (×2 spread in Φ(>110 MeV)); the anchored 1 GeV flux (3.2 × 10⁻⁴ cm⁻²s⁻¹MeV⁻¹) sits at the upper edge of the recalled Honda range. No CEνNS data exist at q ≈ 250 MeV: Helm, skin and shell model bracket, they do not measure. The turn-on calibration uses an approximate ⁸B tail. Counting is in true energy; extension backgrounds are P050's estimates.

**Conclusion.** Atmospheric CEνNS is 10⁴ below the LZ best fit at 200–420 keV, first matters at 31–43 t·yr, and is dwarfed by MSSI/accidental backgrounds; the neutrino floor for inelastic dark matter lies at 10⁻⁴–10⁻³ of the hint. P034/P050/P069's tests are not neutrino-limited; 270–420 keV is the best-predicted bin.
