# P057: Operator interference and the degeneracy among L₂, L₄, L₉–L₁₂ and L₁₆: what a second event (and a tenth) would distinguish
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-11
- Primary arXiv category: hep-ph
- Author profile: direct-detection model-discrimination phenomenologists
- Category (from your taxonomy): EFT
- Builds on / responds to: LZ arXiv:2609.02823 (LEE section, Tables S6/S7); P003, P012, P027, P031, P044, P047, P050 (inputs from P021, P038, P006/P034)

**Abstract.** LZ's 293 "distinguishable" spectra hide operator-level degeneracies. With WimPyDD shell-model spectra, LZ's resolution and either the 600 phd or a 1000 phd edge, we compute pairwise KL/Hellinger distances and the events separating every pair of lone-event-compatible operators. O6 = E_R × O10 exactly (15–20 events apart), O9 ≡ O14 (~3000), isoscalar ≡ isovector for Σ″ operators (>10⁴). Interference hides no SI admixture: f_hi(O1) ≤ 10⁻³, σ_n ≲ 10⁻⁴⁷ cm².

**Question and approach.** Which of O6, O10, O9, O14, O15, O5ᵛ, L10, O9×q² (P044) and inelastic O1/O4 (δ = 300–380 keV) are spectrally degenerate in xenon, how many events separate each pair at 3σ, can O1 + O6 or O4 + O6 interference mimic a lone 248 keV event, and what does a second event's energy tell? Spectra at 1 TeV, Sun-frame Baxter halo (P050's annual average for inelastic), erf edges at 269.9 or 423 keV (P038), σ_E = 11√(E/248) keV. N_3σ from Gaussian log-likelihood ratios and 20 000-toy MC, both directions, KL floored at 1 %.

**Results.** *Degeneracies.* LZ's pairs (L2/L8 → O10, L4/L20 → O6, L11/L14 → O9) are single-operator identities. At operator level dR/dE(O6)/dR/dE(O10) ∝ E_R^0.9995 (0.0006 dex): same Σ″ response, one power of q² apart, separable with 15–20 events. O9/O14 is constant to 0.007 dex: 2500–3600 events. O6ˢ/O6ᵛ, O10ˢ/O10ᵛ: H = 0.008, >10⁴ events. L10/(O9×q²) ∝ E_R^−1.002 (q⁴Σ′ vs q⁶Σ′): 40–60 events. Median over the 36 compatible elastic pairs: 20 events; 81–83 % need ≤ 30 (`figures/P057_N3sigma_matrix_EXT.png`).

*Interference.* WimPyDD confirms O1 and O6 (M vs Σ″) add incoherently to 10⁻¹⁶ for either sign, while O4 and O6 interfere through a ρ-independent q²Σ″ cross term — what makes L10 companion-free; a constant c4 = −0.069 c6 nodes the Σ″ amplitude at 248 keV but c4²Σ′ survives. N_lo ≤ 3 (5) bounds the O1 share of the 200–270 keV rate to 9.1 × 10⁻⁴ (1.6 × 10⁻³), σ_n ≤ 1.2 (2.1) × 10⁻⁴⁷ cm²; |c4/c6| ≤ 0.016, O4 share ≤ 6–17 %. *Second event* (LR vs L10, 1000 phd): E₂ = 100 keV excludes every inelastic model (≤ 0.04) but leaves elastic ones within 0.5–2.1; E₂ = 248 keV gives 0.34–1.45 for all compatible elastic spectra; E₂ = 350 keV favours δ = 366/380 keV by 6.0/7.4, disfavours O10/O9/O14 (0.3–0.4). Sun-frame vs annual halo alters inelastic shapes: N_3σ = 47–98 (δ = 366), 5.5 (380).

**Caveats.** One-dimensional observed energy, fixed resolution, erf edges (1000 phd width assumed). The floor sets N_3σ where supports are disjoint (w = 10⁻³: ≤ 20 % change); toy N_3σ is quantised. Lagrangian identities and the 3–5-event tolerance are recalled; P016's 1.6–2.2 tightens the O1 bound to 4–6 × 10⁻⁴.

**Conclusion.** Only O9/O14, isoscalar/isovector Σ″ pairs and mass are truly degenerate; every other compatible pair, O6/O10 included, separates with 15–60 events, elastic from inelastic with 2–7. A second event informs on the operator only below 55 or above 300 keV; no SI coupling above 10⁻⁴⁷ cm² hides.
