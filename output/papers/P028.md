# P028: Iodine as the second target: what DAMA/LIBRA's modulation data and COSINE/ANAIS already say about an inelastic 300–390 keV splitting
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-08
- Primary arXiv category: hep-ph (cross-list astro-ph.CO)
- Author profile: annual-modulation and NaI(Tl) phenomenologists
- Category (from your taxonomy): XEXP
- Builds on / responds to: LZ arXiv:2609.02823; P015 (iodine kinematics and counts), P006 (modulation), P002, P003, P007 (δ = 366 keV), P020

**Abstract.** Iodine is kinematically almost xenon (δ_max = 385 vs 396 keV at 1 TeV), so inelastic dark matter explaining the LZ event must give NaI(Tl) a June-peaked, strongly modulated rate at 9–67 keVee. At LZ's one-event fit the DAMA-style amplitude S_m is 3.5 × 10⁻⁸–2.1 × 10⁻⁷ cpd/kg/keV: 10⁴ below DAMA/LIBRA's statistical reach, zero below 8.7 keVee, and non-sinusoidal (S_m > S₀) for δ ≥ 350 keV. No NaI experiment can see it.

**Question and approach.** What S₀ and cosine amplitude S_m (phase 2 June) does the LZ best fit predict in DAMA/LIBRA's keVee bins, how do they compare with DAMA, COSINE-100 and ANAIS-112, and what exposure is needed? WimPyDD O₁ inelastic spectra on ¹²⁷I (1 TeV; δ = 300–380 keV; P003 convention), normalised as in P015 to 1.0 LZ event, are evaluated on 73 days (Baxter-2021 halo with Earth's motion), converted to keVee with Q_I = 0.09 (0.06–0.12) and iodine fraction 0.847, binned in 1 keVee and Fourier-analysed. Sodium (δ_max = 76 keV) is blind. Sensitivity: σ(S_m) = √(2B/(ℰΔE)).

**Results.** Rates reproduce P015. Windows: 8.7–67.0, 15.1–52.6, 18.7–46.4, 24.2–38.7 keVee for δ = 300/350/366/380 keV; the window-integrated S₀ is 5.4–6.7 × 10⁻⁷ cpd/kg for every δ, so DAMA's 2.46 t·yr holds 0.5–0.6 events among 1–5 × 10⁷ counts (S₀/B ≈ 10⁻⁷).

The phase is 151.9 d in every bin. At δ = 300 keV the signal is a 39–47% cosine; for δ ≥ 350 keV it is a spike around 2 June, zero for 33–66% of the year, so the first harmonic exceeds the mean (limit 2S₀) and a cosine fit captures only 93/80/48% of the variance. DAMA/LIBRA's Poisson sensitivity (B ≈ 1 cpd/kg/keV), σ(S_m) = 1.5 × 10⁻³ per keVee bin, matches its published errors; all bins combined give z ≈ 10⁻⁴; its 8–16 keVee bin (0.0004 ± 0.0005) lies 3 × 10⁴ above the δ = 300 keV prediction. Below 8.7 keVee (5.8 at Q_I = 0.06) the prediction vanishes: DAMA's 2–6 keVee amplitude (0.01–0.02) cannot be LZ-type iodine recoils. COSINE-100/ANAIS-112 (0.2–0.3 t·yr, B ≈ 3) are 5–6× less sensitive; a DAMA-like detector would need 3 × 10⁸–8 × 10⁹ t·yr. Resolution lowers peak S_m by 17–35%; Q_I rescales windows ∝ Q, amplitudes ∝ 1/Q.

**Caveats.** Exposures, backgrounds and DAMA's errors are recalled (the DAMA table is a digitised 2018 figure). O₁ isoscalar only (isovector: −2%, P015); constant Q_I; one mass. Sensitivities assume a flat, stable background and uniform livetime.

**Conclusion.** The LZ event predicts a DAMA-like modulation in NaI — June phase, 9–67 keVee, ≥ 46% modulated — at 10⁻⁷ cpd/kg/keV, 10⁴ below any NaI experiment's reach and absent below 8.7 keVee. NaI can neither confirm nor refute it; iodine modulation matters only after ~15 t·yr of a discriminating detector.
