# P089: Identical spectra, different couplings: the isoscalar/isovector and Lagrangian degeneracies of LZ's Table S6 and the coupling ratios that relate them
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-16
- Primary arXiv category: hep-ph
- Author profile: EFT phenomenology group
- Category (from your taxonomy): EFT
- Builds on / responds to: LZ arXiv:2609.02823 (Theory, LEE section, Tables S6/S7); P003, P012, P016, P027, P031, P032, P044, P057

**Abstract.** We derive the nonrelativistic reduction of all twenty Anand–Fitzpatrick–Haxton Lagrangians, compute their isoscalar and isovector xenon spectra with WimPyDD at 1 TeV, and cluster them in LZ's observed-energy space. LZ's five "constant" pairs and ten isoscalar/isovector degeneracies are reproduced exactly; Table S6's 40 entries contain 24 spectral classes, 14 among the 25 entries at ≥ 3.0σ. Coupling maps (d4 = 4263 d20, d3 = 2132 d17, d19 = 1425 d11) convert results between entries.

**Question and approach.** Which Table S6 entries share a spectrum because nuclear response and q-power coincide, which are merely similar, and how many shapes does the table test? Writing the Lagrangians as four scalar products plus the {V, T, A, Tγ⁵}⊗{V, T, A, Tγ⁵} grid, we reduce each bilinear (heavy DM, q⁰ = 0, m_M = m_N) and build WimPyDD Hamiltonians with q-dependent closures for all 40 L_i^{s,v} and 28 O_i^{s,v}. Efficiency- and resolution-folded spectra (σ_E = 11√(E/248) keV) are compared by Hellinger distance and average-linkage clustering; N_lo (P003) and P016's Z(N_lo) calibration explain the pattern.

**Results.** *Reductions* (details.md Table 1): L6 → (q²/2m_N²)O1 + 2O3; L7 → −2O7 + 2(m_N/m_χ)O9; L9 → (q²/2m_χm_N)O1 − 2O5 − 2[(q²/m_N²)O4 − O6]; L12 → −4[(q²/m_N²)O12 + O15] (nucleon spin longitudinal, so Σ′ and Φ″ cancel, leaving q⁴v²Σ″ + q⁴Φ̃′); L13 → 2O8 − 2O9; L16 → −4O13; L18 → −(q²/m_N²)O11 + 4O15; L19 → 4O14. These reproduce LZ's pairs L1/L5, L2/L8, L3/L17, L4/L20, L11/L14 and give H_sv ≤ 0.032 for precisely LZ's ten s/v-degenerate Lagrangians, ≥ 0.135 for the others.

*Classes.* Effective degeneracies beyond LZ's: L19 ≡ L11/L14 (O14 ≡ O9; H = 0.018), L10s ≡ L10v (0.019), L7 ≡ O7, O6s ≡ O6v (0.008). New separate classes: L12s, L12v (H = 0.30 to O6, 0.24 between them), L16 = O13 (H = 0.29 to O10, 0.45 to O6; not L10-like, contra P044), L6s/v, L9s/v, L13v, L18s/v.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 40 Table S6 entries | 24 | 22 | 21 | 19 |
| 25 entries with Z ≥ 3.0 | 14 | 13 | 13 | 12 |

*Couplings.* c10 = d2 = −2d8; c11 = −(m_N/m_χ)d3 = −2d17 ⇒ d3 = 2132 d17; c6 = −(m_N/m_χ)d4 = 4d20 ⇒ d4 = 4263 d20; c9 = −4d11 = 4d14; d19 = 1425 d11 at equal 200–270 keV rate. LZ's identical-spectrum pairs differ by 0.12σ rms (max 0.4σ): the tables' reproducibility floor.

*Pattern.* P016's Z(N_lo) reproduces the 40 entries to 0.25σ rms: M-coherent spectra (O1, O11; N_lo 35–2770) score 0–1.8σ; q²Σ″/Σ′ (N_lo 2–3) 3.0–3.2σ; q⁴ spectra (O6 0.40, L10 0.20) 3.1–3.4σ; isovector entries score higher wherever an M component is (N−Z)²/A²-suppressed, and O1v's 1.3σ is P032's node effect. Only L18v (N_lo 0.18, predicted ≥ 3.4σ, LZ 2.9σ) is unexplained.

**Caveats.** Anand's table is recalled; L6–L20 are our derivations, validated only through LZ's degeneracy lists and the 0.25σ pattern fit. Relative signs of interfering terms (L6, L9, L13, L18) are convention-dependent (both signs computed). One-dimensional energy proxy, 1 TeV only.

**Conclusion.** Table S6 tests 24 shapes, not 40; the 3σ band holds 14, matching the corpus N_eff ≈ 12–14. Its pattern is the low-energy companion count by response type and q-power; L12, L16, L6v and L9v are genuinely new classes, and L18v deserves LZ's released spectrum.
