# P010: ER leakage: the recombination fraction required and the ¹²⁴Xe/¹²⁵I double-vacancy hypothesis
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-04
- Primary arXiv category: hep-ex (cross-list physics.ins-det)
- Author profile: nuclear/particle experimentalists with xenon-detector background expertise
- Category (from your taxonomy): BKG
- Builds on / responds to: LZ paper (arXiv:2609.02823) Discussion, Table I, Fig. 4, Fig. 5, Tables S3–S4; dossier P000 §6.3; complementary to P004 (wall MSSI) and P009 (NEST energy scale)

**Abstract.** If the LZ event were a ≈70 keV electron recoil it would need a recombination fraction r = 0.939 against a β-mean of 0.720, a 6.4σ charge deficit on LZ's band width. A common 20% charge suppression for inner-shell-vacancy decays reproduces LZ's 21 ¹²⁴Xe ROI events but yields ≤10⁻³ event-like leaks from 564 ¹²⁴Xe KK decays; any tail heavy enough to give O(1) event predicts 1–9 events in the empty gap above it. ER leakage is not viable.

**Question and approach.** What recombination fraction and fluctuation would an ER have to suffer, how many ¹²⁴Xe/¹²⁵I/¹²⁵Xe/¹²⁷Xe/⁸³ᵐKr candidates existed, and does the empty region between the event and the ER band allow an unmodelled tail? We compute quanta (N_ph = 4910, N_e = 269, N_q = 5179), NEST-LZ mean yields (Table S3), the Table S4 σ_p fluctuation model, and digitise the Fig. 4 bands to fix the model width. Lines at 64.3 and 67.3 keV are placed along constant-N_q loci with a charge suppression f, four tail shapes are compared, and expected counts in an event-like region V (S1c 500–600, log S2c 3.90–4.00) and the gap G (S1c 475–600, 4.00–4.15) are confronted with 1 and 0 observed.

**Results.** E_ee = 69.6 keV (W = 13.44 eV); a 64.3 keV assignment needs W = 12.4 eV, and the event's N_q is 1.6σ above the ¹²⁴Xe KK line, 0.7σ above ¹²⁵I. With α = 0.18, N_i = 4379 and r_req = 1 − 269/4379 = 0.939: 958 of 1226 mean electrons missing. Table S4 gives σ_p = 0.068, but σ_p·N_i over-predicts the drawn band twofold; k = 0.50 reproduces the digitised 10–90% half-widths (0.088/0.103/0.125 vs 0.090/0.102/0.121 dex) and gives σ(N_e) = 150 e, a 6.4σ deficit. LZ's "6.7σ" is the distance in half-widths (6.8), i.e. 8.7 Gaussian σ. Quanta conservation forces any KK or K-capture decay entering the ROI to S1c > 481 or 506 phd, where LZ's IC+EC model holds 0.002 events, so the 21.0 ± 6.3 ¹²⁴Xe ROI events are low-energy and KL/KM modes: summing all modes of 779 decays gives N_ROI = 15.6/21.3/67.6 at f = 0/0.2/0.3, pinning f = 0.20. At f = 0.2 the KK line leaks N_V = 2×10⁻⁷–8×10⁻⁴, the ¹²⁵I K line ≤0.02 (10⁴ decays), the continuum ≤3×10⁻³; extrapolating LZ's Fig. 5 plateau (7×10⁻⁴/bin, cut at +1σ_NR) gives 2.8×10⁻³, 18× the modelled floor. N_V ≳ 0.1 needs f ≥ 0.3–0.4, and then N_G = 1–9 with P(0) ≤ 5–20%. The ¹²⁵I rate at +8 d is 40% of its post-AmBe peak.

**Caveats.** The NEST v2.4.5 combination of σ_p with N_i is inferred, not known; we calibrate to the drawn band and carry the raw width as an excluded upper bound. Tail shapes beyond ~4σ are unmeasured; branching ratios and T½ are recalled. Fig. 5 values are read by eye (±30%). The ¹²⁵I normalisation is unknown to ×30.

**Conclusion.** A 70 keV ER needs r = 0.94, a 6.4σ deficit; with the 20% suppression that fits LZ's own ROI counts, ≤10⁻² leaked events are expected, and heavier tails are refuted by the empty gap. The ER-leakage explanation is disfavoured at ≲0.3%.
