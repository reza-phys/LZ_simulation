# P090: Propagating the 100 % MSSI uncertainty into the local and global significance: what a factor-2, factor-10 or mis-shaped wall/RFR background does to the 3.4σ
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-16
- Primary arXiv category: physics.data-an (cross-list hep-ex)
- Author profile: particle-statistics group
- Category (from your taxonomy): STAT
- Builds on / responds to: LZ arXiv:2609.02823 (Table I, S1/S2, MSSI supplement and comparison table); P052 (engine), P004, P033, P053, P070, P073, P079, P008, P071, P016, P001

**Abstract.** LZ assigns a 100 % uncertainty to its 0.0049-event MSSI background and profiles only its rate. With P052's reconstructed three-sample (S1c, d) likelihood we propagate rate-prior, rate-scale and shape deformations of the MSSI model into the L10 local and global significance. MSSI is 34 % of the background at the event; the local significance drops below 3σ/2σ/1σ only for fiducial MSSI ×2.9/×59/×977, and every route below 2σ is excluded by LZ's sidebands or contradicts its published MSSI shape.

**Question and approach.** How fragile is the 3.4σ against the least-tested background? We refit P052's likelihood (L10, 1 TeV: asymptotic 3.11σ, toy 3.03σ, LZ 3.4σ) with t_MSSI = e^u and (i) Gaussian 100 %, log-normal σ_ln = 0.7/1.5/2.3 or flat priors; (ii) a fiducial rate factor f, either common to the six wall bins of LZ's MSSI table (0.03/0.1/0.5 science, 0.1/0.3/2.2 prompt; 0/0/0, 1/1/0 observed), added as Poisson terms, or fiducial-only; (iii) the wall depth scale λ from P079's 1.23 cm to P033's 4.3 cm, turning the 5.4 t annulus anchor into a fiducial factor f_λ = 6.25/ratio(λ) and a position likelihood ratio from P070's volume profile; (iv) d- and S1c-reshaping; (v) N_eff(Z) from P071 (14.2 at 3.4σ).

**Results.** Baseline = P052 (q0 9.66; MSSI ×2 → 2.98σ); without MSSI 3.29σ: the channel is worth 0.19σ.

*Priors.* Without sideband terms, Gaussian / log-normal 0.7/1.5/2.3 / flat give 3.11/3.12/3.00/2.65/1.76σ, the H0 fit inflating MSSI to 0.006–0.81 events (the sub-band ROI caps it near one event). With the six wall bins profiled, every prior returns 3.16–3.18σ (k̂ = 0.62, k < 1.64, as P004).

*Rate ladder.* Fiducial-only ×2/×5/×10/×50: 3.07/2.87/2.67/2.07σ; Z = 3/2/1 at f = 2.9/59/977. A common factor leaves 3.17–3.18σ for any f because the sidebands pin k·f, with sideband p = 0.044/0.005/5×10⁻¹²/10⁻⁷⁸ at f = 2/2.9/10/59.

*Shape.* λ = 4.3 cm implies f_λ = 7.3 → 2.76σ position-blind, but LR_pos = 6.6 at the event makes a position-aware fit 3.16σ (λ = 1.23 cm: 3.17/3.29σ). MSSI on the NR band costs 0.04σ; all MSSI at S1c > 500 phd (×9.7) gives 2.67σ; g_max = 38 (all MSSI in the neighbourhood) 2.15σ. RFR ×100: 2.87σ but 2150 HE-SB events (18 seen).

*MSSI-only.* Z < 2 needs f·g = 54: rate alone is excluded at 10⁻⁷¹; shape alone cannot; g_max with f = 1.4 survives the counts (p = 0.17) but contradicts Fig. 5 (×10 in S1c, ×3 in d). Globally: 2.34σ (LZ-anchored 2.59) baseline, 1.93 (2.21) for ×10, 2.03 (2.30) for λ = 4.3 cm, 1.41 (1.72) for g_max.

**Caveats.** P052's binned surrogate (rms 0.42σ) sits 0.29σ below LZ; global values use P071's N_eff(Z) mapping (±0.03σ) and P052's toy offset (−0.08σ), not new toys. f_λ rests on P079's contour integrals and P070's volume profile (LR within 15 % of P070). Shape deformations are hypotheses about LZ's simulation, tested only against Fig. 5 and the sideband counts; RFR assumed wall-shaped.

**Conclusion.** The 100 % rate uncertainty is the least dangerous part of the MSSI systematic: sidebands pin the rate and the ROI caps it. Only shape can move the result (≤ 0.5σ); LZ should report shape nuisances anchored to the annulus and HE-SB distributions, or use position.
