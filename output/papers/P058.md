# P058: If the excited state survives: exothermic down-scattering signals in LZ's data and the lifetime window they exclude
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-11
- Primary arXiv category: hep-ph
- Author profile: dark-sector cosmology and direct-detection phenomenologists
- Category (from your taxonomy): COMP (IDM)
- Builds on / responds to: LZ arXiv:2609.02823 (Fig. 4, Fig. S4, MSSI table); P026, P011, P021, P038, P016, P002, P034; feeds P072

**Abstract.** Inelastic-DM readings of the LZ event leave f₂ ≈ 0.4–0.5 in χ₂ after freeze-out (P026). A surviving χ₂ down-scatters, χ₂N → χ₁N, with no velocity threshold. With WimPyDD (δ < 0) the spectrum is broad (68 %: 73–206 keV at δ = 300 keV) and 80–93 % inside LZ's ROI, not a line at E* = δμ/m_N. LZ's counts give f₂ < 2.8×10⁻³ (300 keV) to 1.7×10⁻⁷ (380 keV): τ(χ₂) < (8–3)×10¹⁶ s, m_A′ ≳ 2.4 GeV.

**Question and approach.** Where do exothermic recoils land, which LZ regions constrain them, and what follows for τ(χ₂), the dark-photon ε and m_A′, and for the idea that the event *is* a down-scatter? We compute O₁ kernels K(E, v) with WimPyDD for isoscalar, proton-only (dark photon) and Higgsino (Z) couplings at 400/1000/4000 GeV, δ = ±(250–380) keV, on a 24-day Baxter halo, and count events per unit coupling in 5.4–125, 125–200, 200–270 keV (P016's counts 42/58.6, 0/0.02, 1/5.7×10⁻⁴) and in the empty 600–1700 phd region (0 observed, 0.6 MSSI; Fig. S4, P038 edges). A joint Poisson likelihood with the coupling fixed by μ_endo = 1 gives f₂; τ_min = t_U/ln(f₂,fo/f₂,max).

**Results.** E* = |δ|μ/m_N = 267–339 keV for δ = 300–380 keV (1 TeV), but the window half-width μv√(2μ|δ|)/m_N is 200–225 keV at 250 km/s, so the F²-weighted spectrum peaks at 155–180 keV: 91/9/0.2 % (δ = 300) and 80/19/0.6 % (380) lie below 270 / in 272–670 / above 670 keV. Per endothermic ROI event and unit f₂ there are R_ROI = 916/2.8×10⁴/2.9×10⁵/8.1×10⁶ exothermic events, 59 % of them in the empty 125–200 keV bin. Joint 90 % CL limits (μ_endo = 1; iso, p, Higgsino): f₂ < 2.8, 3.1, 2.4×10⁻³ (300 keV); 5.7, 9.7, 3.5×10⁻⁵ (350); 4.8, 11, 3.2×10⁻⁶ (366); 1.7, 5.7, 3.6×10⁻⁷ (380), hence τ(χ₂) < 8.4–8.6, 4.6–5.1, 3.6–4.1, 2.9–3.2×10¹⁶ s and m_A′ ≥ 2.39, 0.92, 0.53, 0.25 GeV. The 125–200 keV bin dominates; 600–1700 phd alone is 3–6× weaker. Limits are 1.5–3× weaker than P011's criterion, yet τ_min and the α_D-independent m_A′ floor move ≤ 30 %. With the coupling free, f₂ is unconstrained: the exothermic population can replace the event (κ ≈ 10⁻⁷, Δ(−2lnL) = 0.7). *Event as down-scatter.* 248 keV sits at the 97–99.7th percentile of the accepted exothermic spectrum at δ = 278 keV; the hypothesis predicts 2.2–9 companions in 125–200 keV per 200–270 keV event, reaches four-bin Z = 2.7–3.2 with the signal shrunk to 0.07–0.26 events, and needs τ(χ₂) ≳ t_U.

**Caveats.** One-dimensional true-energy regions with erf efficiencies (P038's edges extrapolated to 1700 phd); P016's digitised low-energy counts; μ_endo = 1 conditional on the endothermic interpretation; O₁ only; halo enters through the endothermic denominator (×0.7–1.6 June/December at δ = 300 keV, ×0.4–10 at 350); χ₂ width and f₂,fo from P011/P026.

**Conclusion.** A surviving χ₂ would flood LZ's 125–200 keV bin, not the region above the edge: f₂ < 3×10⁻³–2×10⁻⁷ and τ < (3–9)×10¹⁶ s, confirming P026's dark-photon floor; the Higgsino is unaffected. The event is a spectrally poor exothermic candidate (99th percentile), which P072 should test with LZ's full likelihood.
