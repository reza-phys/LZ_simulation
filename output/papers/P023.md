# P023: Composite dark-baryon dark matter with a 300 keV hyperfine splitting: a UV origin for the LZ event?

**Abstract.** Can a composite dark-matter state have a spin-flip partner split by δ ≈ 300 keV at 1 TeV without tuning? A heavy dark quark dressed by a QCD-like sector can: δ = (B*−B)(m_b/m_Q)(Λ_D/Λ_QCD)² gives 300 keV for Λ_D = 0.42 GeV. Hyperfine partners couple only through spin-dependent operators; the M1 moment giving one LZ event, 2.1×10⁻⁴ μ_N, fixes τ(χ₂→χ₁γ) = 66 μs and caps δ ≤ 326 keV. A G_F-strength axial Z spin flip gives 0.64 events.

**Question and approach.** Which composite regime makes δ/M ≈ 3×10⁻⁷ natural, which mediator drives the transition at the LZ rate, and what do lifetime, self-interactions and relic abundance imply? We write the Coulombic (δ = α_eff⁴m_Q/3), HQET heavy–light (δ = K/m_Q, K calibrated on B*/D* and Σ_Q*/Σ_Q; 1/m_Q law confirmed to 5 %) and light-baryon (δ ~ 0.3M) laws, validated on hydrogen (1418.8 MHz) and positronium (204.4 GHz), and solve them for δ = 250–400 keV, M = 0.3–4 TeV. Rates use WimPyDD with P012's photon-dipole coefficients (elastic normalisation reproduced to 0.4 %), a q²/(q²+m_A′²) dark-photon factor, and O₄ with c₄ = 4√2G_F g_A^χ a_N.

**Results.** *Naturalness.* At 1 TeV, δ = 300 keV needs α_eff = 0.037 (Coulombic), but a confining SU(3)_D at that coupling has Λ_D = 8.5 eV and a thermal coupling gives 3.7 keV: an asymmetric dark atom. The heavy–light law needs Λ_D = 0.42 GeV (meson-like) or 0.63 GeV (Σ_Q-like); at Λ_D = Λ_QCD it predicts 184 keV. A light-constituent baryon needs a 10⁻⁶ suppression. *Transition.* Scalar and charge couplings cannot flip spin: the Higgs portal is out; the dark photon acts through an M1 moment. Photon-M1 at 1 TeV: μ_tr(N = 1) = 2.1×10⁻⁴ μ_N = 0.22 e/(2m_χ) at 300 keV (14× P012's excluded elastic moment, allowed since N_lo = 0) but 3.4×10⁻³ μ_N at 366 keV. The heavy–light hadron then needs ε = 2.6×10⁻⁵–5.5×10⁻⁴ (massless–1 GeV A′, α_D = 0.1), 30–600× P011's vector value; the dark atom needs millicharge 0.05. The Z-axial O₄ channel (T₃ = ½) gives 0.64 events at 300 keV, versus 790 for the Z-vector Higgsino. *Lifetime.* Γ = μ²δ³/π gives τ = 66 μs (50 m) at 300 keV but 0.14 μs (10 cm) at 366 keV; τ ≥ 12.7 μs (P_in ≤ 10 %) requires δ ≤ 326 keV (306–350), closing P007's 358–380 keV window to photon-M1 models. *Cosmology.* η_D = 4.4×10⁻¹³; σ/m ≤ 1.6×10⁻⁵ cm²/g; glueballs ≈ 2.3 GeV must decay before BBN.

**Caveats.** The spin-½ MiDM basis stands in for a 0→1 transition (O(1) spin factor); g_tr, g_A^χ and the c₄ normalisation carry factor-2 risks (N = 0.16–2.6 at 300 keV); the δ ceiling moves ≲ 10 keV with halo or spin factor; attenuation length, hadron inputs and portal bounds are recalled; millicharge and glueball cosmology are qualitative.

**Conclusion.** A 300 keV splitting at 1 TeV is natural for a heavy–light dark hadron with Λ_D ≈ 0.4 GeV (or an asymmetric dark atom); the transition must be M1- or axial-type. Photon-M1 models fit LZ only for δ ≲ 326 keV; an axial Z spin flip fits at 300 keV.
