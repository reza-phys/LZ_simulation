# P069: The xenon world in one likelihood: what LZ, XENONnT and PandaX-4T (past, untouched and future) jointly say and should jointly do

**Abstract.** Of ≈22 t·yr of xenon-TPC data on disk, only LZ's 2.84 t·yr has been examined above 200 keV. We build one exposure ledger (LZ SR1/SR3/untouched, XENONnT, PandaX-4T, archival LUX/PandaX-II/XENON1T) and propagate LZ's best fit through each dataset's ROI edge. The unexamined 19.1 t·yr should hold 2.3 (L10) to 5.2 (δ = 366 keV) events at 200–270 keV; P(≥1 somewhere) = 0.90–0.995. Two found events give 5.5σ; zero exclude the best fit. We propose a pre-registered joint reanalysis.

**Question and approach.** Ledger (t·yr): LZ 0.77 (SR1, 4.71 t FV) + 2.84 + 6.76 untouched; XENONnT 3.1 analysed (ROI ≲ 60 keV) + ≈3.0 untouched; PandaX-4T 1.54 (≲ 100 keV) + ≈2.5; XENON1T 1.0, PandaX-II 0.36, LUX 0.09. The untouched XENONnT/PandaX-4T exposures are recalled and uncertain (ranges 2–5, 1.5–3.5). Signals: WimPyDD L10 and inelastic O₁ (1 TeV, δ = 300/350/366/380 keV; P050 spectra) normalised to 1.0 event per 2.84 t·yr in LZ's ROI and folded with LZ's roll-off (P038) or the published hard edges (P005). Counting in 200–270 keV with b = 5.7×10⁻⁴ per 2.84 t·yr (P016; brackets 2×10⁻⁴–0.0106); flat-prior Gamma intervals; P001/P020 posterior (π_DM = 0.01, π_U = 0.1, L_U = 0.1); P034 timing; trials factors via N_eff.

**Results.** *Ledger.* 22.0 t·yr on disk, 19.1 (16–24) never examined above ≈100 keV; published low-energy ROIs tested 0.20 (L10)/0 (inelastic) of LZ's events. *Hidden 200–270 keV events* (best fit, L10/δ366; ×0.30–2.36): LZ untouched 0.82/1.85, XENONnT 0.73/1.67, PandaX-4T 0.49/1.10, archival + LZ SR1 0.27/0.61; totals 2.30/1.20/3.40/5.23 for L10/δ300/350/366. P(≥1) = 0.90/0.70/0.97/0.995 (best fit), 0.50/0.30/0.64/0.79 (×0.30). *Scenarios* (N_new in 19.1 t·yr):

A free-rate unknown background caps P(DM) at 0.09. *Trials.* N_tot = 3: 5.4σ global with three pre-registered spectra, 5.1σ through LZ's 616-model set, 4.9σ from three separate papers. *By mid-2027* (21.4 t·yr new to end-2026) μ = 2.6 (L10)–5.8 (δ366); P(5σ) = 0.73/0.98 (best fit), 0.18/0.52 (×0.30); zero events exclude 0.89/0.39 of the best fit.

**Caveats.** Untouched XENONnT/PandaX-4T exposures are recalled (±50%); other TPCs' high-energy acceptance and MSSI are assumed LZ-like; hard ROI edges approximate; counts in true energy (resolution moves ≈10% across edges); Asimov medians; P(DM) rests on P001's judgemental U; 400 keV-window counts for δ ≥ 366 keV conflict with LZ's empty sideband (P038).

**Conclusion.** The decisive test is already on disk: 19 t·yr of unexamined xenon data with 2–5 expected events. A coordinated, pre-registered 200–270 (400) keV reanalysis reaches 5σ or excludes the best fit by mid-2027; separate 616-model papers would cost ≈0.5σ of trials and years.
