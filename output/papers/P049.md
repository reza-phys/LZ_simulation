# P049: Muon-induced high-energy neutrons at 4850 ft: an independent estimate of untagged single scatters above 8 MeV and the meaning of the 41-minute-earlier muon
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-10
- Primary arXiv category: physics.ins-det (cross-list hep-ex)
- Author profile: cosmogenic-background specialists
- Category (from your taxonomy): BKG
- Builds on / responds to: LZ paper (arXiv:2609.02823) Discussion and supplement "Neutrons"; extends P013's muon channel; complements P004, P022

**Abstract.** LZ bounds muon-induced neutron single scatters (SS) with a MUSUN+GEANT4 simulation (UL 4.6×10⁻⁴, 80% tagging) and notes an OD muon 41 min before its 248 keV event. Rebuilding the chain from recalled fluxes, yields, spectra and water attenuation, we find 334 neutrons above 8.2 MeV enter the water tank in 220 d, 0.49 reach the LXe, giving 8×10⁻⁴ untagged SS of any energy and 1.3×10⁻⁵ (3×10⁻⁶–5×10⁻⁵) in 200–270 keV. The 41-min gap is statistically ordinary and physically irrelevant.

**Question and approach.** How many untagged single 200–270 keV scatters do rock neutrons from muons produce, independently of LZ's simulation, and what do the 41-/127-min muon gaps mean? Chain: Φ_μ = 5.3×10⁻⁹ cm⁻²s⁻¹, Y_n = 3.5×10⁻⁴ n/(μ g cm⁻²), two-component spectrum (f(>8.2 MeV) = 0.17, f(>100 MeV) = 0.03), escape depth 20–40 cm, tank receiving area 116 m², TPC fraction 0.040, removal Σ = n_Hσ_H(8.2/E)+n_Oσ_nonel below 50 MeV and λ = 0.7/1.0 m above, P013's mono-energetic SS probabilities (1.0–2.6×10⁻⁴ per neutron entering the LXe), LZ's 20% untagged. Muon rates through cylinders use I ∝ cos^3.5θ; gaps are exponential.

**Results.** Rates: water tank 14 h⁻¹ (gap 4.3 min), GdLS envelope 3.9 h⁻¹ (15 min), TPC 0.57 h⁻¹ (105 min). P(gap ≥ 127 min | TPC) = 0.30; P(gap ≥ 41 min) = 0.068 for GdLS-crossing muons but 7×10⁻⁵ for any tank-crossing muon, so LZ's tag selects scintillator-crossing muons. Only muons within ≲ 1 ms could matter: P = 3.9×10⁻⁶. β-n emitters (⁹Li 178 ms, ⁸He, ¹⁷N 4.2 s) are 350–20 000 half-lives dead after 41 min and give E_n ≤ 2 MeV → E_R ≤ 61 keV.

Neutrons: current out of the rock above 8.2 MeV 1.5×10⁻¹¹ cm⁻²s⁻¹; 334 enter the tank (90/126/117 at 8–30/30–100/>100 MeV); 13.4 aimed at the TPC; survival through 2.5 m water S = 4×10⁻⁸ (10 MeV), 6×10⁻⁴ (20), 0.028 (50), 0.082 (≥100 MeV), leaving 0.49, 78% from > 100 MeV.

LZ's UL decomposes exactly as 2.303/1200 yr × 0.602 yr × 0.20 × 2 = 4.62×10⁻⁴. Dominant uncertainties: untagged fraction (×6; our crude GdLS-light/Skin/Gd-capture estimate gives 2–7%), water path (×3), spectrum knee and λ_att (×2.3), yield (×2).

**Caveats.** Every physics input is recalled (20 items) with ×1.5–2 ranges; the attenuation of >100 MeV neutrons through 2–3 m of water, including cascade regeneration, is treated as a single exponential; cavern shape, top/bottom OD tanks and steel are absorbed in the path range; SS probabilities inherit P013's cross sections and black-disk pattern; the GdLS-envelope dimensions are uncertain.

**Conclusion.** An LZ-independent estimate reproduces LZ's simulated bound within a factor 2 and P013's window expectation: 1.3×10⁻⁵ untagged 200–270 keV single scatters, ≥ 5000× short of one event even at the all-high corner. The 41-min and 127-min muons are ordinary gaps and cannot be causally linked to the event.
