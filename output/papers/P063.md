# P063: Spontaneous fission of ²³⁸U in LZ's detector components: multiplicity, veto probability and the odds of a lone 248 keV recoil
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-12
- Primary arXiv category: physics.ins-det (cross-list nucl-ex)
- Author profile: radioassay and neutron-background specialists
- Category (from your taxonomy): BKG
- Builds on / responds to: LZ paper (arXiv:2609.02823) supplement "Neutrons", samples paragraph, Table I; extends P013's fission channel; uses P049, P029, P004

**Abstract.** ²³⁸U spontaneous fission (SF branching 5.45×10⁻⁷) emits ν̄ ≈ 2 neutrons and ~6.5 γ's within femtoseconds. We build a component-resolved ²³⁸U inventory, normalise it three ways, transport the prompt γ's through a simplified LZ geometry and fold in P013's neutron transport factors. Recalled activities give 28 (10–63) fissions in 220 d, but a lone 200–270 keV recoil needs every companion silent: 1.5×10⁻⁹ expected events (4×10⁻¹¹–1.5×10⁻⁸), 16× P013's 9×10⁻¹¹, eight orders short of one.

**Question and approach.** How many fissions occurred in LZ's materials, and with what probability does one leave exactly one 200–270 keV single scatter (SS) while its other neutrons and all γ's evade the TPC (> 3 keV), Skin (> 2.5 phd, ±0.25 μs), OD (> 4.5 phd, ±0.3 μs) and the 600 μs capture window? Route A: recalled radioassay values. Route B: LZ's ≤ 0.118 untagged detector-NR SS → 187 (α,n) neutrons entering the LXe (P013 factors, 8 % untagged) × SF/(α,n) ratio 1.1×10⁻² for PTFE. Route C: a multiple-scatter (MS) neutron-sample cap. Per-γ silent probabilities come from a sampled straight-line MC (XCOM-like attenuation, Compton continuation in Ti/glass, OD deposits visible with probability 0.9); P(ν) is Terrell (2.01, 1.08); γ multiplicity Poisson or a narrow Gaussian with k ≥ 1.

**Results.** Route A: 2.7 (0.95–6.1) Bq of ²³⁸U → 27.6 fissions, dominated by the Ti cryostat (11.4) and PMTs (12.9); PTFE contributes 0.1. Only 0.29 (0.10–0.67) SF neutrons above 8.19 MeV are emitted per run. Route B bounds fluorine-bearing components at ≤ 2.0 fissions, reproducing LZ's "two orders below (α,n)"; route C (≤ 3 unexplained MS events, P(MS per fission) = 0.34) gives ≤ 8.7. γ silence is location-dependent: p_γ = 0.0036 (PTFE wall: γ's must cross ≥ 6 cm of Skin LXe or the TPC), 0.134 (Ti cryostat), 0.148 (PMT arrays) — P013's uniform 0.15 is right for the latter, 40× too generous for the wall. Hence f_γ (narrow model) = 5×10⁻⁵ (wall), 2.7×10⁻³ (cryostat), 3.1×10⁻³ (PMTs); Poisson tails raise the wall value 30×. Companion neutrons: f_n = 0.156 (p_n = 0.1; floor 0.12 from ν = 1). Per fission,

P_lone = ν̄ · p_enter · p_win · u · f_n · f_γ = (1.4×10⁻¹²)–(6.3×10⁻¹¹),

with p_win = 1.6×10⁻⁶ (P013 Watt MC, per neutron entering) and self-untagged u = 0.1. Totals in 220 d: **1.5×10⁻⁹** (Poisson γ's 1.9×10⁻⁹; no-companion bound 3.2×10⁻⁶; effective silent factor 4.6×10⁻⁴ vs P013's 3.8×10⁻⁴). Sensitivity: Ti/PMTs at assay upper limits ×2.2; u = 0.2 ×2; all Ti/glass interactions silent ×30; route C ×0.32; route B ×0.07 (= P013). Stacked: 4×10⁻¹¹–1.5×10⁻⁸. Conversely, the same fissions predict 13 TPC deposits, 8.6 TPC multiple scatters and 9.8 fissions with ≥ 2 delayed captures per run, all prompt-tagged (P(≥ 1 visible γ) ≥ 0.997).

**Caveats.** Material activities, γ multiplicity/spectrum, attenuation tables, geometry and per-neutron silent probabilities are recalled or estimated (×2–3 each; Ti ²³⁸U is an assay upper limit). Straight-line γ transport ignores scattering angles; p_win is P013's wall-source value for all locations; the ≤ 3 MS tolerance is an assumption, not a published count.

**Conclusion.** Even with a ²³⁸U inventory ten times LZ's (α,n)-implied fission share, spontaneous fission yields ≤ 10⁻⁸ lone 200–270 keV recoils in 220 d. Its real signature — prompt γ's plus multiple delayed captures — lets LZ bound the fission rate directly in the MS-neutron and OD-multiplicity samples.
