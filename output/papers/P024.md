# P024: How wide is the NR band at 250 keV? Sensitivity of the "1.5σ below the median" statement to the yield break and fluctuation model
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-08
- Primary arXiv category: physics.ins-det
- Author profile: NEST/LXe microphysics modellers
- Category (from your taxonomy): RESP
- Builds on / responds to: LZ arXiv:2609.02823 (Data Analysis, Figs. 2/4/S1a, Tables S3–S5); P009, P010, P004, P013

**Abstract.** LZ describes its NR band twice: the candidate is "1.5σ below the median", while Figs. 2/4 draw 10–90 % lines; they disagree by ×1.55. We decompose the band variance at 250 keV with nestpy, scan every NEST fluctuation parameter and measure the width from LZ's AmBe points. Electron counting statistics impose a 0.023 dex floor; the model gives 0.031, AmBe 0.036 ± 0.005, the drawn lines 0.021. The 1.5σ reading is right: P(≤event|NR) ≈ 6 %.

**Question and approach.** What sets σ(log₁₀S2c) at S1c ≈ 540 phd, how does it depend on `nr_er_width_parameters`, the p(E) break, g₁/g₂, field and detector terms, how well can ~20 AmBe events constrain it, and what fraction of the NR PDF sits in the MSSI contours? We run nestpy 2.1.1 `GetQuanta` with the LZ_WS2024 width vector and Table S5 yields (P009's contour energy scale; Table S5 as printed as a variant), flat spectrum 200–320 keV, slice |S1c − 540| < 10 phd, detector: binomial S1, extraction ε = 0.80, SE Fano 4, 2 % position residual. We digitise the Fig. 2/4 lines with tick calibration (0.0005 dex), count and fit the purple AmBe points, and read the Fig. S1a contours.

**Results.** *Decomposition (248 keV; N_e = 301, N_i = 2012, r = 0.850).* σ = 0.0313 dex from recombination binomial 0.0231, extraction 0.0125, position 0.0087, SE Fano 0.0085, ω term 0.0083, ion Fano 0.0062 and slice mixing 0.0061, matching the MC. The tunable ω term is 7 % of the variance: its Gaussian is centred at electron fraction 0.5, an NR has 0.06.

*Sensitivity.* Width parameters ±30 % change σ by ≤ 3 % except W₃ (×0.7 → 0.048) and W₄ (×1.3 → 0.038); g₂ ±3 % moves the median (event 1.10–1.99σ); position residual 0/3/6 %: 0.030/0.033/0.041; binomial floor 0.023. Without the p(E) break the event is 5.0σ low. Baseline: event 1.54σ low, P(≤obs) = 5.7 %.

*Drawn lines.* Half-width 0.0271 ± 0.0004 dex → σ 0.0212, event 2.33σ, whereas the paper's 1.5σ implies σ = 0.0328. The drawn width is below Poisson on the model's 300 electrons (0.025) and needs electron fluctuations ×0.41.

*AmBe.* Fig. 2 shows 19 points at 500–580 phd: median ±0.007 dex, width ±16 %. Residuals of 31 single points about the median: sd 0.036 ± 0.005 dex, Δln L = 11.4 for 0.031 vs 0.021; 4/31 lie at or below the event's depth.

*MSSI.* Lower edges at 540 phd: 3.988 (68 %; event 0.021 below), 3.695 (95 %); NR PDF inside 81 %/100 %.

**Caveats.** ε_ext and the position residual are assumed (they move the event between 1.2 and 1.7σ; g₂ ± 3 % between 1.1 and 2.0σ). The NEST variance form is recalled but verified numerically. AmBe widths come from 31 raster-digitised points including out-of-FV events. The ER-band convention (P010: 6.7σ = 6.8 half-widths) remains unexplained.

**Conclusion.** The NR band at 250 keV is ≥ 0.023 dex by counting statistics and 0.031–0.036 dex in model and data; LZ's dashed lines under-draw it by 1.5×. The event is a 1.5σ (P ≈ 6 %) band member, matched by 4 of 31 AmBe points, and indistinguishable from MSSI.
