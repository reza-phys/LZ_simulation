# P030: Streams and substructure: can a fast dark-matter stream produce the 248 keV event without a low-energy population?
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-08
- Primary arXiv category: astro-ph.GA (cross-list hep-ph)
- Author profile: Galactic-archaeology-minded dark-matter theorists
- Category (from your taxonomy): HALO
- Builds on / responds to: LZ arXiv:2609.02823; P002, P003, P006, P007, P018; overlaps P055, P097

**Abstract.** A cold stream is a nearly monochromatic velocity component. Can it (a) remove the low-energy population predicted by elastic scattering or (b) extend the inelastic reach beyond δ_max? Not (a): O1's nuclear response alone fixes ≥ 1180 events in 5.4–55 keV per event in 200–270 keV. For (b), bound streams cannot raise δ_max = 387 keV, but a 1 % retrograde stream near v_esc boosts the δ = 380 keV rate ×50; no known substructure is fast enough.

**Question and approach.** We build Earth-frame halo functions for (1−f_s) SHM + f_s Gaussian stream (σ = 20 km/s; v_lab = |u_s − v_obs|) analytically, validated against WimPyDD (η₀ to 10⁻⁶; rates 1.5–6 % below its bin-averaged tail), and feed them to WimPyDD O1 per-stream kernels (1 TeV; P007 Higgsino coupling σ_n = 7.4×10⁻³⁹ cm²; LZ efficiency; 2.84 t·yr; 16 June). Retrograde bound streams are integrated exactly with a v_esc truncation; known substructures use recalled, flagged kinematics.

**Results.** *Elastic.* A stream above 339 km/s gives a spectrum flat in E_R times the nuclear response, so N_lo ≡ R(5.4–55)/R(200–270 keV) is v_lab-independent: 1182 for O1 (SHM 2569; Helm 353, its node lying at 279 not 267 keV), 11.7 for O4, 1.32 for O10, 0.19 for O6. Streams lower N_lo by only ×2.2 for every operator (P003). *Inelastic.* δ_max grows 0.82 keV per km/s: 248 keV needs v_lab ≥ 801/825/886 km/s at δ = 380/400/450 keV. Pure-stream Higgsino counts at δ = 380 keV are 63, 1.0×10⁴, 2.4×10⁵ for v_lab = 750/800/900 km/s (SHM 0.31), so one event needs f_s = 1.1 %, 7×10⁻⁵, 3×10⁻⁶. Above δ_max, δ = 400 keV needs f_s = 0.10 % (800), 1.3×10⁻⁴ (825), 6×10⁻⁶ (900 km/s); δ = 450 keV needs 0.34 % (850), 9×10⁻⁵ (900). Below the monochromatic threshold these rely on the Gaussian tail: f_s(400 keV, 800 km/s) spans 2.7–0.010 % for σ = 5–40 km/s (Fig. 2). *Ceiling.* Bound streams (v_gal ≤ 544 km/s, v_lab ≤ 810) cannot change δ_max(248 keV) = 387 keV (402 keV for a 270 keV recoil on ¹³⁶Xe). Truncating a v_gal = 500/530/544 km/s retrograde stream at v_esc removes 1.5/25/51 % of it; a 1 % stream then boosts the rate ×7/23/30 at δ = 350 and ×8/35/51 at 380 keV, and reaches δ = 400 keV only for v_gal ≥ 530 km/s, f_s ≥ 5 %, via 257–300 keV recoils on ¹³⁴Xe/¹³⁶Xe. *Modulation.* v_lab varies by ±14.6 km/s (anti-aligned stream, SHM phase) to ±29.8 km/s (ecliptic-plane directions); a retrograde v_esc stream at δ = 350/380 keV modulates with peak/mean 1.55/2.22 versus 2.57/3.30 for the SHM. *Known substructures* (recalled): Sagittarius v_lab ≈ 390–415, S1 ≈ 575, retrograde shards ≈ 545, Sausage ≈ 360–370 km/s give δ_max ≤ 194 keV and boosts ≤ 1.09 (S1, 10 %) or 0.80 (Sausage) at δ = 300–350 keV.

**Caveats.** Isotropic Gaussian streams at fixed ρ₀; recalled substructure kinematics (S1 dispersion isotropised); O1 shapes only (P018); erf efficiency; Δη upper-boundary bias ≤ 6 % at δ = 380 keV; 16 June halo, so P007's annual window shifts up ~10 keV.

**Conclusion.** Streams cannot rescue elastic SI (N_lo ≥ 1180). Only a retrograde stream within ~15 km/s of v_esc helps inelastic DM, boosting the rate ×30–50 just below δ_max = 387 keV; δ beyond ~400 keV needs unbound dark matter. No known substructure qualifies.
