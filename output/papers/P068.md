# P068: A joint astrophysical uncertainty band on everything inferred from the LZ event: ρ₀, v₀, v_esc, v_⊙, the tail shape and the halo frame
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-12
- Primary arXiv category: astro-ph.GA (cross-list hep-ph)
- Author profile: astroparticle systematics working group
- Category (from your taxonomy): HALO
- Builds on / responds to: LZ arXiv:2609.02823; P018 (single-parameter halo tail), P043 (detector counterpart), P012, P021, P007, P034, P002, P035, P030

**Abstract.** Extending P018's single-parameter scans, we propagate a joint prior — ρ₀ 0.3–0.5 GeV cm⁻³, v₀ 220–250, v_esc 500–600 km/s (correlated), V_⊙ ± 5 km/s, Lisanti tail index k = 0–2, three frames — through 400 Latin-hypercube halos to d₁₀, κ̂(δ), the Higgsino window, δ_max, the modulation amplitude and the 248 keV percentile. v_esc controls every inelastic quantity; astrophysics exceeds P043's detector systematics by ×5 (d₁₀) to ×300 (κ̂).

**Question and approach.** What are the marginal 68/95 % bands and correlations of the numbers the corpus quotes, which input dominates each, and how do they compare with P043? All halos are isotropic in the Galactic frame, so P018's cap-limited η(v_min) becomes a one-dimensional Gauss–Legendre integral; the tail is Lisanti's [exp((v_esc² − v²)/kv₀²) − 1]^k, sharp at k → 0. Cached WimPyDD per-stream kernels (O₁ unit coupling; P007 Higgsino; P012 L10; 1 TeV; LZ efficiency; 2.84 t·yr) give rates in the Sun frame, on 16 June and for the 12-day annual mean. Sensitivities are binned first-order variance indices.

**Results.** The reference halo reproduces P018, P007, P012, P002 and P034 to ≤ 2 %. Frame drawn from the prior [reference in brackets]:

κ̂ is infinite (no coupling yields one event) for 2.5/13/26 % of halos at δ = 350/366/380 keV, so upper edges there are unbounded. The frame carries ≤ 4 % of any variance although at the reference point June/annual = 1.4/2.4/3.2/3.9 and annual/Sun = 1.08/1.7/4.6/11 (δ = 300/350/366/380; P035). v₀ ranks second (index 0.19–0.45), the tail index third (0.21 → 0.1 with rising δ). Versus P043 the astro 68 % half-width is ×5.5 the detector 1σ for d₁₀ (equal at fixed ρ₀), ×11 for δ_max (31 vs 3 keV), ×160/300/80/19 for κ̂ at 300/350/366/380 keV, but ×0.33 for pct(248|300); δ_H is detector-insensitive but ±28 keV astrophysically. Gaia-central v_esc (528–580 km/s): δ_H = 362 (340–379), δ_max = 395 (373–412) keV. P007's exclusion at δ ≤ 350 keV holds for 57 % of the prior; P034's exposures (∝ a₁⁻²) vary ×5.4 across the a₁(300) band.

**Caveats.** Priors are recalled ranges, not likelihoods; the +0.5 correlation is assumed; isotropic halos only (no Sausage or streams: P018, P030); O₁ shapes at 1 TeV; no energy resolution (P021's κ̂(380) is ×1.35 ours); 12-day a₁; d₁₀ inherits DR-001. P018's k ≥ 2 form also shrinks the bulk (η ratio 0.018 vs our 0.20 at k = 2, 700 km/s).

**Conclusion.** For every inelastic quantity the systematic is v_esc; for d₁₀ it is ρ₀. Quote d₁₀ = 0.25 ± 0.02, δ_H = 355 (+30/−26) keV, δ_max = 390 (+31/−32) keV, κ̂(366) = 3 × 10⁻³–150; 248 keV stays above the 99th percentile at δ = 300 keV.
