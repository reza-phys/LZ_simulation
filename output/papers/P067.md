# P067: Directional and multi-target strategies to confirm a high-energy recoil population: what a 250 keV recoil track would look like and who could see it
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-12
- Primary arXiv category: hep-ex (cross-list astro-ph.IM)
- Author profile: directional-detection experimentalists
- Category (from your taxonomy): PROJ

**Abstract.** A 248 keV xenon recoil is a 0.15 μm track; only low-pressure heavy gases (mm tracks) or emulsions read directions. We map which directional targets can scatter for the LZ-favoured interpretations, compute recoil-direction distributions with the Baxter halo, the SURF geometry at the event time and rates at LZ's fit. Inelastic recoils near the kinematic ceiling form a beam within 21° of the wind axis, but fluorine detectors are blind and a 1000 m³ xenon-gas TPC needs decades.

**Question and approach.** (a) Which nuclei in existing or proposed directional detectors (CYGNUS He:SF₆, CF₄/CS₂ TPCs, NEWSdm AgBr emulsions, CF₃I or Xe gas, CaWO₄) can scatter for δ = 300–380 keV, and whose track is imageable? (b) What recoil-direction distributions follow for inelastic O₁ at 248 keV and the elastic L10 spectrum, and how many events give a 3σ anisotropy with 30° resolution and 70 % head–tail efficiency? (c) Where would the 16 June recoil have pointed at SURF? (d) How does direction fit the confirmation programme? Kinematics via lzcommon; directions by importance-sampled Radon transform of the boosted truncated Maxwellian (toy-validated); L10/O₁ kernels from WimPyDD; SURF alt-az from the astropy ephemeris with an analytic GMST transform; rates from P012/P015/P050 normalisations.

**Results.** *Who scatters.* Ceilings μv²_max/2 (1 TeV, 16 June): F 63, S 106, Br 253, Ag 333, I 386, Xe 398, W 533 keV (A ≥ 96–125 for δ = 300–380 keV): F/He/S/C/O directional detectors are blind to inelastic DM, silver misses the 358–385 keV Higgsino window, only I, Xe, W are live (tracks: Fig. 1). *Directions.* θ_R ≤ arccos(v_min/v_max) = 65°/30°/19°/14.5°/8° (δ = 0/300/350/366/380): at δ = 366 keV all recoils lie within 21° of the anti-apex (⟨cos γ⟩ = 0.97 vs 0.59 elastic Xe, 0.70 F L10). With 30°/70 % head–tail, N_3σ = 30/25/24/23 (inelastic), 50 (F L10), 69 (Xe elastic); ideal 3–9; axial 4.6 at δ = 366. *SURF.* Apex at altitude −0.5°, azimuth 347°: the recoil would have been horizontal (alt +1°, −13…+14°), heading SSE (az 167° ± 11°). *Rates at LZ's fit.* Fluorine 0.146 per t·yr (125 kg: 2700 years to 3σ); xenon gas (287 kg per 1000 m³, 40 Torr) 0.12/0.22/0.60/3.6 events/yr → 246/111/41/6 years; Ag emulsion 2 × 10⁻³ per 100 kg·yr.

**Caveats.** Track lengths, gas-TPC resolution and head–tail efficiency are recalled or assumed (N_3σ ∝ 1/(2ε−1)²); δ = 380 keV, the only fast directional case, conflicts with LZ's empty 600–1000 phd region (P038, P050); inelastic rates use P015's window fractions and the Sun-frame L10 normalisation; tail cases carry 10–15 % MC uncertainty; one mass, one halo.

**Conclusion.** Direction is the most striking signature of inelastic DM — a collinear beam — and the least accessible: light-nucleus directional detectors cannot scatter, emulsions stop at 333 keV, heavy-gas TPCs collect < 1 event/yr per 1000 m³. Counting, isotopes and tungsten decide first.
