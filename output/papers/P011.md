# P011: Dark-photon-mediated pseudo-Dirac dark matter: fitting the LZ event

**Abstract.** A pseudo-Dirac fermion split by δ and coupled to a kinetically mixed dark photon scatters inelastically off protons with a free cross-section σ_p = 16παα_Dε²μ_p²/m_A′⁴. With WimPyDD the σ_p giving one LZ event is 1.2×10⁻⁴² (δ = 250 keV) to 2.3×10⁻³⁹ cm² (365 keV) at 1 TeV; the proton-only spectrum converts to LZ's isoscalar intervals with a factor ≈ 4, not (A/Z)² = 5.9. Requiring χ₂ to have decayed excludes sub-GeV dark photons for δ ≤ 350 keV.

**Question and approach.** Which (m_χ, δ, m_A′, ε²α_D) give one event, how does the coupling compare with P007's gauge-fixed Higgsino, and is it compatible with dark-photon searches, the relic density and the χ₂ lifetime? In the Majorana basis the A′ current is off-diagonal (χ̄γ^μχ = iχ̄₁γ^μχ₂), so scattering is χ₁N → χ₂N with c_p = 4π√(αα_D)ε/m_A′², c_n = 0 (WimPyDD c⁰ = c¹ = c_p). We compute counts with P007's machinery (WimPyDD kernels, June/annual/Sun-frame Baxter halos, LZ efficiency, 2.84 t·yr) for m_χ = 300, 1000, 3000 GeV, δ = 200–415 keV, plus light mediators (q-dependent coefficient), the Higgsino on the same kernels (1–6 % agreement with P007) and exothermic χ₂ down-scattering.

**Results.** The proton form-factor node sits at 284–291 keV instead of 257–267 keV (isoscalar), so the effective isoscalar→proton conversion is 4.2, 3.9, 2.7 at δ = 250, 300, 350 keV. LZ's converted intervals (1.5–30×10⁻⁴² cm² at 300 keV) contain σ_p(N=1), their upper edges being 3.3–3.9 events.

A light A′ (0.3/0.1 GeV) suppresses the rate by 0.45/0.034 at δ = 300 keV. The relic density (⟨σv⟩ = πα_D²/m²) fixes α_D = 0.0073/0.0245/0.073. With δ < 2m_e, χ₂ decays only to χ₁νν̄ via Z mixing, Γ = 3G_eff²δ⁵/(120π³), G_eff = g_Dε tanθ_W g/(4cosθ_W m_Z²): τ = 60 Gyr for ε = 10⁻⁶ (δ = 300 keV, α_D = 0.1). A surviving χ₂ would give 858 (δ = 300) to 2.7×10⁶ (380 keV) exothermic events at the fitted σ_p, so ε ≳ 4.4–7.8×10⁻⁶. With ε ≲ 10⁻³ (BaBar, recalled) the window is m_A′ = 5.0–57, 2.6–34, 0.9–14, 0.5–8.5, 0.09–3.8 GeV at δ = 250–380 keV; the lower bound is α_D-independent.

**Caveats.** Halo choice shifts σ_p(N=1) by ×1.4–4 (June vs Sun frame at δ ≥ 350 keV); intervals converted through counts, not a shape-aware likelihood; BaBar/NA64/LHCb limits and the annihilation formula are recalled; the χ₂ floor assumes hypercharge mixing and our leading-order width.

**Conclusion.** Unlike the Higgsino, dark-photon pseudo-Dirac DM fits the LZ event at any δ = 204–387 keV with ε ≈ 10⁻⁷–10⁻³, untestable by accelerator searches for δ ≤ 350 keV; the requirement that χ₂ has decayed excludes sub-GeV dark photons and defines a GeV-scale window.
