# P071: Alternative look-elsewhere treatments for the LZ model scan: Bonferroni, Šidák, Gross–Vitells upcrossings, the Bayesian trials factor and the pre-registration counterfactual
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-15
- Primary arXiv category: physics.data-an
- Author profile: look-elsewhere methodologists
- Category (from your taxonomy): STAT
- Builds on / responds to: LZ arXiv:2609.02823 (LEE supplement, Tables S6/S7); P008 (competing), P027, P001, P045, P021, P051, P032, P018, P006

**Abstract.** LZ's 3.4σ → 2.6σ correction is a toy-MC minimum-p over its own 293 spectra. Re-using P008's likelihood with 4 × 50,000 toys we compare look-elsewhere methods and search spaces. Bonferroni (1.3σ), eigenvalue M_eff (3–444 trials) and Gross–Vitells (−45% to +30%) all fail; calibrated toys give N_eff = 14.2 ± 1.1 (2.59σ) after removing a 12% finite-calibration bias. Pre-registering 6 or 30 spectra would have yielded 3.1–3.3σ or 2.85σ; the corpus's ~200 added variants lower the honest global value to 2.50σ.

**Question and approach.** How does the global significance of one 3.4σ event depend on the LEE method and on who defines the search space? We import P008's one-dimensional extended likelihood (2.92 background events, WimPyDD spectra), add 161 spectra the corpus has since tested (P051 light mediators, P032 isospin ratios, P018/P006 halo/date variants, P021's δ scan) and run two independent 50,000-toy calibration/evaluation pairs over 654 models. Because a minimum over noisily calibrated p-values is biased, we re-calibrate on disjoint sub-samples (6,250–50,000 toys) and extrapolate to infinite calibration. Methods: Bonferroni/Šidák; five eigenvalue estimators on correlation matrices of Gaussianised Z, √q₀ and tail indicators; Gross–Vitells with toy-counted upcrossings (1D δ scan) and Euler characteristics (2D grids); P027's Bayesian factor; counterfactual subspaces.

**Results.** *Calibration bias.* N_eff(3.4σ) = 20.6, 17.9, 16.7, 15.9 for 6.25k–50k calibration toys; extrapolated **14.2 ± 1.1 ± 0.7, Z_global = 2.59σ** (LZ 13.9; P008 12.2). *Methods at local 3.4σ.* Bonferroni 293: 1.29σ; given any exceedance, 33 spectra exceed together (95 at 2σ), so bounds err by ×21 in p. Eigenvalue estimators span 3–444; only the participation ratio on tail indicators tracks the toys (4.4/5.7/7.5 vs 3.9/5.4/10.6 at p ≤ 0.1/0.01/0.001). Gross–Vitells: toy upcrossings of the calibrated δ-scan field scale as e^{−c₀²/2} to c₀ = 2.5 (N_up e^{c₀²/2} = 0.48–0.54) but rise 40% at 3.0; extrapolations give N_eff = 5.0–7.3 vs 10.1 directly; 2D grids 13.8 vs 10.8. Bayesian: B_max/B_DM = 10.3 (2.70σ) is M/(29 spectra sharing the observed evidence); the frequentist N_eff is M/(32 spectra sharing an ensemble exceedance) — the same structure. *Threshold.* N_eff ∝ Z^{1.8} but the cost Z_local − Z_global stays 0.72–0.81σ from 2σ to 3.4σ (≈ ln N_eff/Z); at 5σ: N_eff = 24–39, cost 0.65–0.76σ. *Search space* (Fig. 3): one model 3.45σ; six a-priori spectral families 3.09σ (six same-window inelastic spectra 3.29σ); 30 spectra (one mass + inelastic grid) 2.85σ; 140 heavy elastic 2.96σ; inelastic grid 2.69σ; LZ-like 2.59σ; + δ scan to 400 keV 2.53σ (+19% N_eff); + light mediators 2.50σ (+9%); + isospin, halos 2.50σ (+2%). Corpus/LZ ratio 1.32 → 2.50–2.55σ.

**Caveats.** One observable, no nuisance profiling, P008's background (its variants move N_eff 8.7–13.2). The 5 × 10⁴-toy cap forces the extrapolation, whose 1/N vs 1/√N forms differ by ±1.1. Our operator basis and "a-priori" choices are ours, not LZ's Lagrangians. GV 2D grids are coarse (3 masses nearly degenerate). Corpus variants are shape proxies (analytic propagator; O₁ only for isospin/halo).

**Conclusion.** Only toy min-p gives the right answer; bounds, eigenvalue and Gross–Vitells methods err by factors 2–20 in p. The value 2.5–2.7σ is robust across LZ, P008, this work and the Bayesian analogue, but the search space is not: 3.1–3.3σ if pre-registered narrowly, 2.50σ after the community's reinterpretations.
