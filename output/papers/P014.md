# P014: Collider constraints on electroweak-multiplet inelastic dark matter after the LZ event
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-05
- Primary arXiv category: hep-ph
- Author profile: collider phenomenologists
- Category (from your taxonomy): COMP
- Builds on / responds to: LZ arXiv:2609.02823; P007 (Higgsino fit, gaugino table), P002 (kinematic floors); competes with P048

**Abstract.** The Higgsino reading of the LZ event (P007) needs μ ≳ 260 GeV and δ ≈ 360 keV. We compute the collider-relevant chargino splitting and lifetime and map recalled LEP/LHC bounds onto it. Δm± = 342 ± 10 MeV at 1 TeV (cτ = 0.71 cm), independent of δ to 0.2 %: no existing search probes it. Every P007 fit point, including the thermal 1.1 TeV Higgsino, is collider-unconstrained, and HL-LHC will not change this; only FCC-hh or a 10 TeV muon collider would.

**Question and approach.** Do LEP and LHC searches allow the doublet (Higgsino) and triplet (wino) masses and splittings that fit LZ, and what would HL-LHC and future colliders add? We evaluate the one-loop electroweak splitting Δm_rad(M) in Feynman-parameter form (asymptotes α m_Z/2 and α₂m_W(1−c_W)/2 reproduced), add the tree-level O(δ) piece from P007's 4×4 diagonalisation, derive the χ±→χ⁰π±, ℓν widths from the W-exchange operator (normalised so the wino gives the standard cτ ≈ 6 cm), map recalled bounds onto the P002/P007 region, and fold recalled 13 TeV Drell–Yan cross-sections (factor ~2) into pair counts and disappearing-track survival probabilities.

**Results.** Δm_rad rises from 258 MeV (100 GeV) to 342 MeV (1 TeV) and 352 MeV (4 TeV), approaching α m_Z/2 = 356 MeV; the wino gives 161 MeV. The tree piece is (1–21)×10⁻⁴ δ ≤ 0.8 MeV, so Δm± = 314–353 MeV across the five fit points and changes by 0.19 % for δ = 100–400 keV: collider limits are δ-blind. Higgsino at 1 TeV: BR(π, e, μ) = 0.92, 0.05, 0.03, τ = 0.024 ns, cτ = 0.71 cm; wino cτ = 5.8 cm. LEP (92–103 GeV) sits below the LZ floor m ≥ 259 GeV. Disappearing-track limits (ATLAS: wino < 660 GeV, Higgsino-σ < 210 GeV, both at τ = 0.2 ns) do not transfer: a four-hit tracklet needs r > 12 cm and P(r > 12 cm) = 5×10⁻⁸ (βγ = 1) or 2×10⁻⁴ (βγ = 2) for cτ = 0.71 cm, versus 0.17–0.42 for the wino. Soft-lepton searches need Δm ≳ 5 GeV; monojet reach ≲ 150 GeV. Produced χ±χ⁰ pairs (σ ≈ 200, 23, 0.67 fb at 300, 500, 1000 GeV): 2.8×10⁴, 3.2×10³, 93 in Run 2; 6.0×10⁵, 6.9×10⁴, 2.0×10³ at HL-LHC. A toy calibrated on the wino exclusion yields ≤ 5 (300 GeV) and 0.02 (1 TeV) HL-LHC tracklets even at βγ = 2. χ₂ cannot decay to e⁺e⁻ (δ < 1022 keV); τ(χ₂→χ₁νν̄) ≈ 5×10⁵ s, τ(χ₂→χ₁γ) ~ 0.06 s: invisible at colliders. A Y = 0 triplet's neutral state has no Z coupling, so a wino cannot be the LZ scatterer without a new mediator (Fig. 1).

**Caveats.** All bounds are recalled (flags in details.md); the Higgsino cross-section carries a factor ~2 uncertainty and is extrapolated above 1 TeV; the one-loop splitting has a 3–7 % scheme spread (α(m_Z) vs α(0), on-shell vs MSbar c_W); the tracklet toy uses fixed βγ; the prospects (HL-LHC ≲ 0.3 TeV, FCC-hh ≈ 1 TeV, muon collider) are recalled projections.

**Conclusion.** Every Higgsino mass that fits LZ (≥ 259 GeV, thermal 1.1 TeV included) is allowed and unprobed by LEP, the LHC and HL-LHC; the fit predicts Δm± = 342 ± 10 MeV, cτ ≈ 0.7 cm, so a Higgsino tracklet with τ ≈ 0.2 ns would refute it.
