# P033: Geometric probability of a wall-MSSI topology with a 12 keV Compton scatter 27 cm inside the TPC: a photon-transport Monte Carlo
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-09
- Primary arXiv category: physics.ins-det
- Author profile: detector-simulation specialists
- Category (from your taxonomy): BKG
- Builds on / responds to: LZ paper (arXiv:2609.02823) supplement MSSI section; refines P004; uses P022, P024, P029

**Abstract.** LZ's MSSI reading of the 248 keV event needs a 12 keV Compton scatter 26.9 cm from the wall, 26.4 cm above the cathode, plus 77 keV (wall) or 204 keV (RFR) in a dead region. A weighted photon-transport Monte Carlo shows this topology's fiducial deposit falls as e^{−d/4.3 cm} from the FV edge: 1 % (0.16–1.7 % by source) reaches the event's position class, giving ≈ 2 × 10⁻⁶ expected events and k ≳ 1.6–6 × 10⁴.

**Question and approach.** What fraction of MSSI histories (one 8–16 keV FV deposit; 60–95 keV in the ≤ 3 mm dead shell or 150–260 keV in the 13.75 cm RFR; nothing else active) place the FV deposit at d ≥ 25 cm, z ≥ 20 cm? We track 352–2615 keV photons from the wall, bottom, top and cathode planes and ²¹⁴Pb β+γ decays (β deposited locally) in a 72.8 × 145.6 cm cylinder with exact Klein–Nishina scattering, recalled XCOM-like photoabsorption (λ = 2.8/6.3/9.1 cm at 352/1000/2615 keV) and a simplified FV (8 cm stand-off, 9–132.8 cm). Survival biasing, depth-uniform forced FV collisions, forced 8–16 keV deposits and dead-region splitting give unbiased weights, verified against analog transport (pulls ≤ 1.2σ).

**Results.** A 12 keV deposit needs θ = 6.4° (1 MeV) to 2.4° (2615 keV) (KN probability 0.96–0.24 %). Above 609 keV, 42–46 % of the 12 + 77 keV wall class lies within 2 cm of the FV edge; the density is 70–140× lower at the event's distance:

| f_pos(wall12) | 609 keV | 1.1–1.5 MeV | 2615 | mix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| wall | 0.0022 | 0.008–0.015 | 0.026 | 0.014 |
| top | 0.0037 | 0.016–0.022 | 0.035 | 0.017 |
| bottom / cathode | 0.0001 | 0.001–0.002 | 0.005 | 0.0017 |

Equal mix 0.0098; 25 % longer λ gives ×2–3, isotropic emission ×0.9–1.4, a 1.1 mm shell scales the rate (×0.43) not the shape. All ROI wall MSSI share the profile (0.011); energy-class fraction 0.053 vs P004's f_nb 0.035. The RFR class is 6.8× more probable overall but 81 % sits at z < 20 cm; at the event RFR/wall = 1.5, yet LZ's 10⁻⁴ RFR expectation leaves 1.4 × 10⁻⁸.

Normalisation: 0.0048 × 0.035 × f_pos = 1.65 × 10⁻⁶ (2.7 × 10⁻⁷–2.9 × 10⁻⁶ by source) → k_required(10 %) = 6.4 × 10⁴ (3.7 × 10⁴–3.9 × 10⁵; ≥ 1.6 × 10⁴ all variants) versus k < 1.64. Independently, LZ's 8.5 detector-ER single scatters reproduce its 0.0048 wall MSSI to 15 %.

**Caveats.** Attenuation coefficients, TPC dimensions, ²¹⁴Pb branches and the line mix are recalled (λ bracket dominates, ×2–3 on f_pos). No PTFE/skin back-scatter, free-electron Compton, uniform shell and stand-off; the latter over-accepts the bottom corner, where our RFR-MSSI ROI rate exceeds LZ's by ~100, so RFR numbers are relative to LZ's model.

**Conclusion.** A 12 keV MSSI scatter 27 cm inside the TPC is a 1 % tail of a 10⁻⁴-level background: ≈ 2 × 10⁻⁶ expected events, mismodelling ≳ 10⁴ against a sideband-allowed 1.6. P004's f_pos narrows from 10⁻⁴–0.12 to 0.002–0.03.
