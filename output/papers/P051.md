# P051: Inelastic dark matter with a light mediator: how a propagator lighter than the momentum transfer reshapes the 250 keV recoil spectrum and the LZ fit
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-11
- Primary arXiv category: hep-ph
- Author profile: light-mediator dark-sector phenomenologists
- Category (from your taxonomy): IDM
- Builds on / responds to: LZ arXiv:2609.02823; P002, P003, P011, P021, P025, P026, P038

**Abstract.** For χ₁N → χ₂N through a mediator with m_med ≲ q ≈ 0.25 GeV, the propagator m_med⁴/(m_med²+q²)² tilts the recoil spectrum toward the low edge of the kinematic window. With WimPyDD O₁ spectra at 1 TeV the tilt makes 248 keV *more* extreme at δ = 300 keV (99.6th → 99.8th percentile), leaves the likelihood peak at δ = 380 keV, raises the predicted 100–200 keV population by 70 %, and needs ε ≈ 10⁻⁷, 50–150× below the χ₂-decay floor.

**Question and approach.** Does a light dark photon, Z′ or scalar rescue δ ≈ 300–350 keV, and at what cost? We implement c(q) = c₀m_med²/(m_med²+q²) for m_med = 0.05–1 GeV and contact, isoscalar (LZ unit coupling, WimPyDD c⁰ = 2/m_v², P003) and proton-only (dark photon, P011), δ = 250–390 keV, on the full window to 720 keV, for the Sun-frame, 16 June and 12-day annual-mean Baxter halos. Light mediators enter as the per-energy propagator factor at the mean xenon mass, validated against full WimPyDD q-dependent kernels (|ΔZ| ≤ 0.007σ, ≤ 6 % in rate). Efficiency: P038's erf edges (E50 = 271.6 and 423.2 keV); resolution 11√(E/248) keV; statistic: P021's companion-free q₀ = 2[ln(f̃/b) − 1 + b/f̃], b = 5.7×10⁻⁴/70 keV.

**Results.** *Shape (δ = 300 keV, window 90–798 keV).* The M-response node at 266 keV wins: the accepted-spectrum percentile of 248 keV rises from 99.6 % (contact; P002 99.5 %) to 99.8 % at 0.05 GeV. *Likelihood.* Z(δ) peaks at 380 keV for every m_med (Z_peak = 3.49); Z(300) drops 2.67 → 2.41, LR(peak/300) grows 12 → 23, the 68 % range moves up to 369–390 keV. *Coupling (table; α_D = 0.1).* ε saturates at 3.7×10⁻⁸ for m_A′ ≪ q, while surviving-χ₂ down-scattering is less suppressed (N_exo(f₂=1) 850 → 2100), so the χ₂-decay floor stays at (5.4–5.8)×10⁻⁶: m_A′ ≥ 2.6/0.93/0.11 GeV at δ = 300/350/380 keV, α_D-independent. Planck (P025) removes m_A′ < 9 GeV for thermal α_D.

*Predictions.* Per event expected in 200–270 keV, the 100–200 keV band (E⁻ > 55 keV) holds 4.1 → 6.8 events at δ = 300 keV (P(0) 1.7 → 0.11 %), 0.91 → 1.24 at 350; 600–1000 phd events per ROI event fall 30 → 15 (380 keV), 1.05 → 0.37 (350).

**Caveats.** Propagator applied at the mean xenon mass (validated: ≤ 6 % in rate, ≤ 0.007σ); one-dimensional observed-energy proxy with P016's b_H anchor (P021); 10 keV δ grid, and the 390 keV edge truncates the proton-only peak; O₁ only (a light vector with O₄/O₈ admixtures is not covered); accelerator limits recalled (BaBar likely; beam dumps uncertain); χ₂ width to leading order (P011).

**Conclusion.** A mediator lighter than q reshapes the spectrum but not the inference: 248 keV stays at the top percentile for δ ≤ 350 keV, the peak stays at 380 keV, the empty 100–200 keV band is predicted fuller, and the dark-photon coupling falls two orders below the χ₂-decay floor.
