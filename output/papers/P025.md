# P025: Indirect-detection constraints on TeV inelastic dark matter after LZ: γ-rays, antiprotons and the CMB
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-08
- Primary arXiv category: astro-ph.HE (cross-list hep-ph)
- Author profile: indirect-detection phenomenologists
- Category (from your taxonomy): COMP
- Builds on / responds to: LZ arXiv:2609.02823; P007 (Higgsino fit), P011 (dark photon), P012 (L10 dipole), P014 (collider); precedes P075, P084

**Abstract.** We confront the three dark-matter readings of the LZ event with recalled indirect limits. The 1 TeV Higgsino annihilates to WW+ZZ at ⟨σv⟩ = 0.96×10⁻²⁶ cm³/s (×1–3 Sommerfeld), 1.4–10 times below Fermi, H.E.S.S. and AMS-02 bounds. The relic-normalised dark-photon model is Sommerfeld-enhanced by S ≳ 200 at recombination for m_A′ ≲ 1 GeV; Planck excludes m_A′ < 9 GeV at 1 TeV. The L10 dipole WIMP annihilates 10⁴–10⁸ times above bounds unless asymmetric.

**Question and approach.** What annihilation cross-sections and final states follow from (a) a pure Higgsino with δ ≈ 366 keV, (b) a pseudo-Dirac fermion with a light dark photon, (c) a contact dipole with d10 = 0.28, and do Fermi-LAT dwarfs, H.E.S.S., AMS-02 antiprotons and Planck exclude them? We derive threshold cross-sections from a Majorana formula ⟨σv⟩ = g_eff⁴/(8πm²) calibrated on para-positronium and the wino, validate the Higgsino freeze-out formula on the 1.1 TeV thermal mass, compute Hulthén Sommerfeld factors for the secluded model, and estimate L10 annihilation from its operator. All limits are recalled with reliability flags; Higgsino Sommerfeld effects are bracketed (S = 1–3) pending P084.

**Results.** *Higgsino.* With PeV gauginos χ₁ has no diagonal Z or Higgs coupling; only χ₁χ₁ → W⁺W⁻ (chargino exchange, vertex g/2) and ZZ (χ₂ exchange, g/2c_W) survive: ⟨σv⟩_WW = πα₂²/8m², ⟨σv⟩_ZZ = πα₂²/16c_W⁴m², total 9.6×10⁻²⁷ (1 TeV) and 7.9×10⁻²⁷ cm³/s (1.1 TeV), 32 % of the freeze-out value g⁴(21+3tan²θ_W+11tan⁴θ_W)/512πμ², which gives Ωh² = 0.12 at 1167 GeV. Ratios to limits at 1 TeV (S = 1–3): Fermi dwarfs 0.10–0.29, H.E.S.S. Einasto 0.24–0.72 (×10 looser if cored), AMS-02 p̄ 0.10, Planck 0.016; the loop line, 1.2×10⁻²⁸, is 0.3–0.9 of the H.E.S.S. line limit. A 300 GeV Higgsino at full density exceeds the dwarf bound ×3 (Fig. 1).

*Dark photon.* Since χ₁χ₂ → A′A′ vanishes at tree level, σ_eff = πα_D²/2m², so α_D = 0.035 at 1 TeV (P011: 0.0245) and today ⟨σv⟩(χ₁χ₁ → A′A′) = 4.4×10⁻²⁶. The Yukawa enhancement saturates at S_sat ≈ 6α_D m/m_A′ between resonances (m_A′ = 21, 5.3, 2.3, 1.3 GeV…): 2081/225/21 at m_A′ = 0.1/1/10 GeV. Planck (p_ann < 3.5×10⁻²⁸, f_eff = 0.35) allows S ≤ 23: m_A′ < 9.2 GeV is excluded (×10 at 1 GeV), valleys survive at 9–32 GeV and everything above 32 GeV (P011 convention: 5–20 GeV). Against P011's windows, δ = 365–380 keV leaves no m_A′; δ = 300 keV keeps 9–34 GeV. The splitting is harmless (2δ/α_D m_A′ = 0.02); dwarf cascade limits (uncertain) would need m_A′ ≳ 45 GeV.

*L10.* The tensor operator d10/m_v² = (465 GeV)⁻² gives ⟨σv⟩(qq̄) ≈ 3×10⁻²¹–2×10⁻²⁰ cm³/s (the literal (20.9 GeV)⁻⁴ reading gives 2×10⁻¹⁷, violating unitarity): a symmetric relic would be 7×10⁻⁶ of the DM and rescaling d10 runs away. Since χ̄σ^{μν}χ vanishes for Majorana χ, only an asymmetric Dirac relic survives, and it gives no indirect signal.

**Caveats.** Every limit is recalled (×2 for Fermi/Planck/H.E.S.S.-Einasto, ×3–10 for antiprotons, cascades and profiles); the Higgsino line and L10 estimates carry O(3) and O(6) factors; Hulthén resonance positions are approximate (±10 %); positron propagation and J-factors were not computed; d10 carries the DR-001 factor 2.

**Conclusion.** The LZ-fitting Higgsino survives all indirect probes with ≤ 1 decade of margin (CTA-testable); a thermal secluded dark-photon model needs m_A′ ≳ 9–32 GeV, removing its Higgsino-like δ ≳ 360 keV corner; the L10 WIMP is excluded unless asymmetric.
