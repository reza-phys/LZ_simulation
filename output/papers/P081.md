# P081: Posterior predictive counts for LZ's next release: P(0), P(1), P(≥2) high-energy events under the corpus posterior
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-15
- Primary arXiv category: physics.data-an (cross-list hep-ex)
- Author profile: astrostatistics group
- Category (from your taxonomy): STAT
- Builds on / responds to: LZ arXiv:2609.02823; P027, P061, P038, P068, P034, P016, P020, P069, P001

**Abstract.** We fold the corpus posterior — P027's 298-spectrum model average and rate posteriors, P061's hypothesis weights, P038's edge acceptance, P068's halo band and P034's seasonal PDFs — into posterior predictive counts for LZ's next release. Under dark matter (DM) a repeat 2.8 t·yr contains ≥ 1 event in 200–270 keV with probability 0.25 (0.50 above 55 keV); the modelled background gives 5.6 × 10⁻⁴. A new band event kills the modelled and one-off backgrounds (BF ≥ 300) but not a steady unknown; only energy placement does.

**Question and approach.** What will LZ see in 1.0–10 t·yr more, and what would each outcome prove? DM branch: for each spectrum m with P027 weight w_m, p(s | m) ∝ π(s) e^{−s}(1 + ρs/b_H) W_m(s) (P027's likelihood; log-uniform/flat/Jeffreys π); counts in 200–270 keV, 55–200 keV and a 1000 phd extension (270–420 keV, P038 E50 = 423 keV) follow each model's spectral ratios, the window's seasonal factor (P034) and P068's a₁/δ_max spread. Background: b_H = 5.7 × 10⁻⁴, b_M = 0.02 (P016), extension 0.0028 (P038), 55–125 keV 0.10 (estimate). Unknown (P061): one-off, or steady with an Exponential(1) rate. P061's hyper-prior (median P(DM) = 0.015) is propagated in full.

**Results.** The band-rate posterior has mean 0.385 and median 0.19 per 2.84 t·yr (68 % 0.04–0.69): the empty 125–200 keV bin pulls companion-bearing models below 1 (L10 ŝ = 0.42). At 2.8 t·yr the best-fit expectation 0.383 equals the log-uniform posterior mean (0.380) but flat/Jeffreys priors give 0.76/0.57 (companion-free Gamma means 1/2/1.5).

In 55–270 keV, DM P(≥1) = 0.32/0.50/0.66/0.78; with a 1000 phd edge 0.40/0.59/0.73/0.83. A 1.0 t·yr window from 1 April 2024 is summer-heavy (×2–2.6, δ ≥ 350 keV). P(≥1 | DM) = 0.9 needs 123 t·yr in the band (26 in 55–270 keV; 34 for the plug-in best fit). Decision table (2.8 t·yr): N = 1 gives BF_DM:B = 300 but BF_DM:U_steady = 0.67; N ≥ 2: 5 × 10⁵ and 0.32; N = 0: 0.75 and 1.5. Hence P(DM | event, N) stays at 0.014–0.029 for every count. Two events in 270–420 keV give BF 3.8 × 10³ (B), 7.5 × 10³ (U), P(DM) = 0.99; one band + one extension event 0.57; both in-band 0.011. P027's 6.76 t·yr predictive is reproduced (0.915/0.58/0.21 vs 0.92/0.58/0.20); P069's per-model counts agree to +4/+9/+29 %.

**Caveats.** The mixture inherits P061's judgements (f_trans = 0.5; U localised in the band); f_trans = 1 would give P(DM | N = 1) = 0.82. The 55–125 keV background is an estimate. The extension's astrophysical spread (Δδ = 25 keV) is tail-dominated; we quote medians. 1D P016 likelihood; 1 TeV time PDFs; L10-based elastic truncation.

**Conclusion.** Falsifiable forecast: under DM, LZ's next 2.8 t·yr shows ≥ 1 band event with probability 0.25 (0.50 above 55 keV), under background 6 × 10⁻⁴. Any new event excludes the modelled and one-off backgrounds; separating DM from a steady LZ-specific unknown requires events in 270–420 or 55–200 keV, not counting.
