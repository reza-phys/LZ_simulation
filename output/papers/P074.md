# P074: Long-range operators (q⁻², q⁻⁴) versus the LZ event: a systematic map of which mediator masses and operator structures can leave a lone 248 keV recoil
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-15
- Primary arXiv category: hep-ph
- Author profile: light-mediator EFT phenomenologists
- Category (from your taxonomy): EFT
- Builds on / responds to: LZ paper (arXiv:2609.02823, Theory paragraph, Table S6); P003, P012, P031, P040, P044, P051

**Abstract.** We dress every NR operator O₁, O₃–O₁₅ with one or two propagators m²/(m²+q²) and compute, with WimPyDD at 1 TeV, the number N_lo of 5.4–55 keV events per 200–270 keV event for m_med = 0.01–10 GeV and the q⁻², q⁻⁴ limits. Light mediators only soften spectra: no contact-excluded operator is rescued, and the survivors O₆, O₁₅, O₉, O₁₄, O₁₀ need m_med ≥ 0.11, 0.26, 0.29, 0.32, 0.41 GeV. Spectra are contact-like above ≈ 0.46 GeV ≈ 1.8 q(248 keV).

**Question and approach.** P031 found that the operators compatible with a lone recoil need Λ = 1.5–62 GeV, i.e. a light mediator, while P012/P044 showed the photon (m = 0) versions fail. Where does the contact regime end? Since q² = 2m_A E_R, the rate acquires the exact factor [m²/(m²+q²)]^{2n} (n propagators), which we apply to WimPyDD contact spectra (Sun-frame Baxter SHM, P003 efficiency) and validate against full q-dependent WimPyDD closures (≤ 2.2 % in N_lo). We tabulate N_lo(i, m_med, n), the minimum mediator mass for N_lo ≤ 5 (3), the 248 keV percentile, the 100–200 keV yield and the coupling per event, decompose the photon magnetic dipole, and derive the long-range SI scaling analytically.

**Results.** *Map* (Fig. `P074_heatmap_Nlo`). N_lo rises monotonically as m_med falls. For N_lo ≤ 5 (≤ 3): O₆ needs m ≥ 0.106 (0.143) GeV, O₁₅ 0.258 (0.42), O₉ 0.293 (0.49), O₁₄ 0.324 (0.62), O₁₀ 0.412 (never); two propagators: 0.22–0.61 GeV. N_lo is within 50 % of contact for m ≥ 0.45–0.48 GeV (n = 1) or 0.66–0.69 GeV (n = 2), operator-independent to ±3 %: m_med ≳ 1.8–2.8 q(248 keV). Below 0.10–0.14 GeV spectra are fully long-range. *Long-range SI* (O₁): N_lo = 2754 → 3021/6222/5.3×10⁴/7.7×10⁵ at m = 1/0.3/0.1/0 GeV, ∝ m^{−1.94} at 0.1–0.3 GeV (n = 2: m^{−3.96}, limit 4.9×10⁸); analytically N_lo(q⁻⁴)/N_lo(contact) = ⟨E⁻²⟩_lo/⟨E⁻²⟩_hi = (214/12.8 keV)² = 280 (pure ∫dE/E²: 129). *Collapse.* O₆/q² reproduces O₄ (27.8 vs 28.1) and O₁₅/q² O₁₂ (99.6 vs 100.3); q²-rate operators overshoot (O₁₀/q² 353, O₉/q² 506, O₁₁/q² 4.0×10⁴; P044's photon EDM). *Survivors.* A 0.3 GeV mediator moves 248 keV from the 93.5th to the 95.9th percentile (O₆), raises the 100–200 keV yield per event 2.0 → 2.8 and the coupling c^s m_v² 2.9 → 4.7. *Photon dipole* (N_lo = 375): the c₅ charge term gives 88 % of R_lo but 1.8 % of R_hi, the spin term 89 % of R_hi, so N_lo ≈ R_lo(charge)/R_hi(spin); with a massive dark photon N_lo = 5.5 (0.3 GeV), 2.40 (heavy limit), compatible above 0.32 GeV.

**Caveats.** N_lo ≤ 3–5 is P003's recalled tolerance (P016: LZ's fit tolerates 1.6–2.2, moving m_min up to ≈ 0.2 GeV for O₆ and leaving only contact-like O₉/O₁₄/O₁₅). Single mass (1 TeV; P003's ×2.9/×0.87 mass scaling carries over), Sun-frame halo, mean-isotope propagator (≤ 3.7 %), tree-level dressing without form-factor running or pion poles (P031).

**Conclusion.** Mediators lighter than ≈ 0.3 GeV coupled to quarks cannot produce a lone 248 keV recoil unless the coupling is purely to spin with q⁴ weighting (O₆ survives to 0.11 GeV); light-mediator SI dark matter is excluded by 10³–10⁹. Elastic interpretations need m_med ≈ 0.5–60 GeV.
