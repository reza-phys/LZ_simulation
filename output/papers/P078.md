# P078: Isospin violation and the isovector suppression factor for xenon at 250 keV: per-isotope amplitudes, the f_n/f_p scan and what it does to the high-energy/low-energy ratio

**Abstract.** We recompute per isotope, from WimPyDD's shell-model M and Σ responses, the isospin dependence of xenon spectra at 1 TeV for f_n/f_p ∈ [−2, 2], elastic and inelastic (δ = 250–380 keV). The neutron form factor's second zeros span 231–259 keV, so 248 keV is proton-dominated for every coupling: isospin violation changes the low-energy rate ×2500 but the 248 keV rate ≤ 20 %. P032's N_lo minimum (7.2 at r = −0.744) is confirmed.

**Question and approach.** Independently of P032, we factorise WimPyDD's rate per isotope, dR/dE = (1000 GeV/m) Σ_i [c⁰²K_i⁰⁰ + 2c⁰c¹K_i⁰¹ + c¹²K_i¹¹] η(v_min,i), with kernels from single-isotope `diff_rate` calls on a stream halo (K_i ∝ W_i(q) to 3 × 10⁻¹⁶; direct-call agreement 9 × 10⁻¹⁵). Signed F_p, F_n follow from sign changes of W_pn = F_pF_n (J ≤ ½ dyads). Sun-frame and annual-mean Baxter halos; P003/P032 efficiency and windows.

**Results.** *Nodes* (keV; each within 0.4 keV of P032): isovector 173–190 (natural 182), isoscalar 251–278 (267), F_p 279–302, F_n 231–259; the natural node enters 200–270 keV only for r ≤ −1.21 or r ≥ 0.89, crossing 248 keV at r = −0.826. *Elastic, 1 TeV* (N_lo = R(5.4–55)/R(200–270); ρ = rate/isoscalar at equal c_p):

N_lo ≤ 10 only for r ∈ [−0.76, −0.73], never ≤ 5; the shape ratio gains ≤ ×378 while ρ(200–270) ≤ 1 for every r ≤ 1. *Xenophobic point:* at 248 keV, −0.7F_n is 10–35 % of F_p (destructive for A ≤ 131, constructive for ¹³²⁻¹³⁶Xe), so the high-q suppression is ×161 weaker than at low q; 7.2 + 9.8 companions (5.4–55, 55–200 keV) remain, ≈ 3σ in P016's likelihood. *Inelastic (annual mean):* 248 keV percentile 99.5/97.6/79.4/70.7 % (δ = 300; r = 1/0/−0.7/−1; equals P032) and 97.8/92.5/68.1/51.0 % (δ = 350); only at δ = 380 does isospin raise the absolute high-energy rate (×1.8–3.4; Sun-frame ×7–19). *Targets:* 90 % ¹³⁶Xe puts the isoscalar node on 251 keV (rate(248) ×0.023, isovector ×1.6) but the window ratio moves only 0.61–1.78, so a two-target 3σ test needs 124–1884 events; ¹³⁶Xe depletion changes rates ≤ 9 %. *O₄:* ⟨S_p⟩/⟨S_n⟩ = 0.029/0.026 (¹²⁹/¹³¹Xe); isoscalar and isovector shapes agree to 5.7 %; N_lo = 28–30 except a spin-cancellation dip to 3.6 at c_n/c_p = −0.025 (rate ×0.0021).

**Caveats.** One-body DMFormFactor-v6 density matrices, common oscillator parameter, no two-body currents: f_n − f_p at q ≈ 250 MeV is schematic, so nodes carry ±10 keV and ρ(248) ~20 % uncertainty; signs are robust. Efficiency and windows from P003/P032. δ = 380 keV numbers are halo dependent (factor 4).

**Conclusion.** We confirm P032 (N_lo ≥ 7.2, node 182 keV) and explain it per isotope: xenon is neutron-blind at 248 keV. Isospin violation buys shape, not absolute high-energy rate below δ = 380 keV; enriched targets cannot decide the coupling with one event.
