# P062: Mass–splitting degeneracy: why a single recoil cannot fix m_χ above 400 GeV, what the onset and endpoint do, and the (m, δ) ridge
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-12
- Primary arXiv category: hep-ph
- Author profile: direct-detection parameter-estimation theorists
- Category (from your taxonomy): IDM
- Builds on / responds to: LZ arXiv:2609.02823 (LEE section, Tables S6–S7); P002, P007, P018, P021, P034, P037, P038, P046, P050; colliders P014/P048

**Abstract.** For inelastic O₁ scattering the xenon spectrum depends on m_χ only through (1 + m_N/m_χ), so LZ tests one mass above 400 GeV. With factorised WimPyDD spectra we map the degeneracy: the ridge mimicking a reference spectrum is nearly flat in δ (300 → 299 keV, 366 → 377 keV from 1 to 4 TeV), not δ ∝ 1/μ. Separating 1 from 4 TeV at 3σ needs ~640 events (shape) or ~240 (modulation); the Higgsino rate does not help.

**Question and approach.** Which combination of (m_χ, δ) does a xenon spectrum fix, and what breaks the rest? O₁ rates factorise as Σ_i K_i(E) η(v_min,i); per-isotope kernels are generated once (1000 GeV; 1/m_χ scaling exact to 10⁻⁶) and combined with Baxter-2021 halos (Sun-frame, 16 June, annual mean, 24 modulation days). Observed pdfs use σ_E = 11√(E/248) keV in a 5–1000 keV window (ideal efficiency) or an LZ-like one. We compute KL maps (41 masses × 126 splittings), the KL-minimising δ_b(m) for references (1000 GeV, 300/366 keV), Fisher matrices in (ln m, δ) with and without a fixed-coupling rate term, exact two-hypothesis 3σ counts, modulation and endpoint fractions along the ridge, and Higgsino δ(N=1) curves anchored to P007.

**Results.** *Ridge.* In v_min(E) = √(E/2m_N)(1 + m_N/m) + δ/√(2m_N E) the mass only rescales one term by 12/6/3 % at 1/2/4 TeV. Hence δ_b = 298.6/300/299.7/299.2/298.7 keV at 0.7/1/2/4/10 TeV for the 300 keV reference and 355.8/366/375.6/380.3/383.0 keV for 366 keV (Sun-frame). The E* = δμ/m_N rule (346/336 keV at 2/4 TeV) has the wrong sign; onset matching (379/385.5) is closer. Below ~700 GeV the 366 keV spectrum cannot be mimicked (400 GeV ceiling 329–342 keV).

*Fisher* (annual, per event, (1000, 366)): σ_δ = 20.9 keV/√N with m fixed (P050: 19–27), 36 with m free, σ_lnm = 2.1, correlation −0.82; the degenerate eigenvector is pure ln m. LZ-like window: σ_δ(m free) = 255 keV/√N. Exact 3σ count, 1 vs 4 TeV: 642 events.

*Modulation.* v_min* = √(2δ/μ) falls from 776 to 756 km/s along the 366 keV ridge: June/Dec = 28/5.6 at 1/4 TeV; timing separates them with 243 events.

*Endpoint.* E₊ = 501 → 738 keV (1 → 4 TeV) but only 0.02–0.9 % of events exceed 500 keV: unobservable, as in tungsten (P046).

*Fixed coupling.* Higgsino δ(N=1) = 312/343/366/375/377 keV at 0.3/0.5/1/2/4 TeV (P007 ≤ 2 keV) runs within 4 keV of the ridge (N_LZ along it 1.01/1.36/1.04/0.52 at 1/2/4/10 TeV). Only the 300 GeV Higgsino, 6 keV below its June ceiling, is separable (8 events).

**Caveats.** O₁ isoscalar only; ideal window, no background; Fisher is local and over-states finite-interval separations (exact counts quoted); Baxter SHM family, whose tail moves δ by ∓11–17 keV (P018); Higgsino normalisation anchored to P007 (our factor 0.046 vs 0.078 reflects isospin conventions).

**Conclusion.** Xenon fixes δ, not m_χ: above ~700 GeV the degeneracy is a flat ridge in δ, the endpoint is invisible and the fixed-coupling rate adds nothing. The mass must come from colliders (P014/P048) or the relic-density prior.
