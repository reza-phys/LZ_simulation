# P054: Pseudo-Dirac dark matter with a heavy Z′: coupling relations, relic density and the LZ event without a dark photon
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-11
- Primary arXiv category: hep-ph
- Author profile: Z′ and gauge-extension model builders
- Category (from your taxonomy): MODEL
- Builds on / responds to: LZ arXiv:2609.02823; competes with P011; uses P007, P021, P025, P026, P037

**Abstract.** We replace P011's light dark photon by a heavy U(1)′ boson (B−L or leptophobic U(1)_B) coupled to a pseudo-Dirac fermion; the off-diagonal vector current gives isoscalar inelastic scattering and s-wave annihilation. LZ fixes G = g_χg_N/m_Z′² = 1.4×10⁻⁷ (δ = 300 keV) to 2×10⁻⁵ GeV⁻² (380 keV). A heavy thermal Z′ yields a coupling-independent 0.1–0.3 events at 300 keV; a Z′ lighter than χ is viable at δ ≲ 340 keV, decays χ₂ quickly and evades Planck.

**Question and approach.** Which (g_χ, g_q, m_Z′, δ) give one LZ event, what relic density follows from χ₁χ₂ → Z′* → ff̄ and χχ → Z′Z′, and are the points compatible with dijet/dilepton limits, a charge-2q_χ scalar origin of δ, and the CMB and exothermic bounds (P025, P026)? Both charge assignments give nucleon charge 1 (g_N = 3g_q, c_p = c_n = G), so we reuse P011's isoscalar WimPyDD grid (annual halo; reproduced live to 0.1 %). We derive and thermally average (Gondolo–Gelmini) the s-wave σ(χχ̄→ff̄), solve the Boltzmann equation and map the maximal thermal, collider-allowed LZ count over (m_Z′, δ) for m_χ = 0.3–3 TeV.

**Results.** At 1 TeV, G(N=1) = 5.1×10⁻⁸, 1.42×10⁻⁷, 1.0×10⁻⁶, 3.6×10⁻⁶, 2.0×10⁻⁵ GeV⁻² at δ = 250, 300, 350, 366, 380 keV. Since g_χg_N = G m_Z′², perturbative couplings confine a TeV Z′ to δ ≲ 340 keV; P021's maximum needs m_Z′ ≤ 0.53 (366) / 0.22 TeV (380 keV) at g_χg_N = 1. *Contact limit:* LZ and ⟨σv⟩ both scale as G²; at 300 keV the LZ coupling exceeds the thermal one by 3.2 (B−L) / 1.8 (U(1)_B), so Ωh² = 0.011 / 0.037 and the density-rescaled count is 0.095 / 0.31 events — one event only at δ = 242 / 272 keV (0.003 at the 2m_χ resonance). *Light Z′ (m_Z′ < m_χ):* Z′Z′ sets the relic (g_χ ≈ 0.7), LZ fixes g_N; benchmarks (Ωh² = 0.120, N = 1):

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| U(1)_B | 500 | 300 | 0.69 | 0.017 | 1.2e-6 |
| U(1)_B | 300 | 340 | 0.67 | 0.026 | 2.1e-6 |
| B−L | 1000 | 300 | 2.83 | 0.017 | 2.4e-6 (Ωh² 0.112) |

Within recalled collider ceilings, thermal U(1)_B reaches N ≥ 1 up to δ = 343/319/277 keV (m_Z′ = 0.5/1/≥2 TeV), B−L (m_Z′/g′ ≥ 20 TeV) only 298/231 keV. Splitting: δ = y_Sv_S/√2, v_S = m_Z′/2g_χ = 0.2–0.7 TeV → y_S ≈ 10⁻⁶ ≈ y_e. Lifetime (B−L, G_ν = G): Γ = 3G²δ⁵/120π³ → τ = 1.7×10¹⁰ s (300) to 2.6×10⁵ s (380 keV), far below P026's 7×10¹⁶ s floor; U(1)_B needs g_χ sinθ_ZZ′ ≥ 1.5×10⁻⁶. CMB: S₀ = 1.2–2.2, ⟨σv⟩ = (2.7–5)×10⁻²⁶ cm³/s, 3–5 % of Planck (none at tree level if m_Z′ > m_χ).

**Caveats.** Collider ceilings are recalled and uncertain (Fig. 1 steps); g* = 86.25 fixed; Z′Z′ formula recalled; Z′–dark-Higgs final states ignored; the Z–Z′ mixing needed by U(1)_B is assumed; N_unit inherits P011's halo (×1.4–4 at δ ≥ 350 keV).

**Conclusion.** A heavy Z′ can host the LZ event without a dark photon, but only for m_Z′ ≲ m_χ and δ ≈ 250–340 keV; it reaches neither P021's 365–385 keV corner nor, as a ≥ 2 TeV thermal mediator, δ above ≈ 280 keV.
