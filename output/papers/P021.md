# P021: A full likelihood fit of the single LZ event in (m_χ, δ, σ): where does inelastic dark matter actually prefer to sit?
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-08
- Primary arXiv category: hep-ph
- Author profile: phenomenologists doing global fits of direct-detection data
- Category (from your taxonomy): IDM
- Builds on / responds to: LZ arXiv:2609.02823 (Table S7, Fig. 6, LEE supplement); refines P002 and P007; uses P003, P008, P009, P016

**Abstract.** We fit LZ's lone 248 keV event with an extended likelihood in observed recoil energy (WimPyDD O₁ spectra, efficiency, 11 keV resolution, P016's background model). For isoscalar O₁ the profile significance keeps rising past LZ's 350 keV grid edge to δ=380 keV at 1000 GeV (3.6σ), then collapses within 15 keV because the kinematic recoil window slides above the event, not because the rate vanishes. There (c₁ˢm_v²)²=2.2; a pure Higgsino crosses the 68% band at δ=365–370 keV.

**Question and approach.** Where in (m_χ, δ, σ_n) does the O₁ likelihood peak, with what 68/90% regions, and how does Z(δ) compare with Table S7? Model: dN/dE_obs=κ·E·[dR/dE_true×ε]⊗Gauss(σ_E), κ=(c₁m_v²)² (WimPyDD c⁰=2/m_v², P003), ε=0.96 with erf edges at 5.4/269.9 keV (σ 2.5/11.5 keV), σ_E=11√(E/248) keV (P009), annual-average Baxter halo (June and Sun-frame variants). Data: P016's digitised Fig. 5 NR-band bins (5.4–125 keV, ER scale profiled), the empty 125–200 keV bin, one event at 248 keV over b_H=5.7×10⁻⁴ flat in 200–270 keV. κ profiled at each (m, δ); Z=√q₀; intervals from Δ(−2lnL)=1, 2.71 (2-dof: 2.30, 4.61). m=400/1000/4000 GeV, δ from 100 keV to μv²_max/2.

**Results.** *Calibration.* Against Table S7 (34 entries): rms 0.40σ, mean −0.09σ; O₁ˢ runs 0.2–0.9σ low at δ≤250 keV, O₁ᵛ 0.2–0.6σ high (WimPyDD isovector-shape systematic, cf. P008/P016). Our 90% upper edges match Fig. 6 within 6% at δ=250–300 keV, at 350 keV only with the Sun-frame halo. *Shape.* With κ free, q₀=2[ln(f̃/b)−1+b/f̃] depends only on the accepted-spectrum density f̃ at 248 keV. At 1000 GeV, Z=2.67, 3.12, 3.42, **3.59**, 3.47, 3.16, 0 at δ=300, 350, 370, 380, 385, 390, 395 keV: 248 keV moves from the 99.2th observed percentile to the median (δ≈381 keV); beyond, the kinematic window slides past the event (E*=δμ/m_N=354 keV at the ceiling) and κ̂→0. Maxima: 380 keV (1000 GeV), 400 keV (4000 GeV), the ceiling 340 keV for 400 GeV (E*=261 keV). Δq₀=3.1 versus δ=350 keV (likelihood ratio 4.7), 5.7 versus 300 keV. 2-dof regions: 68% δ=360–385 keV, κ=0.013–35; 90% 330–390 keV. The peak shifts ≤5 keV under σ_E, E_obs=246 keV, edge and low-energy variants (Z_peak 3.56–3.61); Sun frame 370, E_obs=262→385 keV. *Coupling.* κ̂(380)=2.19 (68% 0.66–5.2), σ̂_n=6.5×10⁻³⁸ cm², μ̂=1.0 event. O₁ᵛ is flat (3.55→3.68); 400 and 4000 GeV differ ≤0.44σ below 300 keV. *Higgsino.* (c_eqm_v²)²=0.0777 lies inside the 68% band for δ=365–370 keV, κ̂=κ_H at 368 keV (Z 3.39); the fixed-coupling fit gives δ=370 keV (90% 360–380), δ(N=1)=365.5 keV (P007: 366). With P008's N_eff=9–15 the peak is 2.8–3.0σ global.

**Caveats.** One-dimensional observed energy replaces LZ's 2D PDFs; b_H is P016's anchor (×2 or ÷2 shifts Z by ∓0.2σ); asymptotic Z in the one-event regime (exact single-event check within 0.4σ); WimPyDD isovector systematic. The profile is scale-free, so the δ-preference reflects spectral shape only: a uniform-in-κ prior weights by N_unit (×10⁵ between 300 and 385 keV) and favours low δ, while a physical coupling (Higgsino) gives a genuine peak.

**Conclusion.** LZ's grid stops 30 keV short of the isoscalar O₁ maximum at δ≈380 keV (1000 GeV; 400 keV at 4000 GeV), where the local significance is 3.6σ and the required cross-section 10⁻³⁸–10⁻³⁷ cm²; only a physical cross-section prior pins δ near 365–370 keV.
