# P012: Magnetic-dipole dark matter at 1 TeV: the coupling from the LZ fit and the low-energy shoulder
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-04
- Primary arXiv category: hep-ph
- Author profile: dark-matter EFT phenomenologists
- Category (from your taxonomy): EFT
- Builds on / responds to: LZ paper (arXiv:2609.02823, Table I, Figs. 1 and 6, Table S6); P003 (L10 reduction, Fig. 1 digitisation), P005, P007 (digitisation method), P009

**Abstract.** LZ fits its 248 keV event with 1.0 (+1.4, −0.7) events of the magnetic tensor interaction L10^s at 1 TeV. Implementing L10 as the transverse-spin combination 4[(q²/m_N²)O4 − O6] in WimPyDD we find d10^s = 0.28 (0.15–0.43) at 1000 GeV and 0.19–0.52 for 200–4000 GeV, reproduce LZ's Fig. 6 interval up to a factor-2 amplitude convention, show that the low-energy shoulder gives only 0.10 events in the 2024 search, and exclude photon-mediated dipole dark matter.

**Question and approach.** What coupling does the LZ fit imply, what magnetic moment does it correspond to, and could the double-peaked L10 spectrum have been seen in LZ's 2024 3–80 phd search? We compute L10 spectra with WimPyDD (Baxter SHM; c⁰_WD = 2c⁰_Anand per P003; m_M = m_N), fold LZ's efficiency and solve N(d10) = 0.3, 1.0, 2.4 events in 2.84 t·yr for five masses; Fig. 6 (bottom) is digitised from the vector PDF. Photon-mediated dipole scattering is implemented in NREFT (c1, c4 constant; c5, c6 ∝ 1/q²; recalled/likely).

**Results.** *Normalisation.* LZ's unit-coupling Fig. 1 curve is 3.86× our WimPyDD rate at 200 and 1000 GeV (shape rms 0.084 dex), so LZ's d10 is 1.96× ours. The digitised Fig. 6 interval at 1000 GeV, d10 = 0.171–0.614, corresponds to 0.38–4.85 expected events (median sensitivity 2.23) in LZ's normalisation, matching a background-free single-count interval [0.105, 4.36] (expected 2.44) at the upper edge and median; in ours it would be 0.10–1.26 (median 0.58), impossible.

*Couplings (LZ normalisation, provisional pending DR-001).* N(d10 = 1) = 17.6, 28.7, 24.5, 12.9, 3.6 events for 100, 200, 400, 1000, 4000 GeV, so d10(N = 1) = 0.24, 0.19, 0.20, 0.28, 0.52 (0.15–0.43 at 1 TeV for 0.3–2.4 events), inside LZ's intervals 0.15–0.63, 0.11–0.43, 0.12–0.45, 0.17–0.61, 0.32–1.14 (figure `P012_d10_vs_mass`). d10 = 0.28 is a dimension-8 contact strength d10/(m_v²m_N²) = (21 GeV)⁻⁴; the photon-coupled moment reproducing L10's spin–spin amplitude at q = 0.25 GeV is 2.8 × 10⁻⁵ μ_N = 2.9 × 10⁻¹⁹ e·cm (1 μ_N = 1.05 × 10⁻¹⁴ e·cm; recalled/likely).

*Shoulder.* N_lo = 0.60, 0.28, 0.20, 0.17 per 200–270 keV event at 200–4000 GeV (6.1 at 100 GeV): the 2024 exposure (4.2 t·yr) holds 0.09–0.21 events at best fit and ≤ 1.1 at the 90% upper edge; a tolerance of 3 (5, 10) events bites only above 29 (48, 96) LZ ROI events at 1 TeV. Only at 100 GeV (3.9 events at the upper edge) do the searches compete.

*Photon-mediated dipole.* Its Z²/E_R term gives N_lo = 376 (1 TeV): one 200–270 keV event (μ_χ = 2.1 × 10⁻⁵ μ_N) implies 560 events in the 2024 ROI; ≤ 5 requires μ_χ ≤ 2 × 10⁻²⁰ e·cm. L10 is the same transverse-spin structure with 1/q² → 1/m_M²: contact, without the 1/E_R enhancement.

**Caveats.** The factor 3.86 between the recalled Anand reduction and LZ's convention is unresolved (DR-001); it rescales d10 and the moments by 2, not the shoulder or photon-dipole conclusions. The 2024 tolerance (3–5 events) and exposure are recalled; the 2024 efficiency is approximated. Fig. 6's lower edge corresponds to 0.38 events, above a pure count's 0.105.

**Conclusion.** LZ's event corresponds to d10^s ≈ 0.19–0.28 for 200–1000 GeV, a dimension-8 contact dipole–dipole interaction at Λ ≈ 21 GeV. Its low-energy shoulder was invisible to the 2024 search; a photon-coupled magnetic moment is excluded by two orders of magnitude.
