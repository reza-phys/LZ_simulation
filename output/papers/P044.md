# P044: Anapole and electric-dipole dark matter versus the LZ event: contact and photon-mediated versions
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-10
- Primary arXiv category: hep-ph
- Author profile: electromagnetic-moment dark-matter phenomenologists
- Category (from your taxonomy): EFT
- Builds on / responds to: LZ paper (arXiv:2609.02823, Table S6, LEE section); P003, P012 (competing), P016, P027, P031 (work directory)

**Abstract.** LZ's 3.4σ maximum is shared by L10 and L16. With WimPyDD we compute xenon spectra for anapole (O8+O9), photon-mediated electric-dipole (O11/q²) and contact analogues at 200–4000 GeV. Per 200–270 keV event the anapole leaves 27 companions in 5.4–55 keV, the photon EDM 18 000; only q-weighted pure-spin structures (O9, O11×q², O9×q²) are compatible. L16 cannot be the anapole. LZ's fitted event implies 𝒜 = 2.7 × 10⁻⁶ GeV⁻² and d_E = 3.3 × 10⁻²⁴ e·cm.

**Question and approach.** Which electromagnetic moment can give a *lone* 248 keV recoil, and what is L16? After the photon equation of motion the anapole L = 𝒜χ̄γ^μγ⁵χ∂^νF_{μν} is a contact interaction with c8^N = 2e𝒜Q_N, c9^N = e𝒜g_N (recalled/likely; reproduced exactly by the directdm coefficients tabulated in P031's work). The EDM L = (d_E/2)χ̄σ^{μν}iγ⁵χF_{μν} gives the long-range c11^N = 2ed_EQ_Nm_N/q² (matches directdm to 1 %). We add heavy-mediator versions (O11 constant, O11×q², A–V with F₂ = 0, anapole×q²), pure O9, O9×q², O14 and P012's L10, fold the P003 efficiency, and form N_lo = R(5.4–55)/R(200–270), P016's calibrated Z(N_lo), couplings for 1.0 LZ event, the 2024-null bound (4.2 t·yr, ≤ 3–5 events, recalled), and KL-based event counts to separate each shape from L10.

**Results.** N_lo per 200–270 keV event at 1 TeV (200 / 4000 GeV in brackets): photon anapole 26.9 (82 / 23), its O8 charge part alone 1264 and O9 spin part alone 2.2; photon EDM 18 100 (54 000 / 15 600); contact O11 118, O11×q² 2.19; pure O9 2.07, O14 2.35; O9×q² 0.024, anapole×q² 0.29; L10 0.20. Populated shapes peak at 1–46 keV, q⁴ shapes at 211–214 keV (L10: 199). The anapole's velocity- but not q-suppressed O8 term gives 98 % of the low-energy rate, O9 the high-energy rate. One fitted ROI event needs 𝒜 = 2.73 × 10⁻⁶ GeV⁻² = (605 GeV)⁻², 97 % of it below 55 keV; one *248 keV* event needs 1.6 × 10⁻⁵, 3.6× above the 2024-null bound. The photon EDM needs d_E = 3.3 × 10⁻²⁴ e·cm per ROI event, 5.3 × 10⁻²² per high-energy event, against a null bound of 5.6 × 10⁻²⁴ e·cm. Since 3.4σ requires N_lo ≲ 0.3 (P016 calibration: anapole 2.7σ, O9 3.1σ), L16 (Table S6: 2.8/3.3/3.4/3.4/3.2 at 100–4000 GeV) is neither the anapole nor pure O9 — which matches L9 and L11 = L14 (3.0–3.2) — but a companion-free q⁴-spin structure like O9×q² (identity uncertain); its 4 TeV drop escapes our hardness proxy. Separating from L10 needs 2–5 events for populated spectra, 30–50 for O9-type, 190–490 for q⁴ candidates.

**Caveats.** NR reductions are recalled (likely) but checked against directdm; the O8/O9 sign is tested. Anand's L16 form is not recalled; the identification is a pattern match to Table S6 through P016's one-dimensional Z(N_lo) calibration. The 2024 tolerance (3–5 events) is recalled; efficiency is the P003 model. The L10 d10 comparison inherits P012's factor-2 convention (DR-001).

**Conclusion.** Neither the anapole nor the photon-mediated EDM can produce a lone 248 keV recoil: both put tens to tens of thousands of scatters into LZ's low-energy window per high-energy event. The 3.4σ L16 row demands a q⁴-weighted pure-spin operator, spectrally indistinguishable from L10.
