# P020: What LZ's data since April 2024 should show: falsifiable predictions for the untouched exposure

**Abstract.** LZ has taken data at the same field since 1 April 2024: 884 calendar days by the paper's posting, ≈ 6.8 t·yr (2.4 × the first exposure). We convert the published L10 best fit, corpus inelastic spectra and the P001 Bayesian framework into pre-registered predictions: 2.4 events (68% rate range 0.7–5.7), P(0) = 9%, versus P(≥1) = 2.5% for the whole modelled high-S1c background. Two new events give 5.7σ combined; zero excludes the best-fit rate at 90% CL.

**Question and approach.** How many high-energy NR-band events should the unanalysed exposure contain under (a) the modelled background, (b) the L10 best fit with its uncertainty, (c) inelastic O1 at δ = 300/350/366/380 keV (1000 GeV) normalised to one first-run event, (d) the P001 corpus-prior mixture — and what would each observed count N mean? Exposure: calendar days × 220/371 × 4.71 t (range 0.50–0.75, 5.5 t scenario). Signals: Poisson plug-in and Poisson–Gamma (negative-binomial) predictives; WimPyDD seasonal rates with the Fig. S2 efficiency, 40 000-toy likelihood ratios for time clustering; P001's three-hypothesis posterior (π_DM = 0.01, π_U = 0.1, L_U = 0.1); Poisson local significance of the combined sample.

**Results.** *Exposure.* 6.76 t·yr (k = 2.38) to 2 Sep 2026 (5.7–8.5 for live fractions 0.50–0.75; 10.5 by end-2027). *Background.* 0.025 events in the whole S1c > 500 phd panel (P(≥1) = 2.5%). *L10.* Table I equals the n = 1 Poisson interval [0.30, 2.36]. Plug-in mean 2.38: P(N = 0,1,2,3,≥4) = 0.09/0.22/0.26/0.21/0.22; flat-prior Poisson–Gamma (mean 4.76): 0.09/0.12/0.13/0.12/0.54. *Inelastic.* June/Dec = 2.4, 26, 345, ∞ (zero October–March) for δ = 300/350/366/380 keV; the window holds three summers, so one first-run event implies 2.50/2.72/2.85/2.91 new events, 41/59/71/78% in May–July and 13/2.5/0.3/0.1% in Nov–Jan (flat: 31%, 21%). Time clustering is strong (LR > 10) with probability 0/0.39/0.74 for N = 2/3/5 at 366 keV, never at 300 keV; three summer events are a 7% flat-time coincidence. *Mixture.* P(0/1/2/3/≥4) = 0.72/0.19/0.03/0.01/0.05.

**Caveats.** Live fraction (±25%), fiducial mass and livetime uniformity are unknown; the 270 keV roll-off is read from Fig. S2 and controls the δ ≥ 366 keV normalisations by ×2–8; the elastic L10 is treated as time-flat (P006: LR ≈ 0.98); U's rate is P001's judgemental choice and drives the low P(DM) at N = 1; P006's stored δ ≥ 350 keV seasonal tables are flatter than our recomputation.

**Conclusion.** The untouched exposure is a look-elsewhere-free test. Best fit: 2.4 ± Poisson events, 9% chance of none; corpus prior: 72% chance of none. Two events would be 5.7σ combined (P(DM) = 0.36); three decide (>5σ alone, P(DM) = 0.86); a winter event excludes δ ≥ 366 keV.
