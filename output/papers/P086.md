# P086: BBN and CMB constraints on the mediator required for the LZ inelastic cross-section: dark photons, dark Higgs and light Z′ in the early Universe
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-16
- Primary arXiv category: astro-ph.CO (cross-list hep-ph)
- Author profile: early-Universe cosmology group
- Category (from your taxonomy): COMP
- Builds on / responds to: LZ arXiv:2609.02823; P011, P025, P026, P051, P054, P058, P074

**Abstract.** The dark-photon fits to the LZ event (P011: m_A′ = 0.3–10 GeV, ε = 10⁻⁷–10⁻⁴) place the mediator sector in the early Universe. We compute A′ lifetimes, thermalisation, freeze-in abundances and BBN/CMB limits, the relic abundance and lifetime of the dark Higgs, χ₁ self-interactions and N_eff. The A′ is harmless (τ ≲ 10⁻⁸ s, thermalised); the dark Higgs is a late-decaying relic, excluded for m_hD ≲ 0.7 m_A′ unless it mixes with the SM Higgs at θ ≳ 10⁻⁸.

**Question and approach.** Does the mediator itself, rather than χ₂ (P026), survive BBN, FIRAS, Planck and N_eff? We tabulate g*(T), g*s(T) from exact thermal integrals with a smoothed QCD transition (validated: g* = 3.371 at 10 keV, t(1 MeV) = 0.745 s, t(z = 1100) = 1.16×10¹³ s), compute A′ → ℓℓ, hadrons via a recalled R(s), the inverse-decay thermalisation line (Y_FI = Y_eq), freeze-in yields, and test late decays against recalled Kawasaki–Kohri–Moroi, FIRAS and Slatyer–Wu criteria. For the dark Higgs (m_h = √(2λ)v_D, m_A′ = q_h g_D v_D) we solve the Boltzmann equation including the forbidden h h → A′A′ regime, derive its A′-loop and 4-fermion widths, and evaluate σ_T/m of χ₁ with classical Yukawa formulae.

**Results.** *Dark photon.* On the δ = 300 keV band τ_A′ = 3.9×10⁻⁸–2×10⁻¹⁸ s (0.3–30 GeV); thermalisation needs only ε ≳ 10⁻⁸, ≥ 570 below the band; τ_A′ = 1 s needs ε ≈ 10⁻¹¹; BBN/FIRAS exclude freeze-in dark photons only at m_A′ ≤ 0.23 GeV, ε ≤ 2.5×10⁻¹¹. ΔN_eff = 0 (all species non-relativistic at ν decoupling). *Dark Higgs.* A charge-2 Higgs at α_D = 0.1 has r = m_h/m_A′ = 0.35–0.89 for λ = 0.3–2. Its stable relic would be 1.8×10³, 2.4, 1.9×10⁻⁴, 6×10⁻⁷ ρ_DM for r = 0.5, 0.7, 0.9, 1.2; without a Higgs portal τ_h = 3.5×10⁶ s (r = 0.9, ε = 10⁻⁶) ∝ ε⁻⁴. Thus r = 0.5 (0.7) is excluded for ε ≲ 10⁻⁴ (10⁻⁵), i.e. m_A′ = 2.6–9.6 (2.6–4.4) GeV at δ = 300 keV; r = 0.9 excludes ε ≤ 8×10⁻⁷; r ≥ 1.2 nothing. A portal θ ≥ 10⁻⁸ cures it. σ_T/m(χ₁) ≤ 5.5×10⁻⁴ cm²/g, 10³ below the Bullet cluster; the Z′ (τ = 10⁻²³ s) faces only P054's freeze-out. *Map (Fig. 4).* With the χ₂ floor (2.56 GeV), Planck (9.2 GeV, P025) and BaBar (34.8 GeV), the δ = 300 keV band survives at m_A′ = 9.2–34.8 GeV, ε = 7×10⁻⁵–10⁻³; mediator cosmology removes none of it (only 9.2–15.6 GeV at 250 keV, r = 0.5).

**Caveats.** R(s), the BBN envelope (×10), the loop function and the 4-body width (×3–10) are recalled or estimated; ε(τ_h = 1 s) ∝ Γ^{−1/4} is robust to them. The forbidden-yield normalisation (×3) shifts the critical r by ≲ 0.03. The Planck floor is P025's (thermal α_D); accelerator ceilings are recalled. Cannibal 3 → 2 and ε² depletion channels were shown negligible, not tracked.

**Conclusion.** The dark photon of the LZ fit is cosmologically inert, but its dark Higgs is not: unless m_hD ≳ 0.9 m_A′ (λ ≳ 2) or a tiny Higgs-portal mixing exists, BBN, FIRAS and Planck remove the low-m_A′ half of P011's band; the Planck-allowed window above 9 GeV survives.
