# P002: Kinematic map of inelastic dark matter consistent with a 248 keV xenon recoil
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-03
- Primary arXiv category: hep-ph
- Author profile: dark-matter phenomenology group
- Category (from your taxonomy): IDM
- Builds on / responds to: LZ arXiv:2609.02823 (Fig. 1, Tables S6–S7, LEE grid); dossier P000; normalisation deferred to P003

**Abstract.** We map the (m_χ, δ) plane in which a 248 ± 32 keV xenon recoil on 16 June 2023 is kinematically possible (Baxter-2021 halo) and compute O₁ inelastic spectra with WimPyDD. Every LZ grid point except (400 GeV, 350 keV) is allowed; the elastic floor is 73 GeV. But the O₁ spectrum has a form-factor node at 266 keV: 248 keV lies above the 98th in-ROI percentile for all δ ≤ 350 keV and becomes typical only within 15 keV of δ_max, at 10³–10⁴ lower rate.

**Question and approach.** For which (m_χ, δ) can a halo particle produce E_R = 248 ± 32 keV (stat ⊕ sys) on 16 June (v_E = 265, v_esc = 544, v_max = 809 km/s), and where does the inelastic spectrum peak? We use δ_max = v_max√(2m_N E_R) − m_N E_R/μ (ceiling μv_max²/2), with annual-average, December and v_esc = 500/600 km/s variations. Spectra: WimPyDD O₁ isoscalar, unit coupling, day-167 halo, m_χ = 400/1000/4000 GeV, δ = 0–400 keV, on an explicit v_min grid to 830 km/s (WimPyDD's default grid drops the June tail). In-ROI rates use a piecewise-linear efficiency (96 % plateau, 50 % at 5.4 and 269.9 keV).

**Results.** *Kinematics.* δ_max(248 keV) = 341 / 387 / 409 keV at 400 / 1000 / 4000 GeV (June; 317 / 362 / 385 in December; ±1σ energy shifts ≤ 6 keV). Minimum mass: 72.8 GeV elastic, 259 GeV at δ = 300, 453 GeV at δ = 350, 821 GeV at δ = 380 keV. Any m_χ ≤ 50 GeV WIMP gives E_R ≤ 150 keV, explaining the zeros of Table S6; the (400 GeV, 350 keV) dash follows from μv²/2 = 341 keV. *Shape.* We reproduce Fig. 1's shape (nodes at 102 and 266 keV, peak 166 keV at δ = 300; absolute level ×3–5 lower, pending P003). At 1000 GeV the in-ROI peak is at 157 / 166 / 195 / 226 keV for δ = 250 / 300 / 350 / 380 keV; the central 68 % interval (145–202 keV at δ = 300) contains 248 keV only at δ = 390 keV (400 keV at 4000 GeV; never at 400 GeV). The 200–270 vs 5.4–55 keV rate ratio rises from 4 × 10⁻⁴ (δ = 0) to 0.1 (200) and ∞ (δ ≥ 275 keV)—this, not the peak position, drives Table S7's rise. *Rate.* R(350)/R(300) = 0.032 (1000 GeV), 0.078 (4000), 10⁻⁷ (400); R(380)/R(300) = 1.3 × 10⁻⁴ (1000 GeV); 1 % of R(300) at δ = 331 / 359 / 373 keV; June/December = 2.4 (δ = 300), 28 (350). *Degeneracy.* KS(400 vs 4000 GeV) = 0.01–0.07 for δ ≤ 300 keV but 0.98 at 350 keV.

**Caveats.** True recoil energy only; the ±23 keV resolution partly fills the 266 keV node in observed space. O₁ only; O₄ lacks the M-response node. Absolute rates await P003's coupling convention. Simplified efficiency; cosine v_E model (WimPyDD's differs by 1.4 km/s).

**Conclusion.** Kinematics admit inelastic DM with δ ≤ 341–409 keV and m_χ ≥ 73 GeV, but O₁ spectra do not peak at 248 keV unless δ is within 15 keV of δ_max, at 10³–10⁴ lower rate. LZ's grid stops 37–59 keV short of the edge; an extended-δ scan with resolution is warranted.
