# P076: Solar capture of inelastic dark matter with δ ≈ 300 keV and the neutrino-telescope constraints on the LZ-fitted couplings
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-15
- Primary arXiv category: astro-ph.HE (cross-list hep-ph)
- Author profile: astroparticle / neutrino-telescope phenomenology group
- Category (from your taxonomy): COMP
- Builds on / responds to: LZ arXiv:2609.02823; P039, P007, P011, P054, P021, P025, P026

**Abstract.** Inside the Sun a TeV particle moves at w = 620–1370 km/s, so iron-group nuclei up-scatter inelastic DM with δ ≤ 560 keV. With the AGSS09ph profile and 20 isotopes we compute the capture rate for the LZ-fitted Higgsino, dark-photon and Z′ couplings, bracket the annihilation rate between stalled and thermalised populations, and convert recalled IceCube/ANTARES limits. The Higgsino is excluded at every δ by ≥ 15–770×; the dark photon and Z′ survive only for δ ≲ 300–340 keV.

**Question and approach.** Can the Sun capture 1 TeV DM with δ = 250–380 keV at the couplings that give one LZ event, and does its annihilation exceed solar-neutrino limits? We evaluate Gould's capture integral per stream u and shell r with w² = u² + v_esc(r)², the inelastic window E_± = (μ²w²/m_A)[1 − δ/μw² ± √(1 − 2δ/μw²)], the condition E_R + δ ≥ m_χu²/2 and Helm form factors, validated against WimPyC to 2–3 %. We add the exothermic χ₂ down-scatter before exit, the χ₂ decay/down-scatter fate, Griest–Seckel equilibrium versus P039's orbit-confined bracket, and convert σ_SD/σ_SI limits through our own elastic capture rates with channel factors.

**Results.** δ_max at the centre (u = 288 km/s): H 10, He 41, O 161, Ne 200, Mg 239, Si 278, S 317, Ar/Ca 393, Fe 542, Ni 561 keV; only Fe (83–94 %), Ni (5 %) and, below 300 keV, Ca/Ar/S/Si capture, inside r ≤ 0.44–0.23 R_⊙. Capture rates (s⁻¹, ρ_χ = 0.4):

| δ [keV] | Higgsino | dark photon | Z′ | C/C_el |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 250 | 8.1e23 | 1.2e20 | 1.3e20 | 0.16–0.20 |
| 300 | 4.4e23 | 4.5e20 | 5.3e20 | 0.08–0.11 |
| 350 | 2.0e23 | 7.1e21 | 1.2e22 | 0.04–0.05 |
| 366 | 1.5e23 | 4.7e22 | 1.1e23 | 0.03 |

P039's Fe+Ni numbers are confirmed to 9–14 %. The two-step channel adds < 0.6 %. χ₂ decays in flight (Higgsino, 60 km) or down-scatters within 2.5×10⁵ s; the released δ cannot unbind a particle in a 4–10 MeV well. Thermalised populations equilibrate (Γ_A = C/2); stalled ones (r_a = 0.36–0.75 R_⊙) give ×1.3–2 (Higgsino) to ×10³ (dark photon/Z′) less. Conversion: C_SD(H, 10⁻⁴⁰) = C_SI(10⁻⁴³) = 2.54×10²⁰ s⁻¹, so recalled σ_SD ≈ 3×10⁻⁴¹ [10⁻⁴¹–10⁻⁴⁰] gives Γ_lim(WW) = 3.8×10¹⁹ [1.3–13×10¹⁹]. The A′ (ε = 8×10⁻⁷–10⁻³) decays within ≤ 30 m, so only its 15 % τ⁺τ⁻ yields hard neutrinos: Γ_lim = 5×10²⁰; B−L Z′ (23 % νν̄, 15 % ττ): 9×10¹⁹; U(1)_B (quarks): 2.5×10²¹. Higgsino Γ_A = (0.5–7.4)×10²² (366 keV), (1–2)×10²³ (300 keV): 120–1900× the central limit, ≥ 15–770× the weakest bracket even when stalled. Dark photon: unconstrained ≤ 280, marginal 290–340, excluded if thermalised 350–365, excluded ≥ 366 keV. Z′: B−L marginal ≤ 300, excluded ≥ 360 keV; U(1)_B unconstrained ≤ 320, excluded ≥ 370 keV (Fig. 1).

**Caveats.** Neutrino-telescope limits and channel factors are recalled (×3 each way); Helm versus shell-model changes the isovector Higgsino by 10 %; the thermalisation question is bracketed explicitly and dominates the dark-photon/Z′ verdicts; Sommerfeld factors follow P025/P054; off-centre annihilation (ignored) would strengthen limits.

**Conclusion.** The LZ-fitting Higgsino, at any δ, is excluded by solar-neutrino searches independently of thermalisation; the dark photon and Z′ survive only at δ ≲ 300–340 keV, so P021's 360–385 keV preference is closed for all three models.
