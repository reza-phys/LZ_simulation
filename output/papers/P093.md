# P093: Event-topology odds: single nuclear recoil versus wall MSSI versus charge-poor electronic recoil versus accidental versus neutron, combining every published handle

**Abstract.** We assemble every topological handle the corpus has quantified for LZ's 248 keV candidate — position, band distance, pulse shape, veto, timing, energy — into one likelihood table L(handle | H) for eight origins and compute the classification posterior. Per unit signal expectation a single nuclear recoil beats wall MSSI by 10^4.4, RFR MSSI 10^6.9, accidentals 10^4.1, neutrons 10^4.6, neutrinos 10^3.8, a charge-poor ER 10^3.6 and an instrumental artefact only 10^2.5.

**Question and approach.** For one point of a marked Poisson process, P(H | D) = μ_H p_H(D)/Σ μ_H′ p_H′(D). We factorise μ_H p_H into rate (WS-ROI expectation), E (S1c > 500 phd), B (|d| ≤ 2σ_NR), P (position-density ratio, P070), W (LZ's stated pulse checks), V (1 − veto efficiency) and T (date). Cases: (i-a) LZ's Table I/Fig. 5 counts with (S1c, d) only; (i-b) plus topological handles; (ii) corpus-revised counts including the unmodelled charge-poor-ER (7 × 10⁻⁷–2.8 × 10⁻³; P010/P056) and artefact (P041 residual 0.006 × π_U) classes. Position and band are joint for MSSI via P033's class-conditional maps. Ranges propagate through 40 000 log-uniform Monte Carlo draws; the DM normalisation s is scanned.

**Results.** LZ's model puts 3.5 × 10⁻⁴ background events in the S1c > 500 phd, ±2σ_NR neighbourhood (wall MSSI 48 %, accidentals 42 %, ν 9 %) against 0.19 s signal. Handles (i-b) remove MSSI (wall ×15, RFR ×10²–10³), leaving ν (55 %) and accidentals (26 %). In case (ii) the background mass sits in unmodelled classes: artefact 85 %, ER 6 %, ν 5 %.

Most discriminating handle: NR–WALL none (rate, veto, position each ×15–20); NR–RFR pulse shape (+2.0 dex); NR–ER energy × band (+6.9); otherwise energy (+2.9–3.5); band and timing ≤ 0.6 dex.

Unpublished handles shift log₁₀ odds NR : all (NR-like/background-like): S1 pulse shape +0.46/−0.44, artefact audit +0.19/−0.21, others ≤ 0.08; together +0.49/−0.63.

**Caveats.** The two leading backgrounds have rates that are judgements spanning three decades (ER tail shape; artefact prior), and they set the bands. W entries for wall MSSI (0.3–1), RFR (10⁻³–0.1) and accidentals (0.01–1) translate LZ's words into numbers. P(NR) is a statement about s, not dark matter: atmospheric neutrinos are topologically identical.

**Conclusion.** Topology establishes "single nuclear recoil, not MSSI/accidental/neutron" at ≥ 10^3.6 per unit signal; the only margins below 10³ are against unmodelled ER-like classes, testable only with waveform-level quantities (DR-003). Whether the recoil is dark matter is a question of s and the neutrino floor.
