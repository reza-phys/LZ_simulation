# P019: Atmospheric-neutrino processes at 248 keV: the CEνNS tail, loss of coherence, and exotic neutrino interactions
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-05
- Primary arXiv category: hep-ph (cross-list astro-ph.HE)
- Author profile: neutrino phenomenologists
- Category (from your taxonomy): EXO
- Builds on / responds to: LZ paper (arXiv:2609.02823) Table I, Fig. 5, Discussion; dossier §5 (hypotheses A, H); P004 (Fig. 5 calibration), P009 (efficiency, S1 scale), P003 (N_lo criterion)

**Abstract.** We quantify what LZ means by "not consistent with scattering coherence". Normalised to LZ's 0.11 atmospheric-ν CEνNS events, the Helm-based expectation is 4–12 × 10⁻⁵ events above 200 keV and 1–3 × 10⁻⁵ in 225–271 keV, matching LZ's own Fig. 5 curve (3.4 × 10⁻⁵ at S1c > 500 phd). At q = 246 MeV the coherent weight Q_W²F² = 0.9 is below one nucleon's. Incoherent knock-out and every exotic ν interaction either leave MeV signatures or require ≥ 790 low-energy events per 248 keV recoil.

**Question and approach.** What is the atmospheric-neutrino rate at E_R ≥ 200 keV in 2.84 t·yr, what happens as coherence is lost, and can a non-standard interaction make a lone 248 keV NR? We use dσ/dE_R = (G_F²m_N/4π)Q_W²(1 − m_NE_R/2E_ν²)F² with Helm and WimPyDD shell-model weak form factors, eight bracketing flux shapes (E·dΦ/dE flat 10–100 MeV then E^−2…−3; Honda-like) normalised to Table I's 0.11 events with P009's efficiency, digitise Fig. 5's green histogram (validated on the blue total: 0.01082 vs 0.0106), compute Llewellyn-Smith NC quasi-elastic rates with Pauli blocking, NC excitation of ¹²⁹Xe/¹³¹Xe, and recoil spectra for magnetic-moment, Z′, scalar and dipole-portal HNL interactions (cross-section derived; m₄ → 0 reproduces Vogel–Engel).

**Results.** A 248 keV recoil needs E_ν ≥ 123 MeV. The raw fraction of CEνNS above 200 keV is 2.5–7.8 × 10⁻⁴ (Helm), giving N(>200) = 4.3 × 10⁻⁵–1.2 × 10⁻⁴ and N(225–271) = 1.1–3.2 × 10⁻⁵; LZ's Fig. 5 green curve sums to 8.2 × 10⁻⁴ (250 < S1c < 500) and 3.4 × 10⁻⁵ (S1c > 500), reproduced by Helm with Honda-like flux (ratios 0.94, 1.6). N_lo (5.4–200 per 200–270 keV) = 880–2460. Helm F²(248 keV) = 1.6 × 10⁻⁴ for natural Xe (per-isotope nodes 262–307 keV; mixture minimum 278 keV); the shell-model neutron density gives 2.6 × 10⁻⁶ with the node at 246 keV and a tail 15× smaller. Incoherent NC scattering per nucleus exceeds coherent for E_ν ≳ 250 MeV (ratio 0.15 at 100 MeV, 0.66 at 200 MeV); knock-outs total 0.064 events, but the proton channel deposits ≥ MeV and the neutron channel is multi-site or vetoed. The one lone-NR path—escaping untagged neutron, ground-state daughter recoiling with the Fermi momentum (E_max = 279 keV)—gives 1.7 × 10⁻⁴ (8 × 10⁻⁶–5 × 10⁻⁴) events in 225–271 keV, exceeding the coherent tail yet ≤ 0.05% probable; it is absent from LZ's model. NC excitation (3.8 × 10⁻⁵, 3.0 × 10⁻⁵ events) lands at log₁₀S2c ≈ 4.5–4.8, above the ROI. Exotic N_lo: SM/heavy mediator 1631; magnetic moment 20 100; Z′ (10–1000 MeV) 1790–5 × 10⁵; scalar (heavy) 786; dipole HNL m₄ = 0.3–245 MeV 20 100–1280 (threshold mass for 248 keV is 246 MeV). Solar: hep end point 5.79 keV.

**Caveats.** Flux shape above 100 MeV is recalled (bracketed ×3); the Fig. 5 green curve includes an unknown fitted neutron component (≈ 0.009 events, mostly low energy). Pauli factor, k_F = 260 MeV/c, neutron escape (0.1–0.35), untagged (0.08–0.3) and ground-state (0.1–0.5) fractions are order-of-magnitude; the Fermi-sphere fraction overestimates ground-state high-momentum removals. B(σ) for excitation is uncertain ×3.

**Conclusion.** Atmospheric neutrinos, standard or exotic, do not explain the event: coherent tail ≤ 3 × 10⁻⁵, incoherent lone-NR ≤ 5 × 10⁻⁴, and every hardened spectrum predicts ≥ 790 unseen low-energy recoils. Future high-energy searches should add the quasi-elastic Fermi-recoil channel.
