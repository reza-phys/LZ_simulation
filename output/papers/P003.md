# P003: Which NREFT operators can produce a lone high-energy recoil? Spectral shape ratios in xenon and a reproduction of the LZ signal spectra
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-03
- Primary arXiv category: hep-ph
- Author profile: effective-field-theory phenomenologists with direct-detection expertise
- Category (from your taxonomy): EFT
- Builds on / responds to: LZ paper (arXiv:2609.02823); P000 dossier

**Abstract.** A 248 keV recoil without low-energy companions constrains the momentum structure of the interaction more than its strength. With WimPyDD (LZ's code) we compute, for the NREFT operators O1, O3–O15 (spin-1/2 WIMP, isoscalar and isovector, 200–4000 GeV), the number N_lo of 5.4–55 keV events per event in 200–270 keV. Only q²-suppressed spin operators (O6, O9, O10, O14, O15, O5^v) give N_lo ≲ 5. We also fix WimPyDD's normalisation against LZ's Fig. 1 and reproduce the double-peaked L10 spectrum.

**Question and approach.** LZ's 2024 search (same 220 live days, 5.4–55 keV) saw no excess, so any elastic explanation must place its rate at high energy. For each operator we compute dR/dE (WimPyDD 2.0.4, Baxter-2021 SHM, natural Xe), weight it with an efficiency model (0.96 plateau, erf roll-offs to 50 % at 5.4 and 269.9 keV) and form N_lo = R_lo(5.4–55 keV)/R_hi(200–270 keV), which is coupling-independent. Recalling (uncertain) that the 2024 limit tolerated ~3–5 signal events, we classify against N_max = 3, 5, 10. Fig. 1 is digitised from the vector PDF with PyMuPDF.

**Results.** *Normalisation.* LZ's "unit coupling c_i^s = 1/m_v²" (Anand convention, c⁰ = (c_p+c_n)/2) is reproduced by WimPyDD only with c⁰ = 2/m_v²: WimPyDD/Fig. 1 = 1.012, 1.011, 1.028 at 10, 20, 50 keV (0.25 with c⁰ = 1/m_v²). WimPyDD's c⁰ is c_p + c_n; LZ unit-coupling rates are 4× WimPyDD's at c = 1/m_v². Above 110 keV WimPyDD is 9–12 % low.

*Operator scan (1000 GeV; 4000 GeV within 15 %, 200 GeV ≈ 3× larger):*

| N_lo | operators |
|---|---|
| < 1 | O6^{s,v} 0.43/0.45, O15^v 0.75, O5^v 1.05 |
| 1–5 | O15^s 1.9, O9 2.1/2.2, O14 2.3/2.5, O10 3.1/3.2, O13^s 4.9, O3^v 5.0 |
| 5–10 | O8^v 9.2, O13^v 9.5 |
| > 10 | O3^s 12.6, O5^s 14.2, O4 28, O7 32–36, O11^v 34, O12 41–100, O8^s 156, O11^s 258, O1^v 477, O1^s 2752 |

Each q² in the amplitude divides N_lo by ≈10; velocity suppression alone (O7, O8^s) does not help since v⊥² peaks at small q. At N_max = 3 the survivors are O5^v, O6, O9, O14, O15; at N_max = 10 O3^v, O8^v, O13^v join. Efficiency variants move N_lo by ≤ 9 %. Inelastic O1^s: N_lo = 501, 7.6, 0 for δ = 100, 200, 300 keV.

*L10.* The magnetic tensor Lagrangian reduces to ∝ (q²/m_N²)O4 − O6, a pure transverse-spin q⁴Σ′ response, which reproduces the digitised L10^s double peak (dip 52 vs 61 keV, high peak 196 vs 205 keV) to 0.086 dex rms, with N_lo = 0.20 (0.155 from the digitised curve).

**Caveats.** N_max is recalled, not computed. Our proxy is stricter than LZ's PLR: O4/L15 (N_lo ≈ 28) still get 2.7σ in Tables S6/S7, so the LZ fit evidently tolerates tens of low-energy signal events; a STAT paper should extract the effective N_max. The 2024 efficiency shape is approximated; only leading-order Lagrangian mappings were used.

**Conclusion.** A lone 248 keV recoil selects q²-suppressed spin couplings (O6, O9, O10, O14, O15, O5^v, L10-like transverse-spin combinations) and excludes O1, O4, O7, O11, O12 by 1–3 orders of magnitude in N_lo. LZ's unit-coupling spectra require WimPyDD c⁰ = 2/m_v².

**References.** LZ Collaboration, arXiv:2609.02823; Fitzpatrick et al., JCAP 02 (2013) 004; Anand, Fitzpatrick, Haxton, PRC 89 (2014) 065501; Jeong, Kang, Scopel, Tomar, WimPyDD, CPC (2022); Baxter et al., EPJC 81 (2021) 907; LZ Collaboration, PRD 109 (2024) 092003; P000 (dossier).
