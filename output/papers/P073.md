# P073: What the vetoes know: the MSSI tagging efficiency, its wall/RFR split, and the information in the event's silence in Skin and OD

**Abstract.** LZ's 248 keV candidate has no Skin or OD pulse in either veto window. From the paper's own counts we extract the prompt tagging efficiency by MSSI class (wall 0.95, escaping-γ RFR 0.999, contained-γ RFR 0.20); the data test the wall value only to ε > 0.32. A photon-transport MC for LZ's 12 + 77 keV wall decomposition gives ε = 97.7 % (96.0–99.5 %), so the silent vetoes penalise wall MSSI by a further 0.46 (0.10–0.79).

**Question and approach.** (a) Which tagging efficiencies do the MSSI comparison table and Tables I/S1 imply per wall/RFR class, and how well do 0–21 observed events check them? (b) For the event's topology (12 keV Compton at r = 45.9 cm, 77 keV in the ≤ 3 mm dead shell, a ≥ 0.5 MeV γ leaving through PTFE, Skin, Ti and GdLS), what is P(no Skin ≥ 2.5 phd, no OD ≥ 4.5 phd)? A Klein–Nishina ray-march MC in a cylindrical LZ model, both scatter orderings, strict S1 budget (further TPC-visible deposits excluded). (c) The veto-silence likelihood ratio with P004/P033. (d) The delayed-sample count.

**Results.** MSSI 0.077/(0.077 + 0.0049) = 0.940 = λ_MSSI and detector ERs 62.2/70.7 = 0.880 = λ_PG: the prompt expectations derive from the λ's. The direct simulation counts give 0.19 prompt MSSI in the 4.7 t WS ROI (wall 0.949, RFR 0.999), 2.5× Table S1. Wall sidebands (0 science, 2 prompt vs 0.63/2.60) bound ε_wall > 0.32 (90 %); the only real test is the ²¹⁴Pb HE-SB bin, 3/21 = 0.14 (0.07–0.26) vs 0.195.

Topology MC (Fig. 2): after the 77 keV kick (6–29°) the γ re-enters the TPC in 9–16 % of histories; otherwise the Skin tags 73–93 %, the OD 6–24 % more:

Wall-first ordering gives 1.0 ± 0.6 % (a rare sub-class exiting through the cathode). Skin 4/8 cm → 3.5/1.4 %; OD threshold 20–200 keV → 2.1–3.8 %; range 0.5–4.0 %. The RFR decomposition (12 + 204 keV) is 99 % tagged once extra RFR light is forbidden (27–49 % if allowed: the HE-SB regime).

Likelihood ratios P(science | H)/P(science | DM): wall MSSI 0.060 (λ = 0.94) → 0.023 (0.005–0.040) for the event topology; RFR topology 0.008; detector-ER or neutron single scatters 0.12. Extra factor 0.46 (0.10–0.79): P004's k_required 619 → 1350 (530–9000), P033's 6.4 × 10⁴ → 1.4 × 10⁵.

Delayed sample: 55 vs 50.4 ± 2.0 is P = 0.28 (0.6σ) with Poisson fluctuations; 1710 × 0.0286/0.971 = 50.35 reproduces the fit from random coincidences; P042's χ₂ photons would need 6.6–490 clean events.

**Caveats.** Skin/PTFE/OD thicknesses, PMT-array density and veto light yields are recalled (Skin thickness and OD threshold dominate, ×1.7 each); weights are non-uniform (n_eff/N ≈ 0.1; MC errors quoted); γ sources enter only through survival weights; the ×2.5 prompt-count discrepancy is unresolved.

**Conclusion.** The vetoes are 97–98 % efficient for the γ that LZ's wall-MSSI reading requires, not 94–95 %: the silence multiplies the wall-MSSI odds by a further 0.46 (0.10–0.79). The delayed excess is 0.6σ. A ±1 μs sub-threshold Skin/OD search would carry LR ≈ 50–200 if a pulse appeared.
