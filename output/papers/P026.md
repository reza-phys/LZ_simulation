# P026: Cosmological constraints on the excited state: BBN, CMB and the χ₂ → χ₁ transition for δ ≈ 300 keV
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-08
- Primary arXiv category: astro-ph.CO (cross-list hep-ph)
- Author profile: early-Universe cosmologists

- Builds on / responds to: LZ arXiv:2609.02823; P007 (Higgsino), P011 (dark photon), P014 (χ₂ widths), P002

**Abstract.** Inelastic-DM readings of the LZ event leave half the dark matter in the excited state χ₂ after freeze-out. Solving the Boltzmann equation for the χ₂ ↔ χ₁ bath transitions, the Higgsino decouples at T_* = 0.85 MeV with f₂ = 0.42, the LZ-fitted dark photon at 5–27 MeV with f₂ ≈ 0.5. The stored 10⁻⁷ of ρ_DM is invisible to BBN and FIRAS; Planck and SPI exclude only a photon channel with 2×10¹² s < τ_γ < 10²² s.

**Question and approach.** For pseudo-Dirac DM with δ = 200–400 keV and m = 0.3–4 TeV: what fraction stays excited after freeze-out, how fast does χ₂ decay or down-scatter, and is the release of f₂δ per particle constrained by BBN, CMB spectral distortions, CMB anisotropies, the 300 keV sky or the exothermic requirement (P011)? We derive σ(χ₂f → χ₁f) = (c_V² + c_A²)(E+δ)²/2π (reproducing P007's σ_n and the CEνNS total), impose detailed balance Γ_up = Γ_down e^{−δ/T}, and integrate df₂/dt with exact e± thermodynamics for the Higgsino (Z, G_F-fixed) and for the dark photon with P011's σ_p(N=1). Lifetimes follow P014/P011; injection is mapped onto z(τ) with Planck cosmology.

**Results.** *Freeze-out.* The Higgsino's transitions have neutrino-like G_F²E² cross-sections and decouple with the neutrinos: T_* = 0.85 MeV for all δ, so f₂ = 0.446, 0.420, 0.403, 0.394 at δ = 200, 300, 366, 400 keV. The dark photon's LZ coupling is 10⁻⁴–10⁻² G_F² at δ ≤ 350 keV: T_* = 27, 15, 4.7, 2.4, 0.8 MeV and f₂ = 0.50, 0.50, 0.48, 0.46, 0.40 at δ = 250–380 keV. *Decays.* Higgsino τ_νν̄ = 1.2×10⁶ s (300 keV), 4.6×10⁵ s (366); τ_γ ~ 0.1 s (0.01–10 s): f₂(today) = 0. Dark photon: τ_νν̄ = 2.6×10¹⁸ s (m_A′/GeV)⁻⁴ at 300 keV, no photon channel. *Injection.* f₂δ/m = 1.3×10⁻⁷; Δρ/ρ_γ = 6×10⁻⁴/(1+z): photons lie below all photodissociation thresholds, ΔN_eff = 6×10⁻¹⁰; μ ≤ 2×10⁻⁸, y ≤ 1.5×10⁻⁷ versus FIRAS 9×10⁻⁵, 1.5×10⁻⁵. Per baryon, however, the budget is 633 eV, so a *photon* channel decaying after recombination is excluded by Planck (> 0.2 eV/baryon deposited) for τ_γ ∈ [2×10¹², 10¹⁹] s at f₂ = 0.42, down to f₂ = 3×10⁻⁴; SPI (NFW D₁₀° = 7.4×10²¹ GeV cm⁻² sr) adds f₂/τ_γ < 5×10⁻²³ s⁻¹, i.e. τ_γ > 10²² s (f₂ = 0.5). *Today.* P011's exothermic bound f₂ < 1.2×10⁻³ requires τ < 7×10¹⁶ s, hence m_A′ ≥ 2.45, 0.92, 0.55, 0.25 GeV at δ = 300, 350, 365, 380 keV (Fig. 1).

**Caveats.** MB statistics and massless-fermion cross-sections (3 % in rates); hadronic channels ignored; the Planck criterion is a one-number re-expression of Slatyer–Wu (factor ~3) with a crude f_eff; SPI sensitivity, EGB level and the Higgsino dipole moment are recalled/uncertain; the dark-photon width assumes hypercharge mixing (P011).

**Conclusion.** The excited state survives freeze-out with f₂ ≈ 0.4–0.5 in both LZ models yet is cosmologically inert: BBN and spectral distortions cannot see 10⁻⁷ of ρ_DM. Only a photon channel with 10¹²–10²² s lifetimes is excluded; the Higgsino decays in seconds, the dark photon invisibly.
