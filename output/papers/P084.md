# P084: Sommerfeld-enhanced annihilation and the CMB, dwarf-spheroidal and antiproton constraints on the electroweak multiplets that fit the LZ event
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-16
- Primary arXiv category: hep-ph (cross-list astro-ph.HE)
- Author profile: indirect-detection phenomenology group
- Category (from your taxonomy): COMP (MODEL content: electroweak multiplets)
- Builds on / responds to: LZ arXiv:2609.02823; P037, P007, P054; competes with P025

**Abstract.** We compute tree-level WW, ZZ, Zγ, γγ annihilation and the coupled-channel Sommerfeld enhancement for the (n, Y) = (2, ½), (3, 1), (4, 3/2), (5, 2) fermion multiplets that fit the LZ event at δ = 366–383 keV. The doublet has S = 1.7 at 1 TeV and survives all recalled limits; the Y ≥ 1 multiplets have ZZ-dominated rates ∝ Y⁴, S = 5–140, and are excluded by Fermi dwarfs, H.E.S.S. and Planck by ×5–8000, even as thermal sub-components.

**Question and approach.** Are P037's LZ-fitting multiplets excluded once Sommerfeld enhancement is computed properly? Channel amplitudes follow from the gauge vertices with the positronium-normalised threshold formula σv = |Σa|²/32πm² (reproducing the wino and P025's Higgsino rates). We solve the n-channel Schrödinger equation for ψ^(Q)ψ̄^(Q), Q = 0…n−1, with W-exchange off-diagonal terms −(α₂/2)C₊²e^{−m_W r}/r, diagonal Z-Yukawa and Coulomb terms and thresholds 2Δm_Q (recalled CFS formula, re-derived), numerically (not Hulthén), validated against Coulomb (0.5 %), Hulthén (3–11 %), the wino resonance (2.37 vs 2.3–2.5 TeV) and a Majorana-basis δ = 366 keV run (≤ 1.6 %).

**Results.** Tree level, 1 TeV: ⟨σv⟩(WW+ZZ) = 9.6×10⁻²⁷, 9.1×10⁻²⁶, 4.0×10⁻²⁵, 1.2×10⁻²⁴ cm³/s; γγ and Zγ arise only from charged Sommerfeld components. The neutral state is uncharged and charged pairs close below v_thr ≈ 0.05–0.09, so S is flat for v = 10⁻⁶–10⁻²: S_MW = S_dwarf = S_CMB. Resonances: doublet 7.0 TeV only; (3,1) 2.5, 5.7; (4,3/2) 1.3, 2.4, 4.7; (5,2) 0.75, 1.3, 2.1, 3.1, 5.0 TeV. Full-density ratios to anchor limits (Table):

Indirect ratios scale as f², the LZ rate as f (δ(N=1) shifts by −10 keV·ln(1/f)/ln 5): evasion needs f ≤ 0.21 (triplet, δ → 364 keV), 0.024 (quadruplet, 356 keV), 0.006 (quintuplet, 351 keV), against thermal fractions 0.25, 0.17, 0.05. The doublet's Sommerfeld-induced line, 1.2×10⁻²⁸ cm³/s, equals P025's one-loop estimate.

**Caveats.** All limits are recalled (×2 Fermi/Planck; ×3–10 H.E.S.S. profile, AMS, line); the Y ≥ 1 exclusions exceed every band and Planck alone excludes Y ≥ 3/2 profile-free, unlike the doublet's headroom. Y ≥ 1 thermal masses are uncertain (±30 %). Leading order in m_W/m, no Sudakov resummation, maximal-Y multiplets only.

**Conclusion.** Sommerfeld enhancement resolves P025's bracket to S = 1.7 for the LZ-fitting Higgsino, which remains allowed, and excludes every heavier-hypercharge multiplet that fits LZ as dominant dark matter; only a ≤ 20 % Y = 1 triplet at δ ≈ 364 keV survives. Indirect detection, not LZ, singles out the doublet.
