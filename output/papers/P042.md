# P042: The de-excitation photon: what the excited state χ₂ must do after scattering in LZ, and what a single-site event already implies
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-10
- Primary arXiv category: hep-ph (cross-list physics.ins-det)
- Author profile: phenomenologists thinking about detector signatures
- Category (from your taxonomy): IDM
- Builds on / responds to: LZ arXiv:2609.02823 (event description, veto samples); P023 (photon-M1 lifetime), P014, P011, P026, P012, P021

**Abstract.** After an inelastic up-scatter the excited χ₂ leaves the LZ vertex at ≈ 660 km/s and later emits a δ ≈ 300 keV photon, if that channel exists. We trace χ₂ through a model of LZ and transport the photon: TPC decays (≈ 1 μs transit) make a second ER site, OD decays feed the delayed-veto sample, prompt vetoes never occur. The clean single-site event requires τ(χ₂→χ₁γ) > 0.43 μs (90 % CL), excluding photon-M1 models above δ = 358 keV.

**Question and approach.** For which lifetimes would the de-excitation photon have converted in the TPC, been tagged by the Skin/OD, or escaped, and what does the clean, un-vetoed single scatter imply? χ₂ kinematics follow from energy–momentum conservation (v₂² = u² − 2(E_R+δ)/m_χ, 9–10 % speed loss) with Baxter-SHM velocities boosted to the lab on 16 June 2023 and projected onto the SURF vertical (DM wind horizontal, altitude +0.5°). A Woodcock-tracking photon Monte Carlo (photoabsorption + Klein–Nishina) runs in a cylindrical LZ model (TPC, 13.75 cm RFR, PTFE, Skin, Ti vessels, 61 cm GdLS, water) with the paper's veto windows and thresholds; classes are integrated over the decay time for τ = 10⁻⁹–10² s.

**Results.** The χ₂ chord in the active LXe is 65 cm (27–147 cm), i.e. 0.98 μs (0.44–1.74 μs); the Skin takes 0.11 μs, the GdLS 0.99 μs, and χ₂ has left the water after ≈ 3 μs. A 300 keV photon (λ = 2.3 cm) emitted on the TPC chord always converts inside it. For τ ≫ 2 μs, P(not clean) = 1.68 μs/τ: 60.7 % second TPC site, 37.7 % delayed veto, 1.7 % RFR S1-only, 0 % prompt (χ₂ cannot reach the Skin within 0.25 μs). The clean event gives L(τ) = P_clean(τ) = 0.008, 0.10, 0.37, 0.90 at τ = 0.14, 0.43, 1.4, 15.7 μs (Fig. 1): τ > 0.43 μs at 90 % CL, BR_γ ≤ 0.9 for prompt decays. Through Γ = μ_tr²δ³/π, μ_tr < 2.6×10⁻³ (300 keV) to 1.6×10⁻³ μ_N (380 keV). Against P023's LZ-rate moments the photon-M1 model has P_clean = 0.9/0.5/0.1 at δ = 321/344/358 keV: the P007/P021 window 358–385 keV is excluded at ≥ 90 % CL whenever a photon-M1 transition carries the LZ rate. Higgsino, dark photon and an inelastic L10-type contact (loop τ ~ 300 s) escape, P(not clean) ≤ 3×10⁻⁵. A population at τ = 66 μs is 97.5 % clean, 1.5 % two-site, 0.9 % delayed-vetoed; at 1.4 μs (δ = 350 keV) 32/46/22 %. Companions are ≈ 300 keV ERs 42 cm from the vertex, second S1 0.63 μs later: separation = 700 km/s × Δt_S1.

**Caveats.** Geometry beyond the TPC, veto light yields, Skin resolution and attenuation coefficients are recalled (τ₉₀ moves ≤ 15 %); vertex azimuth averaged; K-fluorescence ignored; MiDM width and L10 loop moment are order-of-magnitude formulae; μ_tr(δ) is P023's (factor-2 spin factor, ≲ 10 keV in δ).

**Conclusion.** LZ has already measured the χ₂ lifetime by not seeing the de-excitation photon: τ_γ > 0.43 μs. Irrelevant for Z- or A′-mediated splittings, this closes δ > 358 keV to photon-M1 models. A population splits 60:38:2 between two-site events, OD delayed vetoes and RFR extra S1s, never prompt vetoes.
