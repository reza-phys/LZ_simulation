# P099: Decisive tests and a falsifiable forecast for LZ's next release: what the community should expect to see, and what would kill each interpretation
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-16
- Primary arXiv category: hep-ex (cross-list hep-ph, physics.data-an)
- Author profile: cross-collaboration working group of phenomenologists and experimentalists (position paper)
- Category (from your taxonomy): PROJ
- Builds on / responds to: LZ arXiv:2609.02823; synthesis of P081, P088, P069, P079, P038, P034, P057, P082, P061, P083, P093, P072, P076, P046, P047, P067, P052

**Abstract.** We rank the tests that can settle the origin of LZ's 248 keV event and pre-register a forecast for its next release. Recomputing only counting statistics from corpus rates, we reproduce P081/P088 to ≤ 0.013 in probability. Under the corpus posterior the next 2.8 t·yr shows ≥ 1 band event with P = 0.25 (background 6×10⁻⁴); one event alone leaves P(DM) ≈ 0.03; only off-band energy placement or a second detector lifts it above 0.5.

**Question and approach.** Which observations decide, when, and what falsifies each interpretation? Rates, acceptances and backgrounds come from the corpus: P069/P088 best-fit rates 0.121/0.063/0.178/0.274 events per t·yr in 200–270 keV (L10/δ = 300/350/366 keV); P016 background 5.7×10⁻⁴ per 2.84 t·yr; P038 extension 0.0028; P081's band-rate posterior (mean 0.385, median 0.19), matched here by Gamma(0.55, 0.70); P061's weights (DM 0.015, background 0.148, unknown 0.789, half one-off). We recompute Poisson and Gamma–Poisson counts, the mixture posterior P(DM | N), exact-Poisson 5σ probabilities and null-branch dates, validating each against P081, P088, P069 and P079.

**Results.** *Ranked tests.* (i) LZ's untouched 6.76 t·yr at 1000 phd: P(5σ | band-anchored L10) 0.43 → 0.83 (P079). (ii) Empty 600–1000 phd sideband: P(0 | δ = 366 keV) = 0.022, 1.2×10⁻⁴ with the untouched data; δ = 350 survives (0.08). (iii) XENONnT/PandaX-4T reanalysis (10.1 t·yr): P(≥ 1) = 0.71–0.94 at best fit, 0.50 posterior; two events in 17.9 t·yr give 4.4σ. (iv) Shape (2–7 events, P057), modulation (15–33 t·yr, P034), CaWO₄ (δ ≥ 366 keV only) and DR-003 waveforms (±0.45 dex, P093) follow.

*Forecast, next 2.8 t·yr (DM posterior / background):* F1 N = 0: 0.75 / 0.9994; F2 N ≥ 1: 0.25 / 5.6×10⁻⁴; F4 55–270 keV: 0.50 / 0.11; F5 270–420 keV: 0.26 / 0.003. *Rule:* one band event: BF 300 vs background, 0.67 vs steady unknown, P(DM) = 0.025; two in 270–420 keV → 0.99, band + extension 0.57, both in band 0.011; zero events: P(DM) < 10⁻³ by Aug 2027 (L10) or Nov 2030 (δ300) (Fig. 1).

*Kill conditions.* Inelastic δ = 335–390 keV: empty extended sideband (δ ≥ 366) or any event below 125 keV (LR ≤ 0.04; Higgsino already dead, P076). Elastic L10/O6: zero events in 17.9 t·yr (P(0) = 0.12); XLZD 2033. Exothermic: P(no companion) = 0.005 (P072). One-off background: any new band event; steady unknown: events elsewhere or off-band; artefact: DR-003.

**Caveats.** Not settled before 2030: mass (642 events, P062), δ beyond the ±16 keV halo floor (P050), modulation for δ ≤ 350 keV, operator identity (15–60 events, P057), rates ≤ 0.3× best fit (XLZD 2033), and DM versus a free-rate unknown by counting alone (cap 0.09, P001). "Best fit" differs ×2.9 between band-anchored (P020/P079) and ROI-anchored (P069/P081) normalisations; our Gamma tail overstates P081's 90 % exposure (261 vs 123 t·yr); dates rest on P088's recalled exposures.

**Conclusion.** The verdict is on disk: reanalysis and LZ's extended sideband decide "population or one-off" and kill δ ≥ 366 keV by 2027. The corpus expects LZ's next 2.8 t·yr to be empty (0.75); a lone new band event must not be read as confirmation.
