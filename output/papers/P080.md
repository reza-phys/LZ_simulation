# P080: Millicharged particles, strongly interacting massive relics and other exotic heavy particles as the source of a single 248 keV xenon recoil
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-15
- Primary arXiv category: hep-ph
- Author profile: exotic-relics phenomenology group
- Category (from your taxonomy): EXO
- Builds on / responds to: LZ paper (arXiv:2609.02823); P040 (N_lo floor), P030, P003, P016, P017, P036, P041, P042, P023

**Abstract.** Could a heavy relic other than a WIMP-like particle leave one single-site ~248 keV recoil in LZ's 2.84 t·yr? Halo millicharged particles need ε ≈ 2×10⁻⁸ at 1 TeV but their Rutherford spectrum implies 2×10⁵–8×10⁵ low-energy companions; pointlike SIMPs ≥ 680. An opaque composite (σ ≈ 8×10⁻²⁵ cm², m ≈ 10¹⁷ GeV) gives 1.4, yet predicts 4.8 events in 55–200 keV and the empty high-energy sideband (p = 0.008). Tracks, monopoles and Planck-scale relics fail on dE/dx or flux.

**Question and approach.** Candidates: (a) millicharged particles, halo and relativistic; (b) SIMPs and composites (nuclearites, Q-balls, dark nuclei) scattering once or continuously; (c) charged relics, monopoles, superheavy DM; (d) inelastic composites. Recoil spectra use lzcommon (Helm) and P017's WimPyDD shell response, the LZ efficiency (P003 model), the Baxter SHM and the paper's geometry (4.71 t FV = 1.29 m × 1.24 m; 7 t TPC, mean chord 97 cm). For heavy relics a chord Monte Carlo gives P(exactly one scatter); the zero-event high-energy sideband (800–1700 phd) is mapped to 375–732 keV with LZ-tuned NEST; rock transparency (1478 m), Rutherford and Lindhard stopping and the flux ceiling from N = 1 are evaluated.

**Results.** *Millicharged.* dσ/dE_R ∝ ε²Z²F²/(v²E_R²) is the contact-O1 spectrum times (248/E_R)²: N_lo (5.4–55 per 200–270 keV) = 2.3×10⁵ (Helm) / 7.7×10⁵ (shell) at 1 TeV, 6.6×10⁶ at 100 GeV. One event needs ε₁ = 1.7×10⁻⁸ (σ_SI-equivalent 4.5×10⁻⁴² cm²); at this ε the overburden removes 10⁻⁸ of the kinetic energy and heliospheric fields deflect nothing (thresholds ε > 2×10⁻⁴, 10⁻²). Relativistic MCPs: N_lo = 9.6×10⁴ / 3.2×10⁵, plus 4400 ERs per NR.

*SIMPs.* λ = 97 cm needs σ_χXe = 7.8×10⁻²⁵ cm²; P(1 scatter) peaks at 0.31 with P(≥ 2) = 0.27, i.e. 8 multi-scatter traversals per window event unless σ < 1.2×10⁻²⁵ cm². Pointlike coupling gives N_lo = 676–2270. An opaque composite (E_R flat to 2m_Nv² = 1.3 MeV) gives N_lo = 1.43 but 3.2 events in 55–200 keV and 1.65 in the sideband per window event: P(0 | 1) = 0.008. One event needs m = 1.1×10¹⁷ GeV; rock is transparent above 1.2×10⁶ GeV (Fig. 2).

*Tracks, flux.* Nuclearites deposit σρv² = 2.8 TeV/cm, a unit-charge relic 74 MeV/cm, a monopole 2–12 MeV/cm. N = 1 gives F = 2.2×10⁻¹³ cm⁻² s⁻¹ sr⁻¹ (219× Parker) and m ≤ 3.9×10¹⁸ p GeV (p = interaction probability); a Planck-mass relic crosses the FV 0.32 times.

*Dark nuclei.* δ_max → 405 keV, R_D ≲ 0.8 fm; otherwise the IDM window (P007, P021, P042).

**Caveats.** The opaque-composite spectrum assumes classical isotropic hard-sphere scattering; intermediate composites lie between the flat and F²-weighted cases, both disfavoured. Visible-scatter threshold 1 keV (0.2 % of scatters below). The sideband energy map uses NEST beyond its 300 keV calibration; the 55–200 keV emptiness follows LZ's statement and P016's digitisation. Stopping formulae, TPC dimensions and external limits are recalled (16 items, 5 uncertain); no conclusion rests on the uncertain ones.

**Conclusion.** No exotic heavy relic reproduces a lone 248 keV recoil: millicharged and pointlike strongly interacting relics flood the low-energy band, opaque composites populate 55–200 keV and the empty sideband (p = 0.008), and tracks, monopoles and Planck-scale relics fail on dE/dx or flux. Only inelastic composites survive, as IDM.
