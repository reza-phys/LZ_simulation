# P065: Magnetic inelastic dark matter (MiDM) at LZ: the transition-dipole scattering spectrum, the de-excitation photon and the surviving parameter space
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-12

- Author profile: magnetic inelastic dark-matter model builders

**Abstract.** MiDM: a Majorana pair split by δ with a transition moment μ_χ. In WimPyDD the dipole–dipole term carries 90 % of the 248 keV rate and the Z² term 1 %, so no low-energy tail exists for δ > 246 keV. One event needs μ_χ = 2.2×10⁻⁴ μ_N (300 keV); the implied lifetime excludes δ > 355 keV. The thermal moment fits only at δ ≈ 351 keV (P(clean) = 0.15). MiDM predicts iodine ≫ xenon ≫ tungsten.

**Question and approach.** What spectrum, moment, lifetime, relic density and target scaling follow if the LZ event is MiDM (Chang–Weiner–Yavin) at 1 TeV? We implement the photon-coupled dipole in NREFT (P012/P023 coefficients, WimPyDD inelastic kinematics, Sun-frame Baxter halo with June-16 and annual checks, P003 efficiency, 2.84 t·yr), separating the dipole–charge and dipole–dipole pieces, for δ = 250–380 keV. Γ = μ_χ²δ³/π gives τ(δ) once μ_χ(δ) is fixed by N = 1 (band 0.3–2.4); P042's P(clean|τ) tables convert it into an exclusion. The annihilation χ₁χ₂ → γ* → ff̄ is derived at threshold (Dirac-matrix check); W and I rates use the same Hamiltonian.

**Results.** *Spectrum.* At 248 keV the charge term (∝ Z²v⊥²/q²) is 0.6–0.9 % of the rate for δ = 0–300 keV, the dipole–dipole term 90 %, interference 9 % (elastic: N_lo/hi 386, P012 376). At δ = 200 keV MiDM leaves 0.22 low-energy companions per high-energy event (contact O₁: 7.65); the 5.4–55 keV window is empty for δ > 246 keV. The spectrum is harder than O₁: accepted peak 196 vs 172 keV, 248 keV at the 88.6th percentile (O₁ 99.5th). *Moment.* μ_χ(N = 1) = 2.19×10⁻⁴ μ_N = 2.3×10⁻¹⁸ e·cm = 0.23 e/(2m_χ) at 300 keV (June 1.77×10⁻⁴; annual 2.108×10⁻⁴ vs P023's 2.107×10⁻⁴). *Lifetime.* τ = 61 μs (300 keV, P(clean) = 0.973), 0.93 μs (350); P(clean) = 0.9/0.5/0.1 at δ = 321/342/355 keV (N = 0.3–2.4: ±7 keV; June +10), matching P042 to 3 keV. *Constraints.* No tree-level elastic scattering; low-energy counts in LZ-2024/XENONnT/PandaX ≤ 5×10⁻⁵ for δ ≥ 250 keV. *Relic.* ⟨σv⟩(χ₁χ₂ → ff̄) = αμ_χ²Σ_fN_cQ_f² (mass-independent) = 8.5×10⁻²⁸ cm³/s: Ω h² = 6.2, 52× over-abundant. The thermal moment 1.57×10⁻³ μ_N gives N = 1 only at δ = 351 keV (344–358), where τ = 0.74 μs and P(clean) = 0.15. No 300 keV line: χ₂ decays before BBN. *Targets.* W/Xe = 0.13/0.25/0.94 and I/Xe = 12.4/7.3/1.7 at 300/350/366 keV, versus O₁'s 16.5/108/360 and 0.58/0.21/0.03.

**Caveats.** NREFT coefficients and Γ are recalled (likely); a Majorana normalisation factor of O(1) would shift μ and μ_th together, leaving the δ_thermal conclusion within ±5 keV. Efficiency and June curve are models; P(clean) inherits P042's geometry (≤ 15 %). Tungsten spin response is WimPyDD's Gaussian placeholder; the W⁺W⁻ channel (+10 %) and co-annihilation are omitted.

**Conclusion.** MiDM explains the lone 248 keV recoil as a spin–spin scatter with μ_χ ≈ 2×10⁻⁴ μ_N, survives only for δ ≲ 355 keV, is 50× over-abundant unless another annihilation channel exists, and is refuted or confirmed by an iodine target at 12× the xenon rate.
