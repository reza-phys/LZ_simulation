# P088: Decisive-test timeline: LZ, XENONnT, PandaX-4T, XLZD and CaWO₄ through 2028–2032 — when does each interpretation of the 248 keV event get a 3σ/5σ verdict?
- Simulated arXiv date: 2026-09-16
- Primary arXiv category: hep-ex
- Author profile: experimental-strategy and projections group
- Category (from your taxonomy): PROJ
- Builds on / responds to: LZ arXiv:2609.02823; P016, P020, P034, P038, P046, P047, P050, P057, P058, P059, P061, P062, P067, P069

**Abstract.** We fold the corpus's rates, acceptances, backgrounds and event requirements into one Monte-Carlo calendar. At LZ's best fit the present xenon generation reaches a 5σ population-versus-one-off verdict (new data only) by Apr 2027 for L10 and already holds it for δ ≥ 350 keV; at the lower 68 % rate only XLZD (2033) does. Separating inelastic from L10 needs shape or June timing, both on disk for δ ≥ 366 keV; CRESST-scale CaWO₄ is blind below δ = 366 keV.

**Question and approach.** For A (inelastic O₁, 1 TeV, δ = 300/350/366/380 keV), B (L10) and C (one-off), when do LZ, XENONnT, PandaX-4T, their sum, XLZD and CaWO₄ reach 3σ/5σ? Exposure ledger from P069/P020 (on disk now: LZ 10.5, XENONnT 6.2, PandaX-4T 4.1 t·yr, 17.9 unexamined above 200 keV; accrual 2.79/2.4/1.62 t·yr per year; XLZD 48 t·yr/yr from 2032). Rates per t·yr at the best fit from P069/P050 (200–270 keV: 0.121/0.063/0.178/0.274/0.278 for L10/δ300/350/366/380; 200–400 keV: 0.220/0.112/0.516/1.63/10.5); backgrounds 5.7×10⁻⁴ (P016) and 2.7×10⁻³ per 2.84 t·yr (P050). We recompute only statistics: Asimov Z, 20 000 exact-Poisson first-crossing toys (best fit, ×0.30, Gamma-marginalised), Gamma–Poisson Bayes factors, and P(DM) updates from P061's prior 0.015.

**Results.** *Counting (Fig. 1, table; dates = exposure on disk).* The unexamined data hold 2.2/1.1/3.2/4.9 expected events (L10/δ300/350/366); two new events give 4.3σ (5.5σ with LZ's) and BF > 100. P(5σ new-only by end-2028 | best fit) = 0.78/0.36/0.94/0.995; at ×0.30 only 0.13/0.03/0.27/0.52. LZ's empty SR3 600–1000 phd sideband already gives P(0) = 0.02 (δ366); its 9.6 t·yr expect 3.6/13/103 events at δ = 350/366/380 (P038).

*Settled.* If A/B holds at the best fit, P(BF > 100) = 0.91/0.62/0.98/1.0 by end-2028. If C holds, zero events never give BF < 1/100 for L10 before 2033, but P(DM) falls from 0.015 below 10⁻³ by Aug 2027 (L10), Nov 2030 (δ300). *A vs B.* Shape (2–7 events) is on disk; June timing (6.5/11.5/97 events) is on disk for δ366, Apr 2028 for δ350, never for δ300. CaWO₄ 10 kg·yr: 2.8/0.37/0.03 events (δ366/350/300), P(3σ) = 0.62/0.03/0.004.

**Caveats.** Dates are "needed exposure on disk"; verdicts follow reanalysis (P069: mid-2027 at the earliest). Untouched XENONnT/PandaX-4T exposures, fiducial masses, live fractions, XLZD start and CaWO₄ backgrounds (0.01–0.1/kg·yr) are recalled; other TPCs' high-energy acceptance assumed LZ-like; δ ≥ 366 keV with a 1000 phd edge is already disfavoured (P038); counting cannot beat P001's 0.09 cap against a free-rate unknown.

**Conclusion.** The verdict is on disk: reanalysis decides "population or one-off" at the best fit by 2027–2028 and δ ≥ 350 keV immediately; only a ×0.3 rate defers to XLZD (2033). Tungsten and isotopes add nothing before the mid-2030s.
