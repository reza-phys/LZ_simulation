# 99 · Synthesis: the simulated community response to the LZ 248 keV event, 3–16 September 2026

*Coordinator's synthesis of the 100-paper corpus (P001–P100). Every number is traceable to `results_ledger.csv` and the cited papers; recalled external numbers carry the reliability flags of the papers that used them.*

## 1. Where the simulated community stands after two weeks

The corpus converged on three facts and one open question.

**Fact 1 — the event is a single nuclear recoil at 246–248 keV, not a mundane background.** Wall multiple-scatter-single-ionisation (MSSI) needs a mismodelling factor k ≥ 620 (P004), 6×10⁴ with photon transport (P033), 1.4×10⁵ with the vetoes' silence (P073), against a sideband-allowed k < 1.64, and the whole MSSI channel is worth 0.2σ (P090). Electronic-recoil leakage needs a recombination tail that LZ's Fig. 5 and the band-shape symmetry exclude at the 10⁻⁵–10⁻³ level (P010, P056); Migdal or bremsstrahlung companions push recoils *up*, not down, the band (P095); the charge-poor position is a 6 % fluctuation for a genuine NR (P024). Neutrons give ≤ 10⁻⁵ lone events (P013, P049, P063), accidentals are excluded at 4.3σ (P022), activation cannot reach 248 keV (P029), and atmospheric CEνNS contributes 3×10⁻⁵ events per tonne-year (P019, P087). A single NR beats each modelled class by 10^3.6–10^6.9 (P093); the energy scale is consistent (246–248 keV, σ_E = 11.4 keV; P009, P064, P098).

**Fact 2 — the statistical case is exactly what LZ said, and no stronger.** The 3.4σ local was reproduced to 3.0–3.2σ from the published summaries (P052), the look-elsewhere factor to N_eff = 12–14 (P008, P071), and the global 2.6σ survives every alternative treatment; the ~200 spectra the community itself added lower it to 2.50σ (P071). One event is strong evidence against the *modelled* background (B₁₀ ≈ 10²–10³, P001) but only positive evidence for dark matter once the model space and unmodelled backgrounds are admitted (B_DM = 16–29, P027). Under a sceptical community prior P(DM) has median 0.015 (P061), matching a historical base rate of 0.06 for unpredicted ~3σ hints in direct detection, none of which has turned out real (P083).

**Fact 3 — only two DM readings fit *naturally*.** Elastic spin-independent scattering is excluded by the absence of ~2 700 low-energy companions (P003, P016). Inelastic isoscalar O₁ peaks at δ ≈ 380 keV for 1 TeV (P021), a preference the S1c < 600 phd edge manufactures (P038); the joint direct-detection region is m ≥ 400 GeV, δ = 335–390 keV (P082). The alternative is a companion-free elastic operator of the L10/O6/O9 family, which needs a UV scale Λ ≪ m_χ (P031, P074).

**The open question is whether the event is real at all**, and it cannot be settled from the 2.84 t·yr analysed. The residual probability sits in the *unmodelled* classes — an artefact LZ has not characterised and a genuine one-off — and only new data or waveform-level information moves it. Three demands crystallised, as forecast: analyse LZ's untouched exposure (P020, P079, P088), re-open XENONnT's and PandaX-4T's data above 200 keV (P005, P035, P069), and release enough of the likelihood for independent fits (DR-001–003). The stance tally is telling: 81 of 100 papers declared themselves neutral tools or constraints, 13 statistical reassessments, 5 weakly pro-DM (all by elimination) and none pro-background — the corpus eliminated backgrounds without finding evidence *for* anything (P100).

I raise A from 0.03 to 0.13 because P091's likelihood for A is the *local* background density at the event, whereas the honest reference for "a fluctuation" is the whole 293-spectrum search (global p ≈ 0.006) and the P083 base rate; F is lowered correspondingly. **What changed them.** Classes B–E lost their weight to arguments that did not exist on 2 September: MSSI position and veto ratios (P033, P070, P073, P093), the recombination-tail bound (P056), the accidental UDT count (P022), the neutron chain (P013, P049, P063). Class G collapsed with P009/P064/P098. Class F could not be shrunk: the S1 pulse shape at 540 phd is intrinsically weak (P077), no waveform quantities were published (DR-003), and every computable artefact (charge loss P041, activation P029) was excluded, leaving the uncharacterised residual. Within DM, weight moved from the pure Higgsino to generic pseudo-Dirac fermions with a 9–35 GeV dark photon, because solar capture excludes the fixed-coupling Higgsino at every δ (P076) and Sommerfeld-enhanced annihilation excludes the higher multiplets (P084). Class K shrank as boosted, exothermic-origin, millicharged and composite relics fell to companion counts (P040, P072, P080).

## 3. The ten most important papers

1. **P021 + P038 (inelastic likelihood and the efficiency edge).** The O₁ maximum at δ ≈ 380 keV (3.6σ) is manufactured by the 600 phd edge (1000 phd → 355 keV, 2.9σ).
2. **P004 / P033 / P073 / P090 (wall MSSI).** k_required 620 → 1.4×10⁵ against k < 1.64; 0.2σ of significance at stake.
3. **P076 (solar capture).** The fixed-coupling Higgsino annihilates in the Sun 10²–10³ above IceCube's limits at every δ; the 360–385 keV corner closes for all three mediators.
4. **P052 (LZ likelihood reproduced).** LZ's 2D likelihood rebuilt to rms 0.42σ; 3.03σ toy-calibrated; residual in unpublished within-panel shapes (DR-002).
5. **P027 + P061 + P083 (evidence, priors, base rates).** B_DM = 16–29, P(DM) median 0.015, and a base rate of 0.06 that coincides with the community prior.
6. **P003 + P016 (companion counting).** N_lo (low-energy events per 200–270 keV event) as the decisive statistic: SI 2 750, q⁴-spin ≤ 5.
7. **P082 (global inelastic combination).** m ≥ 400 GeV, δ = 335–390 keV, σ_n = 8×10⁻⁴²–3×10⁻³⁶ cm² at 1 TeV; archival zeros cost 0.06–0.11σ.
8. **P069 + P088 (the xenon world and the timeline).** 18 t·yr on disk never examined above 200 keV hold 2–5 events at the best fit; a 5σ verdict is on disk for δ ≥ 350 keV and comes by 2027 for L10.
9. **P068 + P018 + P085 (astrophysical inputs).** v_esc dominates every inelastic inference (κ spans 3×10⁻³–150 at δ = 366 keV); v_obs = 265.74 km/s; solar focusing ×1.04–1.16.
10. **P093 + P091 (topology odds and the posterior).** NR beats every modelled background by ≥ 10^3.6 but the artefact class by only 10^2.5; the posterior is a two-horse race.

## 4. The most viable dark-matter scenarios

**Inelastic pseudo-Dirac fermion with a GeV-scale dark photon or a heavy Z′ (leading).** m_χ ≥ 400 GeV, flat likelihood above 1 TeV (P062, P082); δ = 335–390 keV at 90 % with the LZ edge, 300–360 keV if the 1000 phd re-analysis moves the peak as P038 predicts; σ_n(N = 1) = 8×10⁻⁴²–3×10⁻³⁶ cm² at 1 TeV (P082), i.e. 1.2×10⁻⁴²–2×10⁻³⁹ cm² proton-only (P011). Viability: m_A′ = 9–35 GeV, ε = 7×10⁻⁵–10⁻³ after Planck, BBN/dark-Higgs and BaBar (P025, P086); thermal relic via secluded annihilation for δ ≤ 356 keV at 1 TeV, any δ with dilution 1.3–2.3 (P075); χ₂ must have decayed, τ < 3–9×10¹⁶ s (P026, P058, P066, P094). Heavy Z′ survives only for m_Z′ ≲ m_χ and δ ≤ 300–340 keV (P054, P075). Solar-neutrino limits already exclude δ ≥ 360–370 keV for both mediators (P076).

**Electroweak multiplets.** The pure Higgsino (δ = 366–380 keV at 1–1.3 TeV; P007, P037, P075) is excluded by solar capture (P076); Y ≥ 1 multiplets fall to Sommerfeld-enhanced annihilation unless they are ≤ 20 % sub-components (P084).

**Companion-free q⁴-spin elastic operators (L10/O6/O9/O14/O15).** d₁₀ ≈ 0.28 (0.15–0.43) in LZ's normalisation at 1 TeV (P012; provisional pending DR-001), N_lo = 0.2–0.4, 2.4 events predicted in LZ's untouched exposure (P020). Requires Λ = 1.5–62 GeV ≪ m_χ (P031); no light mediator rescues an excluded operator (P074).

**Magnetic inelastic and composite dark hadrons.** μ_χ ≈ 2×10⁻⁴ μ_N at δ = 300 keV; the de-excitation photon excludes δ > 355 keV; the thermal moment fits only at δ ≈ 351 keV with P(clean) = 0.15 (P023, P042, P065). Alive but contrived.

**Excluded.** Elastic SI and any velocity-independent contact interaction (P003, P030, P040); exothermic origin (B ≤ 2, 2–7 companions in the empty 125–200 keV bin; P058, P072); nuclear-excitation hybrids (P036); photon-mediated anapole/EDM (P044); boosted, millicharged, SIMP and superheavy relics (P040, P080). No stream, substructure or dark disk reaches 700 km/s (P030, P055, P097).

## 5. The most viable non-DM explanations

1. **A fluctuation of the modelled background** at 2.5–2.6σ global (P008, P071), rate 3.5–9×10⁻⁴ in the event's neighbourhood (P052, P093): the default, and the base rate's favourite (P083).
2. **An uncharacterised instrumental artefact** — the one class bounded only to 10⁻²·⁵ relative odds (P093). Computable variants are excluded (P041, P029, P022); what remains is whatever waveform quantities could reveal (DR-003; S1 pulse shape carries LR ≤ 2–5, P077).
3. **Wall or RFR MSSI with a grossly wrong spatial model.** Needs k ≥ 10³–10⁵ and contradicts the sidebands (P004, P033, P073, P090); still, LZ's own MSSI table implies a wall depth scale of 1.2–1.4 cm, seven times steeper than photon transport (P079), so the model's *shape* is genuinely uncertain.
4. **A charge-poor electronic recoil** (¹²⁴Xe/¹²⁵I double-vacancy line with anomalous charge suppression): ≤ 10⁻² events, excluded by the empty inter-band gap (P010, P056).

Neutrons, astrophysical neutrinos and nuclear excitations are not viable (P013, P049, P063, P060, P087, P036).

## 7. A falsifiable prediction for LZ's next data release

Pre-registered (machinery: P081, P088, P099). Assume LZ releases ≈ 6.8 t·yr (524 live days) with the current selections.

1. **Background or one-off:** zero events in the 200–270 keV NR band with probability ≥ 0.998 (modelled 5.6×10⁻⁴ per 2.8 t·yr; 9×10⁻⁴ with the corpus's residuals); zero in 55–270 keV with probability ≥ 0.99.
2. **Dark matter at the corpus posterior rate:** per 2.8 t·yr P(0/1/≥2) = 0.75/0.17/0.08 in the band and P(≥1) = 0.50 in 55–270 keV; for 6.8 t·yr P(≥1) ≈ 0.5 (band) and ≈ 0.8 (55–270 keV). The band-anchored best fit (P020, P079) is ×2.9 higher than the ROI-anchored posterior mean (P069, P081) and predicts 2.4 events, P(0) = 0.09; both are quoted as a bracket (P099).
3. **Decision rule:** N = 0 → P(DM) < 10⁻³ by August 2027 for the elastic reading, November 2030 for δ = 300 keV; N = 1 in the band → P(DM) ≈ 0.025 (Bayes factor 300 against the modelled background, 0.67 against a steady LZ-specific unknown); N ≥ 2 with one event in 270–420 keV or 55–200 keV → P(DM) = 0.57–0.99; two events both in the band → 0.011.
4. **The extended sideband decides δ.** With a 1000 phd edge the same data must show 3.6/13/103 events at 600–1000 phd if δ = 350/366/380 keV and none if the reading is elastic; the untouched data push P(0 | δ = 366) to 1.2×10⁻⁴ (P038, P088, P099).
5. **Outside LZ:** the 17.9 t·yr on disk contain 2.2/1.1/3.2/4.9 events (L10/δ 300/350/366) at the best fit, P(0) = 0.12/0.32/0.04/0.007.

## 8. Self-assessment of the Phase-2 forecast

**Wrong or over-weighted.** (i) Wall MSSI and the ER tail as the leading mundane candidates (dossier B 0.19, C 0.13): both collapsed under the first quantitative attack (P004, P010); the residual detector weight belongs to the uncharacterised-artefact class, which the corpus could barely move. (ii) The pure Higgsino as flagship: solar capture (P076), a constraint absent from the plan's COMP list, kills it at every δ, and Sommerfeld enhancement removes the higher multiplets (P084). (iii) The δ ≈ 380 keV preference treated as physics; it is the 600 phd edge (P038), and moving the edge is the single most valuable analysis (P079, P088). (iv) "No comparable high-energy xenon analysis exists" — true, but I missed that ≈ 18 t·yr of unexamined data should already contain 2–5 events (P069); the decisive test is a re-analysis, not new exposure. (v) The ±23 keV and "265 keV" taken at face value; resolved by P009, P064, P098. (vi) Four halo-substructure papers (P055, P085, P092, P097) where every effect was a few-percent deficit; only v_esc and the tail matter (P018, P068). (vii) A ×2.9 normalisation split between band- and ROI-anchored "best fits" ran through the projection papers unnoticed until P099; the plan should have fixed one convention.

**Right.** The volume and category mix of the real response (≈ 28 papers, IDM-heavy); the "intriguing but not evidence" consensus; the three community demands; inelastic DM near the edge and q⁴-spin operators as the only natural fits; the statistical critique landing at 2.5–2.6σ global; and the deliberate re-weighting of this corpus towards BKG/RESP/STAT (34 papers), which produced its most robust results.

## 9. Process-level tool summary

**Coordination.** One subagent per paper (100 agents) following `provenance/PAPER_GUIDE.md`, launched in batches of 4–10, ledger updated after every batch (`process_log.md`, 212 steps). The coordinator wrote the dossier, plan, shared library, ledger/inventory tooling and this synthesis, and validated every page with `ledger_tools.py check`.

**Tool calls by the paper agents** (100 provenance JSONs): Read 1 722, Bash 1 517, Edit 1 371, Write 447, Skill (dataviz) 63, ToolSearch 24, TaskStop 21, Monitor 8 — 5 173 in all, 52 per paper. No network, no installs, no interpreter other than `.venv/bin/python` (one deviation: `node` run by the dataviz palette validator in P046/P047 before it was banned).

**Software by kind of paper** (papers of 100): numpy, matplotlib and `lzcommon.py` in all; scipy 93; pandas 88. **WimPyDD 2.0.4** 65 — every IDM, EFT, HALO, XEXP paper and most PROJ/MODEL/STAT papers needing spectra (shell-model responses, q-dependent NREFT closures, per-isotope/per-stream kernels, inelastic and exothermic kinematics, WimPyC capture); its convention was settled by P003 and three pitfalls documented. **nestpy 2.1.1** 21 — all RESP and most BKG/NUC papers. **PyMuPDF** (7) and **Pillow** (8) for figure digitisation, mostly BKG/STAT. iminuit, wimprates, numericalunits, astropy, radioactivedecay, periodictable, healpy, directdm, uncertainties and sympy in 1–4 papers each. Hand derivations were primary in 51.

**Recalled knowledge.** 1 152 flagged items (38 % certain, 42 % likely, 19 % uncertain), catalogued R01–R99 in `tool_inventory.md`. The consequential uncertain ones are external limits (IceCube/ANTARES solar, H.E.S.S./Fermi/AMS, dijet Z′ ceilings, archival ROI edges) and LZ geometry and light-yield numbers absent from the paper.

**Unavailable tools.** No internet (three data requests instead); no GEANT4 (ray-march Monte Carlos in numpy: P033, P042, P063, P073, P077); no NEST beyond nestpy (ER skew model rebuilt in P056); `ps`/`pgrep`/`timeout` absent; harness limits (600 s foreground, 20 concurrent agents, watchdog) forced kernel caching in 14 papers and three agent resumptions.
