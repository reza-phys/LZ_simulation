# P100 — Two weeks after: a critical review of the interpretations of the LZ 248 keV event — research record

Simulated arXiv date 2026-09-16 · hep-ph (cross-list hep-ex, physics.data-an) · STAT (review) · invited critical review by a senior direct-detection group not affiliated with LZ.

## 1. Motivation and framework

Between 3 and 16 September 2026 a simulated community produced 99 papers (P001–P099) on the single ~248 keV nuclear-recoil-like event that LZ reported in arXiv:2609.02823 (S1c = 540.1 phd, S2c = 9268 phd, 16 June 2023, 2.84 t·yr, local 3.4σ, global 2.6σ). This review reads the whole corpus, states where the evidence stands in each of the twelve taxonomy categories, and asks the questions a referee would ask of the corpus itself: where it is internally inconsistent, which of its results rest on recalled numbers of uncertain reliability, which are provisional pending the three data requests, whether its collective stance is biased, and what it failed to ask. **No new physics is computed**; the only computations are tallies and ratios from `output/results_ledger.csv` and the paper pages (`output/code/P100_review_tallies.py`, runtime 1.4 s).

Status of the concurrently written papers at finalisation (checked four times: at the start, before writing Results, after the first script run, and before finalising): `output/papers/P090.md` and `output/papers/P091.md` both exist and both now have ledger rows. The ledger grew from 97 rows (start) to 98 (P090 appended during the first script run) to 99 (P091 appended during the budget check). The final tallies therefore cover **99 papers = 99 ledger rows**; the script retains the ability to add a page absent from the ledger (used transiently for P090 and P091 in earlier runs; `added_from_pages_not_in_ledger` is now empty). The verdict table cites P091's explanation-class posterior as instructed.

## 2. Method

1. **Reading.** The ledger (all 98 rows, all columns) and the full one-page papers P001, P003, P004, P007, P008, P009, P016, P020, P021, P024, P027, P033, P035, P038, P052, P054, P056, P061, P068, P069, P071, P073, P076, P079, P081, P082, P083, P084, P088, P089, P090, P091, P093, P098, P099 (35 pages), plus the three data-request files and their index, the P099 provenance JSON (format template) and the P099 details header, and the LZ paper's passages on salting/blinding, fiducial volume, detector stability around 16 June and the Data Release (lines 60, 94, 112, 132, 137, 160–165, 300–303, 316, 334, 428–434).
2. **Verdict table.** Hand-authored per category from the reading: what was established, the load-bearing papers, the confidence, what remains open. Stored machine-readably as `verdict_table.csv`, `verdict_table.json`, `verdict_table.md`.
3. **Tallies.** From the ledger + P091: stance classes (parsed from the free-text `stance` field: "supports" / "favours" / "statistical reassessment" split into weakens / strengthens / neutral by keyword / "neutral"), confidence (leading word), result type, primary category (token before "/" or "("; the five dual-category rows P032 IDM/EFT, P046 XEXP/PROJ, P058 COMP (IDM), P064 NUC/RESP, P070 BKG/STAT are assigned to their first label), data-request dependencies (`data_dependence` containing "provisional" or "inherit"), recalled-knowledge counts and the presence of an "uncertain" flag.
4. **Halo-frame scan.** Regular-expression counts of "Sun-frame", "annual/time-averaged" and "16 June/June halo" in every paper page.
5. **Convention spreads, recalled-uncertain dependencies, data-request map.** Tabulated from the cited pages (`tables/`).
6. **Figure.** Category × stance and category × confidence stacked bars (dataviz reference palette; categorical hue fixed by stance class; ordinal blue ramp for confidence; legend below the axes).

## 6. Critical section (a): internal inconsistencies and inconsistent conventions (`tables/P100_convention_spreads.csv`)

1. **Halo frame (P035 finding).** WimPyDD's default `wd_halo()` is a single-day Sun-frame halo (v_E = 250.6 km/s), not an annual average; the two differ by ×1.08/1.7/10 in the inelastic rate at δ = 300/350/380 keV, and LZ itself used the time-averaged SHM (P007, P021). Nine papers used the Sun frame only (P011, P035, P037, P059, P065, P067, P074, P076, P089), eight the annual mean only, ten both (as variants), twelve the 16 June halo (correct for date-specific quantities). *Conclusions that depend on it:* P021's likelihood peak (380 keV annual vs 370 keV Sun frame), P007's Higgsino δ(N = 1) (±5 keV), P035's absolute zero-event limits above 360 keV (×1.7–10; P035 chose the Sun frame because it reproduced LZ's Fig. 6 edge at 350 keV within 8 % whereas the annual mean gave ×0.63 — which itself suggests LZ's Fig. 6 at 350 keV is not on the annual-mean halo, an unresolved puzzle), P082's region width (22 %), P054/P011's dark-photon couplings above 350 keV (×1.4–4). *Conclusions that do not:* every N_lo ratio, every result at δ ≤ 300 keV, all background eliminations, P068 (frame is ≤ 4 % of the variance once v_esc is scanned). The corpus never adopted one convention; the honest statement is that any δ quoted above 350 keV carries a ±10 keV convention uncertainty on top of P068's ±30 keV astrophysical band.
2. **The ×2.9 "best-fit" normalisation (flagged by P099).** P020 and P079 anchor "the L10 best fit" to one event *in the 200–270 keV band* per 2.84 t·yr (0.352 events/t·yr), predicting 2.4 events in the untouched 6.76 t·yr with P(0) = 9 % and "zero excludes the best fit at 90 %". P069, P081, P088 and P099 anchor to LZ's Table I fit of 1.0 events *in the whole ROI* (0.121/t·yr in the band), giving 0.8 events and P(≥ 1) = 0.25 under the posterior. Both are called "best fit"; only the second is LZ's. P079 concedes the ×2.7 in a caveat. Consequences: P020's headline forecast and P079's P(5σ) = 0.83 are ×2.9 optimistic relative to LZ's own fit; P088's dates use the ROI-anchored rate; a reader combining P020 with P081 would find them contradictory. Recommendation: the ledger should carry one normalisation.
3. **σ_E = 11 vs 23 keV (P098).** P008 and P027 smeared spectra with 23√(E/248) keV (LZ's quoted ±23 stat); P009, P021, P038, P089 and P098 used 11√(E/248) (quanta statistics; P098 shows 23 keV would need 4× the physical variance). P052 found that adding the 23 keV resolution *improved* its reproduction of Tables S6/S7 (rms 0.52 → 0.42σ). Either LZ smears with its systematic folded in, or the tables encode something else; only DR-002 resolves it. Results that depend on the choice: the in-window shape factor ρ in P027 (0.36–0.57 at the node), N_eff for the δ scan in P008 (resolution-wide windows), P021's peak (≤ 5 keV).
4. **Wall-MSSI depth scale 4.3 vs 1.2 cm.** P033's photon-transport MC finds the 12 keV wall-MSSI Compton class falls as e^{−d/4.3 cm}; P079 infers λ = 1.23 cm from LZ's own 5.4 t / 4.7 t MSSI table (6.25 wall events in the annulus). P090 shows the difference is worth 0.4σ: λ = 4.3 cm implies a fiducial MSSI factor 7.3 and 2.76σ for a position-blind fit (3.16σ position-aware). The two λ's probably describe different populations (the 12 keV Compton class vs the low-S2 leakage that motivated the 8 cm stand-off), but the corpus has not reconciled them, and P004's, P033's and P073's k_required chain multiplies factors from both descriptions. Direction is robust (wall MSSI stays excluded by ≥ 10² in every combination), magnitude is not (k_required spans 620 to 1.4×10⁵).
5. **NR-band width.** The corpus converged (P009 → P024 → P052 → P098) on 0.031–0.036 dex against LZ's drawn 0.021 dex; P052 shows using LZ's drawn width would cost 1.4σ. This is the one inconsistency the corpus resolved in LZ's favour: the paper's "1.5σ" text is right and its figure lines are wrong.
6. **Out-of-order finishes.** P006 ran before the `wd_halo` v_min-grid fix and its δ ≥ 350 keV time-PDFs are flatter than P020's recomputation (coordinator note; LRs at δ ≤ 350 keV accurate to 20–30 %). P061 writes that the base rate "awaits P083" and then P083 confirms P061's prior median — a small circularity: P083 calibrated its prior against P061 and P061 anticipated P083. P050's page carries a "not yet available" placeholder. P092–P099 were written before P090 and P091 (P099 read 88 ledger rows; P091 read 95); this review started with 97 rows and finished with 99 (P090 and P091 were appended while it was written). None of these changes a headline, but the ledger's row order is not the corpus's causal order.
7. **P(DM): 0.015 vs 0.25.** P061 (community log-uniform prior, median π_DM ≈ 0.017) and P083 (base rate 0.06) give P(DM) = 0.012–0.015; P091, using the dossier prior π_DM = 0.20 and the same eliminations, gives 0.25 (0.08–0.54). P091's own sceptic row (0.013) reproduces P061. The factor 17 is entirely prior; the corpus should quote both rather than either.
8. **Two verdicts on the Higgsino.** P084 ("allowed, 0.16–0.8 below all limits") and P076 ("excluded at every δ by ≥ 15–770×") both concern the LZ-fitting 1 TeV doublet; they are not contradictory (different channels: γ-ray/CMB vs solar neutrinos) but a reader of P084 alone would keep a model P076 has killed. P082 and P099 correctly propagate P076.

This is the request with the largest leverage on the corpus's bottom line, and the one least likely to be satisfiable from a standard data release (DR-003 itself notes waveform quantities may not be public).

## 8. Collective bias

The stance tally is 81 neutral, 13 statistical reassessments (8 weaken, 2 strengthen slightly, 3 neutral), 5 "supports DM" — every one "weakly, by elimination" — and 0 "favours background/artefact". Two readings are both defensible and both wrong on their own:

*Too kind to the dark-matter reading?* Twenty-four papers (IDM 11 + EFT 7 + MODEL 6) fit couplings, splittings or UV completions to one event, and the corpus never produced a "favours background" stance although its own numbers moved that way: it raised LZ's neighbourhood background from 3.5×10⁻⁴ to 9.4×10⁻⁴ (×2.7; P093 case ii) — a shift that in P001's box model costs ~0.4σ — and the stance taxonomy makes eliminating a background count as "supports DM" while finding an unmodelled one counts as "neutral". The five "supports DM" papers support only the proposition "single nuclear recoil", which atmospheric neutrinos (P019: 1–3×10⁻⁵) and an unsized artefact class satisfy equally. The corpus also let the ×2.9 band-anchored normalisation stand in three projection papers, inflating expected confirmations.

*Too kind to the null?* The corpus's central P(DM) ≈ 0.01–0.02 (P061, P083) is 56 % prior (π_DM) and rests on a hypothesis U ("LZ-specific unknown") that carries 0.79 of P061's weight, is assigned L_U = L_acc + L_rest with L_acc = 9.3×10⁻³, of which 65 % is an artefact class for which P041 found no probable mechanism (every candidate predicts 65–101 charge-deficient ERs or a 19–61σ hole in the ⁸³ᵐKr map, none seen). Without U, P001 gives P(DM) = 0.51 at π_DM = 0.01, b = 10⁻³. P091 makes the same point from the other side: the artefact class absorbs 0.64 of the posterior "because every alternative was eliminated, not because it fits well" (L_F ≈ 4×10⁻³ vs L_I ≈ 3×10⁻³). A catch-all that is never penalised for predicting nothing is a null-favouring device.

*Verdict on bias.* The corpus is not biased in its numbers — eighteen background papers (BKG + EXO) found LZ's model conservative where it could be tested (P056 tails, P073 veto efficiency, P090 MSSI) and thirteen STAT papers reproduced LZ's significances rather than inflating or deflating them. It is biased in its *labels* and its *framing*: "neutral" is the default for 82 % of papers because the stance field cannot express "this background is more important than LZ said", and the bottom-line probability swings by ×17 (0.015 → 0.25) purely with the prior. The honest summary is a two-horse race (P091) whose odds nobody can compute: one horse is a real single nuclear recoil of dark-matter origin whose rate is unpredicted; the other is an unidentified instrumental effect whose likelihood is a guess spanning 1.7 decades.

## 9. Questions the corpus did not ask

1. **LZ's own blinding and post-hoc definitions.** LZ states the analysis is non-blind, the salt failed (salt drawn to 250 ± 25 keV fell above the signal region because it was tuned before AmBe), the S1c < 600 phd edge was fixed in 2023, and the FV shrank by 14.5 % with an 8 cm stand-off (l. 132–137, 160–163). P061 parametrised this as f_FP ∈ [1, 10] and P038/P079 studied the edge and stand-off as design choices; nobody asked *when* the FV contour and the ±2σ NR-band definition of the "neighbourhood" were frozen relative to the first look at the high-S1c data, or what the seven earlier salt events (already removed in the 2024 analysis) implied about the reviewers' exposure to this region. **Answerable only by LZ** (analysis notes, dated cut definitions).
2. **The 2024 low-energy search's own high-S1c population.** The 2024 search (same 220 live days) used S1c = 3–80 phd; everything above was recorded but not searched, and this paper reuses its selections "unchanged" (l. 162). Were high-S1c single scatters inspected during the 2024 analysis (e.g. for the salt or the accidentals model)? **LZ only.**
3. **Detector time-stability on 16 June 2023 beyond "no abnormalities".** A ⁵⁷Co source in a CSD tube was withdrawn 25 minutes before the event (l. 300). P029 handled activation and P053 the ²¹⁴Pb flow, but nobody asked about the CSD mechanics (tube purge, light leaks, HV or grid behaviour during retraction), the electron-lifetime and ⁸³ᵐKr-map values within ±24 h, or the S1-only / single-electron pulse rates in the hour around the event. DR-003 requests part of this. **LZ only.**
4. **Independent NEST validation at 250 keV with real calibration data.** P009, P024, P064 and P098 inferred an 8 % N_q offset and a 0.031–0.036 dex band width from *figures*; LZ's AmBe calibration extends to 330 keV NRs (l. 92) but no paper filed a data request for the AmBe band at 200–330 keV. Filing DR-004 for those points would settle RESP's two open items. **Answerable by LZ; askable by the community** — it was not asked.
5. **The three vetoed events ≥ 5σ from the ER band** (Fig. S5a; P070 notes them): do they share the candidate's topology and would they have passed with a different veto window? **LZ only.**
6. **The reproducibility floor of LZ's own tables**: P089 found identical-spectrum pairs differ by 0.12σ rms (max 0.4σ) in Tables S6/S7; nobody asked LZ whether this is toy noise or a real asymmetry. **LZ only.**
7. **What the community would do with a null.** P099 gives kill conditions for the DM hypotheses; nobody wrote the kill condition for the artefact hypothesis except "DR-003", nor asked whether the corpus's U would simply be re-fitted to any second event.

## 10. One-paragraph verdict

Two weeks and 99 papers after arXiv:2609.02823 the event is established as a genuine single nuclear-recoil-like scatter of 246–248 ± 11 keV on LZ's efficiency plateau, 1.5σ below the NR-band median and typical of AmBe recoils; wall and RFR MSSI, neutrons of every origin, fission, accidentals, activation, ²¹⁴Pb and every exotic astrophysical or particle channel are excluded as its origin by three to nine orders of magnitude, and LZ's 3.4σ local and 2.6σ global significances are reproduced independently. As dark matter it can only be a q⁴-spin elastic coupling (L10/O6-class, no low-energy companions) or an inelastic splitting within 5–30 keV of the kinematic ceiling at m ≥ 400 GeV — with the Higgsino completion killed by solar capture and δ ≥ 366 keV already disfavoured by LZ's own empty 600–1000 phd sideband. Its probability of being dark matter is not a number the corpus can supply: 0.015 or 0.25 depending on the prior, because the only surviving competitor is an unidentified instrumental class whose likelihood is a guess. The verdict is on disk — 19 t·yr of unexamined xenon data, LZ's 600–1000 phd sideband and its own waveforms — and the corpus's most useful act would be to stop fitting the event and start asking LZ for those.
